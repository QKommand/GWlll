Historical Archive // Record 01

Ancient Dragonrises

Before 10,000 BE

Tyria's oldest history begins in darkness. There were no human kingdoms, no Pact, and no Commander. Even the civilizations remembered today as ancient were still struggling against forces older than their records.

The Elder Dragons had already begun shaping the world.

Archive Context

Much of this era survives only through fragmented records, ancient ruins, racial memory, and discoveries made thousands of years later. The exact order and dates of several events remain uncertain.

Record 01 // The First Known Dragonrise

A world already ruled by dragons

The oldest known Dragonrise is placed at roughly 20,000 BE.

By then, the Elder Dragons were already part of Tyria's natural cycle. They awakened when magical energy had grown abundant, consumed that energy from the world around them, and eventually returned to hibernation once there was too little magic left to sustain them.

Their awakenings were not isolated disasters. Each cycle could erase civilizations, transform entire regions, and scatter the peoples caught in their path.

Record 02 // The Great Giants

The last Giganticus Lupicus disappear

Among the creatures that inhabited ancient Tyria were the Giganticus Lupicus, more commonly remembered as the Great Giants.

Their enormous bones can still be found across Tyria and Elona, from the Crystal Desert to the Tarnished Coast, the Shiverpeaks, and lands once inhabited by the charr.

Around 10,000 BE, the last known Great Giants disappeared from the Tyrian continent.

Historical Connection

Their disappearance is associated with the suspected beginning of the last great Elder Dragon rising before recorded history.

Record 03 // The Elder Alliance

Five civilizations face extinction

Five races are known to have survived from this ancient period: the dwarves, Forgotten, jotun, mursaat, and Seers.

Their civilizations possessed magical knowledge far beyond what most modern peoples would later understand.

Faced with the Elder Dragons, these races formed an alliance in an attempt to survive the Dragonrise.

Their cooperation would not last.

Record 04 // The Failed Assault

Zhaitan could not be killed

At some point during the Dragonrise, the mursaat and Forgotten attempted to destroy the Elder Dragon Zhaitan.

The campaign failed.

Both peoples suffered devastating losses, and later records suggest that the failure deepened tensions within the alliance.

The elder races were learning a brutal truth: directly defeating the dragons might not be possible.

Record 05 // The Seers' Answer

Hide the magic

The Seers pursued another solution.

If the Elder Dragons awakened to consume magical energy, then perhaps that energy could simply be hidden from them.

The Seers first created the Shadowstone and later the Bloodstone.

The surviving races could place their magic within the Bloodstone, removing much of it from the world and denying the Elder Dragons the energy they sought.

Legacy

Thousands of years later, this same Bloodstone would be used by the Human Gods to control the amount of magic available to mortal races.

Record 06 // The Betrayal

The mursaat leave the alliance

The mursaat refused to surrender their magic to the Bloodstone.

Instead, they withdrew from the alliance and phased themselves into the Mists, escaping much of the destruction that followed.

Later evidence suggests the mursaat believed the other elder races had already betrayed them during the failed attempt against Zhaitan.

Whatever the original cause, the alliance was broken.

Record 07 // Seers and Mursaat

One struggle becomes another war

The conflict between the Seers and mursaat continued for centuries.

The mursaat wielded powerful magic, including the devastating ability known as Spectral Agony.

By the end of the conflict, the Seers had been brought close to extinction.

Two civilizations that had once faced the Elder Dragons together had nearly destroyed one another.

Record 08 // Those Who Hid

Survival did not always mean fighting

Across Tyria, other peoples found their own ways to endure the Dragonrise.

The kodan sought refuge in the distant north. One group later migrated south through Janthir and eventually into Orr.

Other civilizations abandoned territory, concealed themselves, or simply tried to outlast the dragons.

Record 09 // Glint

A dragon champion is given a choice

Glint was originally a champion and scion of the Elder Dragon Kralkatorrik.

Around 1680 BE, the Forgotten performed a ritual that freed Glint from Kralkatorrik's control.

The ritual cost Glint much of her earlier memory, but it gave her independent thought and free will.

Glint then helped conceal the surviving races from the Elder Dragons as the world waited for the Dragonrise to end.

Record 10 // The Long Sleep

The dragons finally return to slumber

With much of the world's accessible magical energy hidden or consumed, the Elder Dragons eventually lacked enough power to remain active.

One after another, they entered hibernation.

Tyria survived.

But the civilizations that emerged from the Dragonrise were diminished. Some had vanished. Others had lost much of the knowledge and power they once possessed.

Younger peoples such as the charr, kodan, and tengu began to rise in the world left behind.

The Elder Dragons slept beneath them.

And for thousands of years, most of Tyria forgot they were there.
