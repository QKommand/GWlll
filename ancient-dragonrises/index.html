Historical Archive // Record 01

Ancient Dragonrises

Unknown Past – Approx. 10,000 BE

Ancient Era Partially Mythological Dates Approximate

The oldest road through Tyrian history does not begin with a kingdom, a hero, or even humanity.

It begins in fragments.

Broken monuments, racial memories, surviving magical artifacts, and traditions passed down from civilizations that watched Elder Dragons rise long before the modern world existed.

Archive Reliability

The earliest Tyrian chronology is reconstructed from myths, ancient records, ruined civilizations, magical evidence, and later historical interpretation.

Dates in this archive should therefore be read as estimates unless a record specifically states otherwise.

Record 01 // Before History

The oldest story begins with a dragon

One ancient creation tradition claims that an original Elder Dragon created the world of Tyria and later created the other Elder Dragons.

This account reaches farther back than surviving historical records.

It belongs to the oldest layer of Tyrian tradition rather than firmly established historical chronology.

Source Status // Creation Tradition

This account should not be treated as uncontested fact. The archive preserves it because it is part of Tyrian lore, while keeping its uncertain status visible.

Record 02 // Earliest Known Dragonrise

The dragons rise to consume magic

The master historical calendar places the earliest currently recorded Dragonrise at approximately 20,000 BE.

Elder Dragons periodically awakened when ambient magical energy had accumulated across the world.

They consumed that magic on a colossal scale.

When the supply diminished, they eventually returned to hibernation.

This cycle would repeat across Tyrian history.

Record 04 // The Alliance

Ancient peoples attempt to resist the dragons

Five elder races appear to have cooperated against the Elder Dragons.

The surviving evidence points to an alliance involving the Seers, mursaat, Forgotten, dwarves, and jotun.

Their cooperation was extraordinary.

It did not last.

Historical Confidence // Partial

The alliance is supported by surviving lore, but many details concerning its organization and chronology remain unknown.

Record 05 // Zhaitan

The alliance attempts to kill an Elder Dragon

The surviving chronology describes an attempt by mursaat and Forgotten forces to destroy Zhaitan.

The effort failed.

The attack devastated both peoples and contributed to growing mistrust inside the elder-race alliance.

Later mursaat accounts appear to have interpreted the failure as evidence that they had been abandoned or betrayed.

Record 06 // Shadowstone

The Seers search for another solution

The Seers developed powerful magical technologies during the struggle against the Elder Dragons.

The chronology identifies the Shadowstone as preceding the Bloodstone.

This establishes an ancient progression in Seer magical technology rather than treating the Bloodstone as an isolated invention.

Record 07 // The Bloodstone

Magic is hidden from the dragons

Near the end of the ancient Dragonrise, the Seers created the first Bloodstone.

Its purpose was not originally to divide mortal magic into schools.

The Seers used it to gather and conceal magical energy from the Elder Dragons.

By starving the dragons of accessible magic, the surviving races hoped to force them back into hibernation.

Record 08 // The Mursaat Betrayal

The ancient alliance breaks apart

The mursaat refused to surrender their magic to the Bloodstone strategy.

Conflict with the Seers followed.

The mursaat abandoned the alliance and escaped the Elder Dragons by phasing into the Mists.

Their departure left the remaining peoples to endure the Dragonrise without them.

Historical Uncertainty

The exact motives and sequence surrounding the betrayal remain incomplete. Later evidence gives the mursaat more context than simply describing their actions as cowardice.

Record 09 // Ancient War

The Seers nearly disappear

The collapse of cooperation between the Seers and mursaat became a prolonged conflict.

The mursaat possessed devastating magical abilities, including Spectral Agony.

The Seers were brought close to extinction.

Only fragments of their civilization survived into later ages.

Record 10 // Giganticus Lupicus

The Great Giants vanish from the world

Around 10,000 BE, the last known Giganticus Lupicus disappeared from Tyria.

Earlier Guild Wars histories could not explain their extinction.

Later evidence associates their disappearance with the devastation of the ancient Elder Dragon awakening.

Their enormous remains can still be found across regions of the world.

Record 11 // Glint

A dragon champion turns against the cycle

Glint had originally served Kralkatorrik.

The Forgotten performed a ritual that freed her from the Elder Dragon's control.

Glint then helped conceal surviving peoples while the Elder Dragons consumed what magic remained available and eventually returned to sleep.

Her defiance would echo through Tyrian history thousands of years later.

Record 12 // The World After

Civilization emerges into a diminished world

Once the Elder Dragons returned to hibernation, surviving peoples emerged from hiding.

But the world they returned to was different.

Jotun civilization had largely collapsed. The Seers were nearly gone. Other ancient peoples had been diminished or scattered.

Charr, kodan, tengu, and other peoples would become increasingly prominent in later ages.
