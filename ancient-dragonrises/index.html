Historical Archive // Record 01

Ancient Dragonrises

Before 10,000 BE

Before human kingdoms, before the Six Gods entered the historical record, and before most surviving calendars began, Tyria had already lived through ages of destruction.

The oldest surviving memories belong to a world shaped by the Elder Dragons.

Archive Context

Exact dates are uncertain this far back. Most surviving knowledge comes from ancient ruins, dwarven traditions, jotun records, mursaat lore, and discoveries made thousands of years later. This archive therefore follows the broad sequence of the previous known Dragonrise rather than pretending every event can be dated.

Record 01 // The Ancient World

Tyria was already old

Long before humanity appeared in the historical record, powerful civilizations already occupied Tyria.

Among the peoples known to have survived from these distant ages were the dwarves, Forgotten, jotun, mursaat, and Seers.

Their world was more magically advanced in some ways than later civilizations would realize.

Record 02 // The Great Giants

The Giganticus Lupicus vanish

The enormous beings later called the Giganticus Lupicus, or Great Giants, once roamed large portions of the world.

Their remains can still be found across Tyria and Elona.

By approximately 10,000 BE, the Great Giants had disappeared.

Later Tyrian scholarship associated their extinction with the previous rising of the Elder Dragons.

Record 03 // Dragonrise

The Elder Dragons consume the world's magic

Elder Dragons periodically awakened when enough ambient magic had accumulated in the world.

Once awake, they consumed enormous amounts of magical energy and devastated the civilizations around them.

The previous Dragonrise reduced powerful cultures to remnants, forced populations into hiding, and permanently changed the balance of the ancient world.

Record 04 // The Alliance

Five ancient peoples resist

The surviving elder civilizations did not face the Dragonrise entirely alone.

Five of the elder races formed an alliance against the Elder Dragons.

The alliance represented one of the earliest known attempts to confront the dragons through cooperation rather than simple survival.

It did not remain united.

Record 05 // The Bloodstone

The Seers choose concealment

The Seers developed a different strategy for surviving the dragons.

They created the first known Bloodstone and used it to store magical energy away from the Elder Dragons.

By reducing the magic available for the dragons to consume, the surviving races could wait for the creatures to weaken and eventually return to hibernation.

Why This Matters Later

Thousands of years afterward, the Human Gods would use this same ancient artifact for an entirely different crisis: limiting the magical power available to mortals.

Record 06 // The Mursaat

One ally abandons the struggle

The mursaat refused to follow the same path as the other allied races.

After conflict with the Seers and the failure of cooperation against the dragons, the mursaat withdrew from the alliance.

They phased themselves into the Mists, escaping the destruction consuming the physical world.

Their departure left the remaining races to endure the Dragonrise without them.

Record 07 // Ancient Rivalry

The Seers are nearly destroyed

The relationship between the Seers and mursaat deteriorated into a long and devastating war.

The mursaat possessed powerful magic, including the technique later known as Spectral Agony.

By the end of the conflict, the Seers had been reduced to only a tiny number of survivors.

One of the oldest rivalries in Tyrian history would survive long after both civilizations disappeared from ordinary view.

Record 08 // Glint

A dragon champion turns against the cycle

Glint had once served the Elder Dragon Kralkatorrik.

A Forgotten ritual freed her from her master's control and granted her independent will.

During the closing stages of the Dragonrise, Glint helped conceal surviving peoples until the Elder Dragons weakened and returned to sleep.

Record 09 // The Silence

The dragons sleep again

With less magic available to sustain them, the Elder Dragons gradually returned to hibernation.

The world that emerged afterward was very different from the one that had existed before the Dragonrise.

Some civilizations were gone. Others had lost much of their knowledge or power.

The survivors became the foundation for the next age of Tyria.
