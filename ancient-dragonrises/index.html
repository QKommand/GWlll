Author's Preface

Where Tyrian history begins

Every historian eventually reaches a point where the road becomes difficult to follow. Written accounts disappear, dates become estimates, and what remains must be reconstructed from ruins, fossils, magical artifacts, surviving testimony, and records written thousands of years after the events they describe.

That is where this history begins. Tyria was already old, shaped by cycles of magic, ancient peoples, and forces powerful enough to erase entire ages from the record. Among the greatest of those forces were the Elder Dragons.

Their awakenings consumed enormous quantities of magic and devastated the world around them. The peoples who endured the last ancient Dragonrise survived through very different choices, and those choices would shape much of the history that followed.

Note on Dating

The oldest surviving chronological references place an ancient Dragonrise around 20,000 BE, while the better documented previous Dragonrise occurred roughly 10,000 years before the modern era. The surviving evidence does not support treating every ancient reference to the Elder Dragons as part of one continuous awakening.

— Commander Q
