YOU ARE HERE // Abaddon Gives Magic

Historical Archive // Record 04

The Gift of Magic

1 BE

For generations, humanity had expanded beneath the watch of the gods. Kingdoms formed, cultures spread, and civilizations learned to shape the world around them.

Then Abaddon gave mortals something powerful enough to change the balance of Tyria itself.

Magic.

Archive Context

Magic existed in Tyria long before 1 BE. The mursaat, Seers, Forgotten, Ritualists, and other ancient peoples possessed supernatural abilities before humanity received Abaddon's gift. What changed in 1 BE was the amount and accessibility of magical power available to the peoples of Tyria.

Record 01 // The Gift

Magic enters a new age

In 1 BE, the god Abaddon gave the knowledge and power to use magic to the sentient races of Tyria.

The gift was intended to empower the peoples of the world.

Instead, civilizations immediately discovered how effectively magic could be used against one another.

Conflict spread rapidly.

Record 02 // The Wars of Magic

The gift becomes a weapon

The races of Tyria used their new magical strength in struggles for dominance.

The resulting wars brought enormous destruction.

The gift that had been intended to make life easier instead threatened to destroy the societies that had received it.

Humanity itself stood near catastrophe.

Historical Pattern

Magic did not create greed or conflict. It magnified the ability of civilizations to act on them.

Record 03 // King Doric

A king asks the gods to intervene

With magical warfare spreading across Tyria, King Doric traveled to Arah, the City of the Gods.

Doric was the ruler of the united human lands of continental Tyria.

He pleaded with the gods to stop the destruction and take back the power that had been unleashed.

The gods listened.

Record 04 // The Bloodstone

An ancient artifact is used again

The gods turned to an artifact that was already ancient: the Bloodstone created by the Seers.

During the previous Dragonrise, the Seers had used the Bloodstone to hide magical energy from the Elder Dragons.

Now the gods used it for another purpose.

They gathered much of the world's magic and sealed it within the Bloodstone.

Connection to the Ancient Era

The solution to humanity's magical crisis came from a civilization that had fought the Elder Dragons thousands of years earlier.

Record 05 // Five Stones

Magic is divided

The Bloodstone was divided into five major pieces.

Four represented separate schools of magic. The fifth served as a keystone required to reunite them.

Preservation

Magic associated with healing and protection.

Destruction

Magic associated with overwhelming destructive force.

Aggression

Magic associated with pressure and punishment.

Denial

Magic associated with shutdown, control, and restriction.

The arrangement prevented any single individual from easily wielding the full power of all four schools at once.

Record 06 // The Blood of Doric

The king becomes part of the seal

King Doric had asked the gods to restore balance.

The burden of protecting that balance was placed upon him and his descendants.

The gods used Doric's blood to seal the Bloodstones.

His bloodline would remain tied to the stones long after his death.

Record 07 // Abaddon's Mouth

The stones are hidden within a volcano

The five major Bloodstones were taken to the Ring of Fire Island Chain.

There they were placed within the great volcano known as Abaddon's Mouth.

The gods had limited the power available to mortals.

But one of their own refused to accept that decision.

Record 08 // Abaddon's Objection

The giver of magic refuses the new order

Abaddon opposed the decision to restrict magic.

The same god who had given mortals access to greater magical power now stood against the other gods who sought to limit it.

He began transforming his followers, the Margonites, into powerful demonic beings.

The dispute over magic was becoming something far larger.

Record 09 // The Edge of Exodus

The gods go to war

Abaddon declared war upon the other gods and assaulted the Gates of Heaven.

What began as a dispute over how much magic mortals should possess became a war between divine beings.

The consequences would reshape continents.

And when the war ended, the gods would no longer walk openly among mortals.
