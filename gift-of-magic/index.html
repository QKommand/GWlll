Historical Archive // Record 04

The Gift of Magic

1 BE

Magic was older than humanity.

But in 1 BE, the gods opened access to magical power on a scale mortal civilizations had never known.

What was meant to elevate the peoples of Tyria quickly became a source of war. Before the year was over, the gods would reverse their decision and reshape the ancient Bloodstone.

Archive Context

Magic did not begin with the Human Gods. Ancient peoples such as the Seers and Mursaat possessed powerful magical traditions long before humanity arrived.

The original Bloodstone was itself an ancient Seer creation. By the time of the Human Gods, the artifact had been brought to Arah.

This archive concerns the events of 1 BE, when Abaddon used the Bloodstone as part of the gods' great gift of magic and the disastrous consequences forced the pantheon to reverse course.

Record 01 // The Gift

The gods give magic to mortal races

In 1 BE, the Six Gods decided to spread magical power among the mortal peoples of Tyria.

Abaddon, God of Knowledge and Water, took on the task of distributing the gift.

He used the ancient Bloodstone to release magic that had been sealed away since the previous Dragonrise.

Different peoples received access to powerful forms of magic.

The world already knew magic.

What changed was its scale and availability.

Important Distinction

Abaddon did not create magic. The gift dramatically expanded mortal access to power that already existed in Tyria.

Record 02 // The Magical Wars

Power spreads faster than restraint

The gift did not bring lasting prosperity.

Civilizations began using their newfound magical power against one another.

Rivalry became conflict.

Conflict became war.

The resulting destruction spread across Tyria and caused enormous loss of life.

The gods had increased what mortals were capable of doing.

They had not changed what mortals might choose to do with it.

Record 03 // King Doric

A king asks the gods to intervene

King Doric ruled the human lands on the Tyrian continent.

Watching the magical wars grow increasingly destructive, Doric made the long journey to Arah, the City of the Gods.

He pleaded with the Six to end the bloodshed and restrain the power they had released into the world.

Most of the gods agreed.

Record 04 // The Bloodstone

The gods turn back to an ancient solution

The gods did not create the original Bloodstone.

It had been forged thousands of years earlier by the Seers as a way to hide magic from the Elder Dragons.

Now the gods used that same ancient artifact to restrain mortal access to magic.

Magic was drawn back into the Bloodstone, greatly reducing the power available throughout the world.

In doing so, the gods repeated the basic logic of the Seers' ancient solution: control the flow of magic by concentrating it somewhere else.

Record 05 // Five Great Pieces

The Bloodstone is divided

Still in 1 BE, the gods shattered the original Bloodstone into five major pieces.

Four became associated with the traditional schools of magic:

Record 06 // The Keystone

The fifth stone becomes the key

The fifth major piece became the Keystone.

The Keystone was required to reunite the four other major Bloodstones.

Without it, no one could simply reconstruct the original stone and regain control over the complete concentration of magic it represented.

Smaller Bloodstone fragments were also created during the sundering.

Record 07 // Doric's Blood

Doric becomes part of the seal

King Doric had been the mortal ruler who asked the gods to restrain magic.

His blood was used to seal the Bloodstones.

The stones would thereafter be known by the name Bloodstone, and Doric's bloodline was entrusted with responsibility for guarding them.

The Name

The artifact itself was far older than Doric. Its familiar name, however, became associated with the sealing of the stones using the king's blood.

Record 08 // Ring of Fire

The five stones are hidden

The five major Bloodstones were taken from Arah to the Ring of Fire.

They were placed within the great volcano later known as Abaddon's Mouth.

Mortal access to magic had been dramatically reduced.

The magical wars could now be contained.

But the decision had created a new enemy.

Record 09 // The Breaking Point

Abaddon refuses the new limits

Abaddon had overseen the distribution of widespread magic.

He fiercely opposed the decision to revoke the gift and restrict mortal magical power.

His protests were rejected by the other gods.

The conflict soon escalated beyond disagreement.

Abaddon began transforming his devoted Margonite followers and turned against the rest of the pantheon.

Before the next year began, a mortal crisis over magic had become a war among gods.

The Exodus of the Gods

The Gift of Magic and the Bloodstone crisis both unfolded in 1 BE.

The next year, 0 AE, would become one of the great dividing points in Tyrian history.

Abaddon's rebellion would devastate the Crystal Sea, destroy the old Margonite civilization, create the Crystal Desert and Desolation, and end with the fallen god imprisoned in the Realm of Torment.

Afterward, the remaining gods would leave Tyria in the event remembered as the Exodus of the Gods.
