THE GIFT OF MAGIC

Volume IV // The Divine and Human Age

The Gift of Magic

Abaddon's Gift, the wars that followed, the division of the Bloodstone, and the rebellion that broke the Divine order.

Compiled and Annotated By

Commander Q

Captain Commander of the Vanguard Knights
Researcher of Tyrian History and the Mists

Author's Preface

Magic was already part of the world

The Gift of Magic was not the beginning of magic. Mortals had studied, crafted, regulated, and used magical power long before Abaddon's Gift.

What changed in 1 BE was access. Greater power became available to far more people, and conflicts that already existed became far more destructive.

The crisis that followed forced the Gods to decide whether the power they had allowed to spread should remain in mortal hands.

— Commander Q

I

Abaddon expands mortal access to magic

In 1 BE, Abaddon greatly expanded mortal access to magical power.

Abaddon was still one of the Six Gods: keeper of knowledge and secrets, patron of the Margonites, and an established member of the Divine order in Arah.

His Gift placed greater power into mortal hands than the existing system had allowed.

Research Note // The Gift

Surviving accounts differ in how they describe the mechanics of the Gift. Its historical effect is clearer: mortal access to magic increased dramatically.

II

Magical warfare spreads

Greater access to magic intensified conflicts between mortal peoples.

Existing rivalries could now be fought with destructive magical power on a much greater scale. The same forces capable of protecting cities and transforming daily life could also destroy settlements, armies, and fortifications.

The fighting became severe enough that King Doric sought Divine intervention.

Commander Q // Observation

Magic did not create every conflict. It changed how much damage those conflicts could do.

III

King Doric goes to Arah

King Doric traveled to Arah and asked the Gods to end the magical wars.

The Gods were still physically present within the city and remained capable of intervening directly in the magical order they had helped shape.

Their answer relied on an artifact already preserved inside Arah: the original Bloodstone.

IV

The Bloodstone is given a new purpose

The Seers had created the original Bloodstone during the previous Dragonrise to keep magic beyond the reach of the Elder Dragons.

The Gods now used it to restrict mortal magic.

With King Doric's blood used in the sealing, the Bloodstone was divided into five pieces.

Four became associated with Aggression, Destruction, Preservation, and Denial. The fifth served as a keystone.

The divided stones were then placed beyond easy mortal reach. Later events scattered them across Tyria.

Research Note // Bloodstones' Unmaking

Bloodstone-related machinery existed within Heavencrystal Palace in Arah, but the surviving record does not securely identify every device used during the division.

V

Abaddon rejects the restriction

The division of the Bloodstone reversed the direction established by Abaddon's Gift.

Mortal access to magic was restricted again. Abaddon opposed that decision and refused to accept the new order imposed by the other Gods.

The disagreement became open defiance.

Abaddon had once governed beside the Gods he now opposed. The conflict therefore began inside the pantheon itself rather than between Humanity and some outside enemy.

VI

The Margonites remain loyal to Abaddon

The Margonites had worshiped Abaddon during their long history around the Crystal Sea.

When their God turned against the rest of the pantheon, many Margonites followed him.

Their loyalty tied the fate of their civilization to his rebellion.

The Margonites entered the conflict as a Human people. Their later demonic transformation belongs to the rebellion and its aftermath, not to their earlier maritime history.

VII

The Gods go to war

Abaddon's rebellion became a war between Divine powers.

Abaddon and his followers fought the other Gods and the forces aligned with them.

The conflict devastated the southern world. The Crystal Sea was destroyed and became the Crystal Desert, while other scarred lands became the Desolation.

The destruction demonstrated that a war between Gods could reshape Tyria itself.

Commander Q // Observation

The Gods had restricted mortal magic because war was becoming too destructive. Their own conflict left an even deeper mark on the world.

VIII

Abaddon is defeated

The other Gods eventually defeated Abaddon.

He was imprisoned within the Realm of Torment rather than destroyed.

His followers were driven into exile with him, and the accepted Human pantheon was reduced from Six to Five.

Abaddon's name and symbols were later suppressed, obscuring his earlier place among the Gods. Hidden monuments, records, and his reliquary in Orr preserved fragments of the history that public worship no longer acknowledged.

IX

The Divine Age reaches its end

Abaddon's defeat ended the rebellion, but it did not restore the world that existed before it.

The Crystal Sea was gone. The Margonite civilization had fallen. The Bloodstone had been divided and scattered. Mortal magic now existed under new limits.

The remaining Gods had also seen what direct Divine warfare could do to Tyria.

Their next decision would end the age in which they physically ruled from Arah.

Archive Note

What remains uncertain

Abaddon's motives

Abaddon expanded mortal access to magic and later opposed its restriction, but the surviving record does not preserve a neutral account of every argument that divided the Gods.

The mechanics of the Gift

The increase in mortal magical access is clear. The exact mechanism by which Abaddon accomplished it is less completely preserved.

Margonite transformation

Abaddon's followers were eventually transformed into the demonic Margonites known from later history, but the surviving record does not establish every stage of that transformation with equal precision.

End of Volume IV

The Gods prepare to leave Tyria

The Divine war had altered the land, broken the pantheon, and shown what could happen when Gods fought openly in the physical world.
