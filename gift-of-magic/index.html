Historical Archive // Record 04

The Gift of Magic

1 BE – 0 AE

Magic was older than humanity.

But in 1 BE, the amount of magical power available to the peoples of Tyria changed dramatically.

What was intended as a gift quickly became a weapon. Within a year, the struggle over magic would bring mortals before the gods and divide history itself into before and after.

Archive Context

Magic did not begin with the Human Gods. Ancient races such as the Seers and mursaat possessed magical traditions long before humanity arrived. This record concerns the later event in which Abaddon greatly expanded mortal access to magic, triggering the crisis that led to the Bloodstone's division.

Record 01 // The Gift

Abaddon gives widespread magic to the races

In 1 BE, Abaddon gave the intelligent races of Tyria far greater access to magic.

The world already knew magic.

What changed was its availability and power.

Humans, charr, tengu, dwarves, and other peoples suddenly possessed abilities capable of transforming everyday life.

Record 02 // The Magical Wars

Power spreads faster than restraint

The gift did not bring lasting prosperity.

Races began using their new magical power against one another.

Competition became warfare.

The resulting destruction became severe enough to threaten entire populations.

Important Distinction

The crisis was not the invention of magic itself. It was the sudden expansion of magical power available to mortal civilizations.

Record 03 // King Doric

A king asks the gods to intervene

Human civilization was still united under King Doric.

Watching the magical wars grow increasingly destructive, Doric traveled to Arah, the City of the Gods.

He pleaded for the gods to restrain the power that had been unleashed upon the world.

The gods agreed.

Record 04 // The Bloodstone

The gods turn to an older creation

The Bloodstone itself was not originally created by the Human Gods.

It had been created much earlier by the Seers during the ancient Dragonrise.

The gods now used that ancient magical structure to restrict the enormous amount of power available to mortals.

Much of the accessible magic of the world was gathered into the Bloodstone.

Record 05 // Five Great Pieces

The Bloodstone is divided

In 0 AE, the Bloodstone was divided.

Four major pieces became associated with the traditional schools of magic:

School of Magic

Preservation

Magic associated with protection, healing, and preservation of life.

School of Magic

Destruction

Magic associated with direct destructive force and overwhelming power.

School of Magic

Aggression

Magic associated with pressure, punishment, and offensive action.

School of Magic

Denial

Magic associated with disruption, restraint, and preventing another's actions.

Record 06 // The Keystone

One final piece prevents reunification

A fifth major piece became the Keystone.

Without it, the other four major Bloodstones could not simply be reunited into their former whole.

The system ensured that no individual could easily command the complete concentration of magical power that had caused the crisis.

Numerous smaller Bloodstone shards were also created during the division.

Record 07 // Doric's Blood

The king who asked for peace becomes part of the seal

King Doric had been the mortal who asked the gods to restrain magic.

His blood was therefore used in the sealing of the Bloodstones.

This created a lasting connection between the stones and Doric's bloodline.

Record 08 // Ring of Fire

The five great stones are hidden

The five largest Bloodstone pieces were taken to the Ring of Fire.

They were placed within the great volcano later known as Abaddon's Mouth.

The magical wars had been brought under control.

But the gods themselves were no longer united.

Record 09 // The Breaking Point

Abaddon refuses the new limits

Abaddon had been responsible for giving widespread magical power to the mortal races.

He opposed the decision to restrict that power.

The disagreement did not remain a debate.

Abaddon turned against the other gods.

The crisis over mortal magic was about to become a war among the divine.

The magical wars had been contained, but the decision to restrict magic fractured the pantheon itself.
