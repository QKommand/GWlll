Historical Archive // Record 04

The Gift of Magic

1 BE

Humanity had built kingdoms, empires, and trade routes across the known world.

Then Abaddon gave Tyria something that changed every civilization at once: far greater access to magic.

The gift was meant to empower. Instead, it nearly tore the world apart.

Archive Context

Magic existed in Tyria long before 1 BE. The Seers, mursaat, ancient Ritualists, and other peoples already practiced forms of supernatural power. What changed in 1 BE was the scale of access. Abaddon's gift made powerful magic far more widely available.

Record 01 // The Gift

Abaddon opens the door wider

In 1 BE, the god Abaddon gave the sentient peoples of Tyria greater knowledge and access to magic.

This did not create magic itself.

Magic was already woven into Tyria and had been used by ancient civilizations long before humanity appeared.

Abaddon's gift changed how much of that power ordinary mortal civilizations could wield.

Record 02 // A Changed World

Magic becomes part of everyday civilization

The gift reached many intelligent peoples rather than belonging to one race or kingdom.

Magic could heal, protect, destroy, summon, manipulate, and reshape the battlefield.

For civilizations that had already spent centuries competing over territory and political power, the new possibilities were enormous.

So were the dangers.

Record 03 // The Wars

The gift becomes a weapon

Conflict followed almost immediately.

Races and nations used their new magical power against one another, turning existing rivalries into increasingly destructive wars.

Magic that could have made life easier instead became another means of domination.

The resulting destruction grew severe enough that entire populations were threatened.

The Central Problem

The danger was not magic simply existing. The crisis came from enormous magical power becoming available faster than the civilizations receiving it learned how to restrain themselves.

Record 04 // King Doric

A king asks the gods to intervene

King Doric, ruler of the human lands of continental Tyria, traveled to Arah.

There he pleaded before the gods for an end to the destruction.

Doric asked them to take back the power that had turned into a curse for the peoples wielding it.

The gods agreed that unrestricted magic could not continue.

Record 05 // The Ancient Solution

The gods turn to a Seer creation

The gods used the ancient Bloodstone, an artifact originally created by the Seers.

Rather than removing magic from the world entirely, they used the Bloodstone to restrict how its power could be accessed.

The artifact that had once helped ancient civilizations survive the Elder Dragons now became the foundation of a new magical order.

Record 06 // The Four Schools

Magical power is separated

The Bloodstone was divided so that no single mortal could easily command the full range of magical power.

Four major stones became associated with four broad schools:

School 01

Preservation

Magic associated with healing, protection, and keeping allies alive.

School 02

Destruction

Magic focused on direct destructive force and overwhelming damage.

School 03

Aggression

Magic associated with pressure, punishment, and sustained offensive power.

School 04

Denial

Magic associated with disrupting, limiting, or preventing an opponent's actions.

Record 07 // The Fifth Stone

One piece holds the system together

A fifth major stone became the keystone.

Without it, the four magical stones could not simply be reunited to restore unrestricted access to the full power sealed within them.

The design forced separation where the original gift had allowed dangerous concentration.

Record 08 // The Blood Seal

Doric becomes part of the safeguard

The gods used the blood of King Doric to seal the Bloodstones.

Doric had been the mortal ruler who asked for the intervention, and his bloodline became tied to the magical protections placed upon the stones.

From this point forward, the Bloodstones carried both an ancient Seer legacy and a human one.

Record 09 // Abaddon's Mouth

The Bloodstones are hidden away

The major Bloodstones were placed within Abaddon's Mouth, the great volcano in the Ring of Fire island chain.

The immediate magical crisis had been contained.

Mortals could still use magic, but the overwhelming concentration of power that had fueled the wars was now restricted.

The solution brought temporary stability.

It also created a new conflict among the gods themselves.

Record 10 // The Breaking Point

One god refuses to accept the restriction

Abaddon had been the god who championed the greater gift of magic.

He did not accept the decision to limit it.

The disagreement that began over mortal access to magic now threatened to become something much larger.

The next conflict would not be fought between mortal kingdoms.

It would be fought between gods.
