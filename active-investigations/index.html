Commander Q's Notebook

Notes on Guild Wars 3

Notes from the Orrian Golden Age as new pieces of the Guild Wars 3 timeline come into view.

Kept by Commander Q, Captain Commander of the Vanguard Knights and Researcher of Tyrian History and the Mists.

Guild Wars 3 is giving us a closer look at an age that has mostly existed as ancient history until now.

I am keeping my notes here as new details appear, comparing them with the older record and trying to place the pieces in order.

Starting Point

So where exactly are we?

ArenaNet // Guild Wars 3

Guild Wars 3 begins on the continent of Orr, over a thousand years before the events of Guild Wars Reforged, in a period before the Human Gods left this world. The former God of Death has recently been usurped. It is a time before the First Guild War, when hostilities between rival guilds are rising and the Order of the Vael is on the back foot.

That gives us a surprisingly useful stretch of history to work with.

Orr is alive. The Gods are still in Tyria. The old God of Death has only recently fallen. Guilds already have enough power and influence for their rivalries to become dangerous, but the First Guild War has not started.

Commander Q

We are not visiting ancient Orr. We are visiting Orr before it became ancient.

Dating the Game

Grenth gives us our best clue so far

The phrase that keeps catching my attention is recently usurped.

The older historical record places the rise of Grenth over Dhuum around 48 BE.

If that chronology still holds, Guild Wars 3 should sit somewhere reasonably close to that event. "Recently" does not give us an exact number of years, but it narrows the picture considerably.

Current Timeline

~48 BE → Guild Wars 3 → 1 BE → 0 AE

Grenth rises → GW3 → Gift of Magic and the Abaddon crisis → Exodus of the Gods

The other hard boundary is the Exodus. ArenaNet tells us directly that Guild Wars 3 takes place before the Gods leave the world.

The Exodus happens in 0 AE. Whatever the exact opening year turns out to be, it must fall before that.

Commander Q

Right now, the period around 48 BE interests me more than trying to force an exact date from the phrase "over a thousand years."

Timeline

What I can line up so far

Several pieces still lack exact dates, but their order is becoming clearer.

700+ years before GW3

Humanity has already been in Tyria for centuries

By the time Guild Wars 3 begins, Humans have lived in Tyria for more than seven hundred years. Cantha is old, Elona is prosperous, and Ascalon remains Humanity's youngest major holding.

150+ years before GW3

The Order of the Vael is established

Dwayna and Melandru sanction the Order and its use of signets. By the opening of Guild Wars 3, the Vaelwardens have already been protecting Orr for more than a century and a half.

Around 48 BE

Grenth replaces Dhuum

The surviving chronology places Grenth's rise around this period. ArenaNet describes the former God of Death as having been recently usurped when Guild Wars 3 begins.

Exact year unknown

Guild Wars 3 begins

Orr is in its Golden Age. The Human Gods remain in the world, guild hostilities are increasing, and the Order of the Vael is losing ground.

Later

First Guild War

The First Guild War has not happened when Guild Wars 3 begins. Its exact dates remain unclear, but the rivalries that will feed it are already present.

1 BE

Gift of Magic

The Gods broaden Humanity's access to magic. King Doric later appeals to them as magical conflict spreads, leading to the repurposing and division of the Bloodstone.

1 BE

Abaddon's rebellion

Abaddon rejects the restriction of magic and eventually turns against the other Gods. The conflict destroys the Crystal Sea and reshapes the region into the Crystal Desert and Desolation.

0 AE

Exodus of the Gods

After Abaddon's defeat and imprisonment, the Gods withdraw from physical Tyria into the Mists.

1072 AE

Guild Wars

The events of the original Guild Wars begin more than a thousand years after the world shown in Guild Wars 3.

1325 AE

Guild Wars 2

The Commander enters the historical record and begins the campaign against Zhaitan.

Orr

I have to stop picturing the ruins

Most of what we have known about Orr comes from looking backward at something already lost.

Guild Wars 3 turns that around.

Arah still stands as the City of the Gods. Orr is populated, wealthy, magically rich, and politically active. Guilds compete for influence. Settlements continue to spread. The Vael still inhabit the land.

The Cataclysm is not the defining feature of Orr yet. It has not happened.

Commander Q

We have spent years knowing Orr mainly through the manner of its death. Now we get to meet the civilization first.

The Gods

Still here, but something is changing

The Human Gods have not yet left Tyria.

They are still part of the political and religious world surrounding Orr, yet mortal guilds are carrying responsibilities that once sat much closer to Divine authority.

The Order of the Vael is a good example. Dwayna and Melandru sanction the Order to protect the Vael and their lands, giving mortals the means to carry out that work themselves.

We also know meetings between the Gods and guild representatives have become less common.

Commander Q

I keep wondering whether the Exodus was the end of Divine withdrawal rather than the beginning of it.

Guilds

They already matter before the Guild Wars

The guilds of this period are much more than adventuring groups.

They protect land, manage resources, influence settlement, work with magic, and compete for political and economic power.

By the opening of Guild Wars 3, those rivalries are becoming openly hostile.

The First Guild War is still ahead, but the world capable of producing it already exists.

Commander Q

I want to keep watching whether the story is really about guilds inheriting more responsibility from the Gods and then disagreeing over what to do with it.

Crystal Sea

The Margonites still have a homeland

The Crystal Desert does not exist yet.

The Crystal Sea remains a living body of water, and the Margonites are still a Human maritime civilization associated with its waters and surrounding coast.

Abaddon's rebellion, the destruction of the sea, and the later transformation of his followers remain ahead.

Commander Q

Like Orr, this may be another place where we finally get the civilization before we get the tragedy.

Current Notes

Where the trail leads so far

Guild Wars 3 appears to sit somewhere in the final stretch of the age when the Human Gods still inhabited Tyria.

Grenth has recently risen over Dhuum. Orr is alive and flourishing. Guild power is growing. The First Guild War has not yet begun. The Gift of Magic and Abaddon's fall remain ahead, and the Gods have not yet departed for the Mists.

The exact year can wait. For now, the shape of the age is beginning to come into view.
