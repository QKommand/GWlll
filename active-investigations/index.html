OPEN FILE // EXPECT CORRECTIONS

Kept by Commander Q, Captain Commander of the Vanguard Knights and Researcher of Tyrian History and the Mists.

This is not meant to be a finished history.

Guild Wars 3 is giving us pieces of an age that has mostly existed as ancient history until now. I am keeping those pieces here as they arrive so I can compare them with the older record.

Some notes will be right. Some probably will not. When something changes, I would rather leave the correction visible than pretend the earlier thought never happened.

Starting Point

So where exactly are we?

ArenaNet // Current Description

Guild Wars 3 begins on Orr more than a thousand years before Guild Wars Reforged, before the Human Gods left the world. The former God of Death has recently been usurped. The First Guild War has not yet happened, rival guilds are becoming hostile, and the Order of the Vael is struggling.

That is the best starting point we have.

Orr is alive. The Gods are still here. Grenth is apparently a relatively new God of Death. The guilds are already powerful enough to fight over influence, but the First Guild War has not started.

Q's Note

We are not visiting ancient Orr. We are visiting Orr before it became ancient.

Dating the Game

The Grenth problem might help

ArenaNet says the former God of Death was recently usurped.

The older record places Grenth's rise over Dhuum around 48 BE.

If ArenaNet is using that older chronology, then GW3 should be somewhere reasonably close to that event. We still cannot turn the word "recently" into a number, but it gives us something much better than simply subtracting a thousand years from Guild Wars.

Current Scribble in the Margin

~48 BE → ? → 1 BE → 0 AE

Grenth rises → Guild Wars 3 somewhere in this neighborhood → Gift of Magic → Exodus of the Gods

GW3 probably takes place around 72 AE.

No. That came from treating "more than a thousand years before Guild Wars" like an exact mathematical date.

ArenaNet explicitly says the Gods have not left the world yet. The Exodus happens in 0 AE.

Q's Thought

Right now I am more interested in the space around 48 BE than in inventing an exact year.

Orr

I have to stop picturing the ruins

Most of what we know about Orr comes from looking backward at something already lost.

GW3 flips that around.

Arah still stands as the City of the Gods. The Gods themselves remain in Tyria. Orr is full of people, guilds, magical resources, settlements, Vael, trade, arguments, ambition, and ordinary life.

The Cataclysm is nowhere close to being the defining feature of Orr yet.

Q's Note

I think this is going to force a rewrite of how I describe Orr throughout the Library. We have spent twenty years knowing the kingdom mainly through the manner of its death.

The Gods

Present, but maybe already pulling away

This part is worth watching closely.

The Gods have not yet left Tyria, but newer material suggests mortal guilds are taking on responsibilities that once sat much closer to Divine authority.

The Order of the Vael is one example. Dwayna and Melandru empower an organization to protect land and its inhabitants rather than simply doing all of that work themselves.

At the same time, contact between the Gods and the guilds appears to be becoming less common.

Q's Thought

Maybe the Exodus is not the beginning of Divine withdrawal. Maybe it is the end of a much longer process.

Guilds

They already matter before the Guild Wars

This may be one of the bigger changes in how I picture the period.

Guilds are not just adventuring clubs that eventually begin fighting.

They protect territory, manage resources, influence settlement, trade in magical knowledge, and compete for power.

The First Guild War has not happened yet, but the machinery needed to create it is already sitting there.

Q's Note

I want to keep an eye on whether GW3 is really the story of guilds inheriting power from the Gods and then discovering they do not agree on what to do with it.

Crystal Sea

The Margonites are still living in the world we lost

The Crystal Desert is not a desert yet.

The Crystal Sea still exists, and the Margonites remain a Human maritime people connected to its waters and surrounding coast.

Abaddon's later rebellion, the destruction of the Crystal Sea, and the transformation of his followers are still ahead.

Q's Note

This is another place where GW3 may give us the civilization first and the tragedy second.

End of Current Notes

For now.

The current picture puts Guild Wars 3 somewhere in the final stretch of the age when the Gods still inhabited Tyria.

Grenth is newly risen, Orr is alive, guild power is growing, the First Guild War is still ahead, and the Exodus has not happened.

That is enough to start drawing the shape of the era. The dates can fill themselves in as the record grows.
