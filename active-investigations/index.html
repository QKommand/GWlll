Current Working Position

Where I currently place Guild Wars 3

Working

This is the current interpretation of the evidence. It should change whenever stronger information appears.

Guild Wars 3 begins during the Orrian Golden Age, before the Gods leave physical Tyria, after the rise of Grenth, and before the later Gift of Magic and the historical Guild Wars.

The exact BE year remains unknown. "More than a thousand years before Guild Wars" is useful context, but not enough to assign a precise calendar date.

Official GW3 Reveal

Confirmed

Orr is in its Golden Age

Orr is alive, populated, politically active, and rich in magic. The kingdom has not yet suffered the Cataclysm or Zhaitan's rise.

Archive impact: Early Orr should be treated as a living civilization rather than primarily as the precursor to its destruction.

Official GW3 Reveal

Confirmed

The Gods are still present in Tyria

The Human Gods remain associated with Arah and continue to interact with mortal institutions, although those interactions are becoming less frequent.

Archive impact: GW3 must occur before the Exodus of the Gods.

Official GW3 Reveal

Confirmed

The Vaelwardens are more than 150 years old

The Order of the Vael predates the opening story by more than a century and a half.

Archive impact: This gives us an internal clock for early Orrian settlement and Divine involvement.

Official GW3 Reveal

Confirmed

Grenth has recently replaced Dhuum

Dhuum's overthrow is recent enough to belong to the living religious context of the period, rather than distant myth.

Archive impact: A future exact date for Grenth's rise could sharply narrow the GW3 calendar window.

Official GW3 Reveal

Confirmed

Guilds already hold real power

Guilds are responsible for protection, magical resources, settlement, trade, and competing political interests across Orr.

Archive impact: The system that eventually produces the Guild Wars already exists before the first historical Guild War.

Official GW3 Reveal

Confirmed

The Crystal Sea still exists

The Margonites remain a Human maritime people associated with the Crystal Sea and its coasts.

Archive impact: Abaddon's later rebellion and the destruction of the Crystal Sea remain in the future.

Commander Q's Notebook

Things I keep coming back to

Working Note

We are not visiting ancient Orr. We are visiting Orr before it became ancient.

Working Note

The guilds may be the real bridge between the age of direct Divine stewardship and the later Human political world. The Gods do not disappear overnight. Responsibility seems to be moving gradually into mortal institutions.

Working Note

Grenth being newly established changes how I picture religion in this period. To us, the pantheon looks ancient and fixed. To people living here, one of the major Divine offices may have changed within living memory.

Working Note

The Margonites matter more than I first thought. The Crystal Sea is still a functioning Human world while Orr is at its height. That means Abaddon's later fall destroys an existing international landscape, not merely a remote religious faction.

Working Interpretations

Theories we are not calling canon

Theory

These are useful ways of connecting evidence. They stay here until something actually confirms them.

Theory

GW3 may show the beginning of Divine withdrawal

The Gods are still present, yet their direct councils with guilds are becoming less common. This may represent the gradual movement toward their later seclusion in Arah.

Theory

Guild rivalry may become the political failure that defines the era

Guilds were entrusted with real responsibilities. Their growing competition may show how a useful institution evolves into the conflicts later remembered as the Guild Wars.

Theory

Abaddon's story may already be beginning

The Margonites already worship him and control the Crystal Sea. Future reveals may show tensions that eventually lead toward the Gift of Magic, his rebellion, and the destruction of their homeland.
