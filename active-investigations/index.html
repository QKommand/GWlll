Commander Q's Journal

Guild Wars 3

A dated record of new lore, changing historical placement, and the theories forming around the Orrian Golden Age.

Kept by Commander Q, Captain Commander of the Vanguard Knights and Researcher of Tyrian History and the Mists.

Guild Wars 3 is filling in a period of Tyrian history that we previously knew mostly through scattered accounts and events that happened much later.

These entries follow that information as it appears, recording what it adds to the timeline and how it changes the way I currently understand the era.

13 August 2026

Humanity gives us the wider world

ArenaNet's look at Humans establishes that Humanity has already been in Tyria for more than seven hundred years by the opening of Guild Wars 3.

Cantha is the oldest Human civilization. Elona is prosperous. Ascalon remains Humanity's youngest major holding. The Margonites still occupy the Crystal Sea and its surrounding coasts.

Orr sits in the middle of this Human world rather than at the end of its history. It is still a living kingdom and one of the places where Human expansion, Divine influence, magic, and guild power are meeting.

16 August 2026

ArenaNet gives us the first real boundaries

ArenaNet // Guild Wars 3

Guild Wars 3 begins on the continent of Orr, over a thousand years before the events of Guild Wars Reforged, in a period before the Human Gods left this world. The former God of Death has been recently usurped. It is a time before the First Guild War, when hostilities between rival guilds are rising and the Order of the Vael is on the back foot.

This gives us several separate anchors.

The Gods have not yet left Tyria. The First Guild War has not yet begun. Dhuum has already lost his place as God of Death. The Order of the Vael has already existed long enough to rise and begin declining.

ArenaNet also says this is more than a thousand years before Guild Wars Reforged, but this description should be treated as an approximation.

Current placement

This firmly places Guild Wars 3 in the pre-Exodus Divine age.

16 August 2026

Grenth may narrow the date further

The most useful phrase in ArenaNet's description may be that the former God of Death was recently usurped.

Older chronology places Grenth's rise over Dhuum around 48 BE.

If Guild Wars 3 continues to use that chronology, then the playable period should sit somewhere reasonably close to 48 BE.

We cannot assign a number to "recently," but this is a stronger clue than simply counting backward from Guild Wars.

Current Working Range

~48 BE → Guild Wars 3 → 1 BE → 0 AE

Grenth rises over Dhuum → Guild Wars 3 → Gift of Magic and Abaddon's crisis → Exodus of the Gods

Theory at this point

Guild Wars 3 may take place sometime during the final decades before the Exodus rather than more than a century before it.

The exact year remains unknown.

16 August 2026

The Order of the Vael gives us another clock

The Vaelwardens have already existed for more than one hundred and fifty years when Guild Wars 3 begins.

Their origins connect them directly to Dwayna and Melandru, who sanctioned their work protecting the Vael and their lands and enabled their use of signets.

By the opening of the game, the Order is described as being on the back foot while other guilds are growing in influence.

What this adds

Guild organizations were already important long before the First Guild War.

They were trusted with protection, land, magical practices, and responsibilities that could outlast generations.

This makes the rising hostility between guilds less like the sudden beginning of a conflict and more like tension within a system that has existed for a long time.

17 August 2026

The Vaelwardens change what we thought we knew

ArenaNet's history of the Vaelwardens gives us a much clearer picture of the Orrian Golden Age and changes several assumptions I had been carrying into this period.

ArenaNet // The Vaelwardens of the Orrian Golden Age

During the deepest point of the Elder Dragons' slumber, Orr's magic flourished freely enough to give rise to the Vael and draw the Human Gods from the Mists to Tyria.
Read the ArenaNet article →

What we knew

Guild Wars 3 takes place during Orr's Golden Age, before the Exodus of the Gods and before the First Guild War. Dhuum has recently been deposed, the Vaelwardens are already in decline, and the later Gift of Magic has not yet happened.

What we learned

The Elder Dragons are at the deepest point of their slumber during this era. Their hunger for Tyria's magic is silent, allowing magic to accumulate and develop more freely throughout the world.

Orr is the greatest expression of this change. Magic flows there so richly that it draws the Human Gods from the Mists to Tyria. Free from the Elder Dragons' predation, that magic also develops into the Vael, an ecosystem of magical spirits unique to this period of Tyrian history.

The Order of the Vael is the first guild ever chartered in Orr. Its beginnings reach back to the earliest days of Human settlement in the region, when a catastrophe caused the first great loss suffered by the Vael.

Those who would become the Vaelwardens appealed to the Human Gods for help. Melandru and Dwayna agreed to sanction the new Order and gave its members the ability to create and refine signets.

This is described as the first time the use of magic passed into mortal hands. Until then, magic had been held by the Gods and Tyria's elder species.

Correction

I had been treating the later Gift of Magic as the point when mortals first gained access to magic. That is no longer accurate.

The Vaelwardens were already using Divinely granted signet magic generations earlier.

The later Gift of Magic appears to be a much broader expansion of mortal access to magic rather than its beginning.

Magic spreads before the Gift

The Vaelwardens were the first mortals given this power, but they were not the last.

As Orr developed, more guilds gained access to signet manufacture. Over the following decades, signets became commodities that could be bought, sold, stolen, and trafficked through black markets reaching across Tyria.

Orr itself became increasingly valuable. Magic crystallized in its soil, condensed in its flora, and produced resources that settlers, fortune-seekers, and guilds could exploit.

What this changes

By Guild Wars 3, magic is already becoming part of trade, industry, wealth, and political power.

The later conflicts surrounding magic therefore emerge from a world where mortals have already spent generations learning how to use and profit from it.

The Vaelwardens are older than we realized

Their vigil has lasted for more than one hundred and fifty years by the opening of Guild Wars 3.

During that time the Vaelwardens gradually decline. The Vael suffer repeated losses, including the deaths of several elders and ancients, while settlement and development continue around them.

By the start of the game, ordinary Orrians and rival guilds increasingly see the Vaelwardens as an isolated order standing in the way of progress.

The Gods are becoming more distant

The Gods remain in Tyria, but they appear increasingly content to leave the affairs of Orr to mortal guilds while they concern themselves with matters in Arah.

Their high council sessions with the envoys of Orr's empowered guilds have become rarer with every passing year.

ArenaNet also places the recent deposition of Dhuum within this same period of growing distance between the Gods and the guilds.

Current theory

The Exodus may not mark the beginning of the Gods' withdrawal from mortal affairs.

Guild Wars 3 may instead take place during an earlier stage of that withdrawal, when the Gods still inhabit Tyria but increasingly leave its everyday affairs to the guilds.

The timeline now looks like this

Deepest Dragon slumber → Vael emerge → Humans settle Orr → Vael catastrophe → Vaelwardens founded → signet magic spreads → Dhuum deposed → Guild Wars 3

The Order has already existed for more than 150 years when Guild Wars 3 begins.

What this may tell us about the date

The traditional placement of Grenth's rise around 48 BE remains our strongest possible numerical clue.

ArenaNet now describes the deposition of Dhuum not only as recent to Guild Wars 3, but as a recent event affecting the current affairs of the Gods.

If the older 48 BE chronology still holds, then a Vaelwarden history extending more than 150 years would place the Order's founding sometime before roughly 198 BE.

That is an inference rather than a confirmed date, but it gives us another point to compare against older accounts of early Human settlement in Orr.

Where this leaves us

The Orrian Golden Age now looks like a very specific moment in the Elder Dragon Cycle.

The Dragons are deeply dormant. Magic is flourishing without their hunger suppressing it. The Vael have emerged from that abundance. The Gods are still present but becoming more distant, while mortal guilds gain control over magic, land, trade, and political power.

Guild Wars 3 begins while that world is changing.
