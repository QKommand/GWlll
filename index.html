Archive Method

Follow the record, not a perfect certainty

Tyrian history is assembled from surviving records, ruins, dialogue, artifacts, chronicles, and events witnessed across Guild Wars and Guild Wars 2. Some ancient dates and sequences remain approximate.

Where the historical record is uncertain, individual volumes distinguish established events from interpretation rather than forcing every fragment into a certainty the sources do not provide.
