The Road Through Time

Tyria remembers more than any one hero ever could. Kingdoms rose where dragons once slept. Seas became deserts. Cities vanished beneath the waves. Gods walked among mortals and eventually disappeared into the Mists.

Begin with the oldest surviving memories and follow the road forward. Each archive opens another piece of the history that shaped the world of Guild Wars.

Ancient Dragonrises

Long before humanity crossed Tyria's shores, civilizations already lived beneath the shadow of the Elder Dragons.

The Elder Races

Dwarves, Forgotten, jotun, mursaat, and Seers carried memories of a world that existed long before the human kingdoms.

Before 786 BE – 1 BE

The Gods and Humanity

The Six Gods came to Tyria from the Mists. Humanity entered a world whose history was already thousands of years old.

Humans settled Cantha, Elona, Orr, Kryta, and Ascalon, founding kingdoms and empires that transformed the world.

1 BE

The Gift of Magic

Abaddon gave greater access to magic to the peoples of Tyria.

Magical warfare followed. King Doric pleaded with the gods to intervene, leading them to use the ancient Bloodstone to limit mortal power.

Explore the Gift of Magic →
05

0 AE

The Exodus of the Gods

Abaddon rejected the restriction of magic and turned against the other gods.

His defeat transformed the Crystal Sea, created the Desolation, and ended the age when the gods openly walked beside mortals.

Explore the Exodus of the Gods →
06

1 AE – 1069 AE

Kingdoms and Guild Wars

Human kingdoms flourished while trade, dynasties, religious movements, and political rivalries reshaped the world.

Guilds grew into military powers, and generations of conflict weakened Ascalon, Kryta, and Orr just before the charr marched south.

Explore Kingdoms and Guild Wars →
07

1070 – 1080 AE

The Guild Wars Era

The Searing shattered Ascalon. Orr sank beneath the sea. The White Mantle seized power in Kryta.

Shiro returned to Cantha, Nightfall threatened Elona, Abaddon fell, and Primordus began to stir beneath Tyria.

Explore the Guild Wars Era →
08

1078 – 1320 AE

The Dragons Awaken

Primordus awakened beneath Tyria. Jormag drove the norn south. Zhaitan raised Orr from the sea.

Kralkatorrik carved the Dragonbrand across Tyria. The ancient cycle had returned.

Explore The Dragons Awaken →
09

1325 – 1335 AE

The Age of the Commander

The Pact formed against Zhaitan, beginning a campaign that changed the relationship between mortals and the Elder Dragons.

Zhaitan, Mordremoth, Kralkatorrik, Jormag, Primordus, and Soo-Won fell while Aurene rose to inherit the burden of Tyria's magical balance.

Explore the Age of the Commander →
10

1336 – 1338 AE

Beyond the Dragon Cycle

With the Elder Dragon cycle ended, hidden powers stepped into view.

The Wizard's Court, Astral Ward, Kryptis, Nayos, Janthir, and the return of the Titans revealed that Tyria's history extended far beyond the Dragon Cycle.

Explore Beyond the Dragon Cycle →

1338 – 1339 AE

Visions of Eternity

The road reaches the current era.

The Inquest, led by Director Vloxx, launches an expedition toward Castora in search of Ancora, carrying Tyrian history into another unknown chapter.
