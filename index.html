The road through time

Start at the beginning or jump directly to the age that interests you. The deeper records will branch from these major chapters.

01

Before 10,000 BE

Ancient Dragonrises

Long before human kingdoms, the Elder Dragons rose to consume the world's magic. Civilizations vanished, the Great Giants disappeared, and the survivors learned to hide from forces that could reshape continents.

02

Ancient Era

The Elder Races

Dwarves, Forgotten, jotun, mursaat, and Seers once possessed knowledge beyond most modern civilizations. Their alliance against the dragons ended in betrayal, war, and near extinction.

Explore this era →
03

Before 786 BE

The Gods and Humanity

The Six Gods entered Tyria through Orr and brought humanity into an already ancient world. Human settlements spread across Cantha, Elona, Orr, Kryta, and Ascalon.

Explore this era →
04

1 BE – 0 AE

Magic, Abaddon, and the Exodus

Magic was offered as a gift but became a weapon. King Doric begged the gods to intervene, the Bloodstone was divided, Abaddon rebelled, and the gods departed Tyria.

Explore this era →
05

1 AE – 1070 AE

Human Kingdoms and Guild Wars

Human nations flourished while guilds grew into armies. Rivalries between Ascalon, Kryta, and Orr weakened the kingdoms just as the charr prepared to return.

Explore this era →
06

1070 – 1080 AE

The Guild Wars Campaign Era

The Searing destroys Ascalon, Orr sinks beneath the sea, Shiro returns to Cantha, Nightfall threatens Elona, and ancient powers awaken beneath Tyria.

Explore this era →
07

1080 – 1325 AE

The Lost Two Centuries

Elder Dragons awaken one after another. The norn flee south, the asura reach the surface, Joko conquers Elona, Orr rises, and the modern nations of Tyria take shape.

Explore this era →
08

1325 – 1335 AE

The Modern Dragon Cycle

The Pact rises against Zhaitan. Scarlet awakens Mordremoth. Aurene grows from Glint's second scion into the Elder Dragon who helps bring the ancient cycle to an end.

Explore this era →
09

1336 – 1338 AE

The World After Dragons

With the Elder Dragon cycle ended, hidden threats emerge. Kryptis cross from the Mists, the Wizard's Court is revealed, and ancient Titans return in Janthir.

Explore this era →
10

1338 – 1339 AE

Present Day

The Inquest reaches Castora in search of Ancora. Ancient Seer ruins and forgotten magical practices pull Tyria's oldest mysteries back into the present.
