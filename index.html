Archive Guide

One history, divided into readable volumes

Tyria's history stretches across thousands of years, several continents, the Mists, lost civilizations, Human kingdoms, the Guild Wars, and repeated awakenings of the Elder Dragons.

The Road Through Time follows that history chronologically. Each volume continues into the next until the record reaches the present era.

Readers looking for a particular person, artifact, location, organization, or historical event can instead use the Research Archives below.
