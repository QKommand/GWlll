The Road Through Time

Tyria remembers more than any one hero ever could. Kingdoms rose where dragons once slept. Seas became deserts. Cities vanished beneath the waves. Gods walked among mortals and eventually disappeared into the Mists.

Begin with the oldest surviving memories and follow the road forward. When a moment catches your attention, open its archive and step deeper into the history behind it.

Before 10,000 BE

Ancient Dragonrises

Long before humanity crossed Tyria's shores, civilizations already lived beneath the shadow of the Elder Dragons.

The Great Giants disappeared. Ancient peoples struggled to survive. The Seers created the Bloodstone, and the mursaat vanished into the Mists.

Ancient Tyria

The Elder Races

Dwarves, Forgotten, jotun, mursaat, and Seers carried memories of a world that existed long before the human kingdoms.

Their alliances, wars, magical discoveries, and failures would quietly shape events thousands of years later.

Before 786 BE – 1 BE

The Gods and Humanity

The Six Gods came to Tyria from the Mists. Humanity followed into a world whose history was already thousands of years old.

Humans settled Cantha, Elona, Orr, Kryta, and Ascalon, founding kingdoms and empires that would dominate the next age.

04

1 BE

The Gift of Magic

Abaddon gave powerful magic to the peoples of Tyria. What was intended as a gift quickly became fuel for devastating wars.

King Doric traveled to Arah and pleaded with the gods to intervene before magical conflict destroyed the civilizations they had helped build.

0 AE

The Exodus of the Gods

Magic was sealed within the Bloodstone. Abaddon rebelled against the other gods, and the conflict transformed the Crystal Sea into the Crystal Desert and Desolation.

Abaddon was imprisoned within the Realm of Torment. The remaining gods departed Tyria for the Mists.

1 AE – 1069 AE

Kingdoms and Guild Wars

Human civilization flourished across the known world. Guilds grew from organizations into political and military powers.

Conflict between Ascalon, Kryta, and Orr eventually consumed generations and weakened the kingdoms just as another enemy prepared to strike.

1070 – 1080 AE

The Guild Wars Era

The Searing shattered Ascalon. Orr sank beneath the sea. The White Mantle seized control of Kryta.

Shiro Tagachi returned to Cantha, Nightfall threatened Elona, and Abaddon's thousand-year plan finally reached its conclusion.

1078 – 1320 AE

The Dragons Awaken

Primordus stirred beneath the earth. Jormag drove the norn south. Zhaitan raised Orr from the sea.

Kralkatorrik carved the Dragonbrand across Tyria. The cycle that ancient civilizations once feared had begun again.

1325 – 1335 AE

The Age of the Commander

The Pact formed against Zhaitan and began a struggle that would eventually challenge the ancient Elder Dragon cycle itself.

Scarlet Briar, Mordremoth, Balthazar, Kralkatorrik, Jormag, Primordus, and Soo-Won transformed the magical balance of the world.
