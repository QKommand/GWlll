HISTORICAL RECORD ACCESS // ACTIVE

Welcome, traveler.

You are accessing the Living Lore Library, a historical collection maintained within the library archives of Terminal 77-B of the Durmand Priory.

These records preserve the long memory of Tyria: civilizations that rose before humanity, the ancient awakenings of the Elder Dragons, the arrival of the gods, the rise and fall of kingdoms, the wars of guilds, and the events that shaped the world we know today.

Not every account has survived intact.

Some histories remain only in ruins. Others were recovered from forgotten writings, ancient artifacts, distant lands, or the testimony of those who witnessed history firsthand.

The Priory has gathered those fragments here so that they may be followed as one road through time.

Select an era and begin where you wish.

History does not require that you start at the beginning.
