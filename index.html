The History of Tyria

Every age leaves a story behind.

Long before the Commander walked Tyria, kingdoms rose, civilizations disappeared, gods fought, and Elder Dragons reshaped the world.

Follow the history of Guild Wars from the oldest surviving records to the present day, then step into the archives to discover the people, places, conflicts, and forgotten stories behind the events.

Main Chronology

The road through time

Start with Tyria's oldest surviving history and follow the major events that shaped the world. Each era can open into deeper records when you want to explore further.

01

Before 10,000 BE

Ancient Dragonrises

Long before human kingdoms, the Elder Dragons rose to consume the world's magic. Civilizations vanished, the Great Giants disappeared, and those who survived learned to hide from beings powerful enough to reshape continents.

02

Ancient Era

The Elder Races

Dwarves, Forgotten, jotun, mursaat, and Seers once possessed knowledge beyond most modern civilizations. Their attempts to survive the Elder Dragons eventually fractured into betrayal, war, and near extinction.

Explore this era →
03

Before 786 BE

The Gods and Humanity

The Six Gods entered Tyria through Orr and brought humanity into an already ancient world. Human settlements spread across Cantha, Elona, Orr, Kryta, and Ascalon.

Explore this era →
04

1 BE – 0 AE

Magic, Abaddon, and the Exodus

Magic was offered as a gift but quickly became a weapon. King Doric begged the gods to intervene, the Bloodstone was divided, Abaddon rebelled, and the surviving gods eventually departed Tyria.

Explore this era →
05

1 AE – 1070 AE

Human Kingdoms and Guild Wars

Human nations flourished across Tyria, Cantha, and Elona. Guilds became military powers, political rivalries deepened, and generations of conflict slowly weakened the human kingdoms.

Explore this era →
06

1070 – 1080 AE

The Guild Wars Campaign Era

The Searing destroys Ascalon. Orr sinks beneath the sea. Shiro returns to Cantha. Nightfall threatens Elona. Ancient powers begin stirring beneath the world.

Explore this era →
07

1080 – 1325 AE

The Lost Two Centuries

Elder Dragons awaken one after another. The norn flee south, the asura reach the surface, Palawa Joko conquers Elona, Orr rises from the sea, and the modern nations of Tyria begin to take shape.

Explore this era →
08

1325 – 1335 AE

The Modern Dragon Cycle

The Pact rises against Zhaitan. Scarlet Briar awakens Mordremoth. Balthazar returns. Aurene grows from Glint's second scion into the Elder Dragon who helps bring the ancient cycle to an end.

Explore this era →
09

1336 – 1338 AE

The World After Dragons

With the Elder Dragon cycle ended, hidden threats step into the open. Kryptis cross from the Mists, the Wizard's Court is revealed, the Tyrian Alliance forms, and Titans return in Janthir.

Explore this era →
10

1338 – 1339 AE

Present Day

The Inquest reaches Castora in search of Ancora. Ancient Seer ruins and forgotten magical practices pull Tyria's oldest mysteries back into the present.
