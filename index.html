/// Project Record

Bringing the past forward

GWlll is a personal archive project dedicated to organizing the history of the Guild Wars universe into a clear, connected, and easy-to-navigate library.

The project begins with a main chronology, then opens branching records that explain why major events happened, what they changed, and how they connect to later stories.

Archive Access Warning

This library contains major story spoilers

The Guild Wars Living Lore Library contains historical records from across the Guild Wars universe. These records may reveal major characters, identities, deaths, alliances, locations, conflicts, mysteries, and story outcomes.

The library is intended to be a complete historical archive rather than a spoiler-free gameplay guide.
