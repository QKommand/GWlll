The Road Through Time

Tyria remembers more than any one hero ever could. Kingdoms rose where dragons once slept. Seas became deserts. Cities vanished beneath the waves. Gods walked among mortals, then disappeared into the Mists.

This library follows that road from the oldest surviving memories of Tyria to the world we know today.

Historical Record

The Road Through Time

Follow the major turning points of Tyrian history. Some records can be opened to explore the people, conflicts, discoveries, and mysteries hidden beneath the larger story.

01

Before 10,000 BE

Ancient Dragonrises

Long before humanity crossed Tyria's shores, civilizations already lived beneath the shadow of the Elder Dragons.

The Great Giants disappeared. Ancient races struggled to survive. The Seers created the Bloodstone, and the mursaat vanished into the Mists while the dragons slowly returned to their slumber.

Explore Ancient Dragonrises →
02

Ancient Tyria

The Elder Races

Dwarves, Forgotten, jotun, mursaat, and Seers carried memories of a world that existed long before the human kingdoms.

Their alliances, wars, magical discoveries, and failures would quietly shape events thousands of years later.

03

Before the Exodus

The Gods and Humanity

The Six Gods came to Tyria from the Mists. Humanity followed, spreading across Cantha, Elona, Orr, Kryta, and Ascalon.

The world was entering an age that would be dominated by human kingdoms, divine influence, and increasingly powerful magic.

04

1 BE – 0 AE

The Gift of Magic

Abaddon gave powerful magic to the peoples of Tyria. What was meant as a gift quickly became fuel for devastating wars.

King Doric pleaded with the gods for intervention. Magic was sealed within the Bloodstone, Abaddon rebelled, and the resulting conflict reshaped entire regions of the world.

05

0 AE

The Exodus of the Gods

Abaddon was defeated and imprisoned within the Realm of Torment.

The remaining gods departed Tyria for the Mists, ending the age in which humanity lived directly beside its gods.

06

Human Age

Kingdoms and Guild Wars

Human civilization flourished, but power slowly shifted toward increasingly influential guilds.

Competition for land, influence, and magical power eventually pushed Ascalon, Kryta, and Orr into generations of conflict.

07

1070 – 1075 AE

The Guild Wars Era

The Searing shattered Ascalon. Orr sank beneath the sea. The White Mantle rose in Kryta.

Shiro Tagachi returned to Cantha, Nightfall threatened Elona, and Abaddon's thousand-year plan finally reached its conclusion.

08

1078 – 1320 AE

The Dragons Awaken

Primordus stirred beneath the earth. Jormag drove the norn south. Zhaitan raised Orr from the sea, and Kralkatorrik carved the Dragonbrand across Tyria.

The ancient cycle had begun again.

09

1325 – 1335 AE

The Age of the Commander

The Pact formed against Zhaitan, beginning a campaign that would eventually challenge nearly every Elder Dragon.

Scarlet Briar, Mordremoth, Balthazar, Kralkatorrik, Jormag, Primordus, and finally Soo-Won changed the magical balance of the world.

10

1336 – 1338 AE

Beyond the Dragon Cycle

With the Elder Dragon cycle ended, Tyria discovered that the dragons had never been the only forces moving beyond the world's borders.

The Wizard's Court, Kryptis, Nayos, Janthir, and the return of the titans opened chapters of history that had remained hidden for centuries.

11

1338 – 1339 AE

Visions of Eternity

The road now reaches the present.

The Inquest, led by Director Vloxx, launches an expedition toward Castora in search of Ancora, carrying Tyrian history into another unknown age.
