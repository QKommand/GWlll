Historical Archive // Record 05

The Exodus of the Gods

0 AE

The mortal wars over magic had been contained.

The argument among the gods had not.

Abaddon's refusal to accept the restriction of magic became a divine war that scarred the world, destroyed a civilization, and ended the age when the gods openly walked Tyria.

Archive Context

This record begins immediately after the Bloodstone was divided and sealed. Abaddon's rejection of that decision escalated into open war against the other gods. The entire crisis marks Year 0 of the Mouvelian calendar. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Record 01 // The Rebellion

Abaddon rejects the restriction of magic

Abaddon had been responsible for greatly expanding mortal access to magic.

When the other gods chose to restrain that power through the Bloodstone, he rejected their decision.

The disagreement became open rebellion.

Record 02 // The Margonites

Abaddon changes his followers

Abaddon's closest mortal followers were the Margonites.

During his rebellion, he transformed them into ethereal demonic beings.

They became his army in the coming war against the rest of the pantheon. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Record 03 // War Among Gods

Abaddon assaults the Gates of Heaven

Abaddon and his Margonite followers waged war against the other gods.

Their campaign reached the Gates of Heaven.

A conflict that had begun over mortal magic had now become a war within the divine order itself. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

Record 04 // Abaddon's Defeat

The rebellion ends at the Mouth of Torment

Abaddon's forces were ultimately defeated.

The decisive confrontation occurred at the Mouth of Torment.

Abaddon lost the war.

But the world around the battlefield would never look the same again.

Record 05 // A World Scarred

The Crystal Sea becomes a desert

The force unleashed during the divine conflict transformed the region surrounding the battle.

The floor of the Crystal Sea rose.

What had once been water became the Crystal Desert.

The lands farther south became the sulfurous Desolation. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Permanent Change

The geography of modern Elona and the Crystal Desert still carries the physical scar of this war.

Record 06 // Realm of Torment

Abaddon is cast into the Mists

Abaddon could not simply be left within Tyria.

He and his followers were imprisoned within the Realm of Torment.

The realm became the prison from which Abaddon would spend more than a thousand years attempting to influence the mortal world. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

Record 07 // Divine Censorship

The Five try to erase Abaddon from history

Defeating Abaddon was not the end of the response.

The remaining Five Gods attempted to remove his presence from mortal memory.

Records of him were destroyed.

His Orrian temple was sunk.

His influence was hidden or erased, and the Temple of the Six was pulled into the Realm of Torment. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

Record 08 // What Mortals Remembered

Later histories inherit an incomplete story

The attempt to erase Abaddon had consequences for the historical record.

Older sources often speak simply of "the gods" when describing the gift of magic.

Abaddon's specific role was obscured.

Some contradictions in later accounts may therefore reflect deliberate divine censorship rather than ordinary historical error. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

Record 09 // The Exodus

The Five withdraw into the Mists

The war had demonstrated how destructive direct divine intervention could become.

After Abaddon was imprisoned, the remaining Five Gods left Tyria and withdrew into the Mists.

Legends later claimed they departed to create other worlds.

Their withdrawal became known as the Exodus of the Gods. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

Record 10 // Year Zero

History begins counting from the Exodus

The Exodus became one of the great dividing lines of Tyrian history.

The event established Year 0 of the Mouvelian calendar.

Years before it became BE — Before the Exodus.

Years after it became AE — After the Exodus.

The age of gods openly walking among humanity was over. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}
