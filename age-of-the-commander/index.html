Volume IX // The Age of the Commander

The Age of the Commander

On the war against the Elder Dragons, the unraveling of the ancient Dragon Cycle, and Tyria's search for a new way to live with magic.

Compiled and Annotated By

Commander Q

Captain Commander of the Vanguard Knights
Researcher of Tyrian History and the Mists

Author's Preface

Killing the Dragons was never the whole answer

The previous record ended in 1325 AE with several Elder Dragons active and the peoples of Central Tyria living in a world shaped by their awakenings. For thousands of years, civilizations had survived the Dragon Cycle by hiding, fleeing, or enduring until the Dragons returned to sleep.

The generation that followed chose a different answer. The Durmand Priory, Vigil, and Order of Whispers eventually combined their strength, while Humans, Charr, Norn, Asura, and Sylvari increasingly fought beside one another against threats that ignored national borders.

At first, the lesson seemed simple: Elder Dragons could be killed. What took longer to understand was that the Dragons were also part of Tyria's magical structure. Their deaths released the enormous stores of magic they had consumed, and every victory changed the balance inherited by whatever remained.

The history of this age is therefore not merely the story of Dragons dying. It is the story of Tyria discovering why the old cycle existed and attempting to replace it before destroying the world along with it.

— Commander Q

I

Three Orders become one army

In 1325 AE, Zhaitan's growing campaign against Central Tyria brought the Durmand Priory, Order of Whispers, and Vigil to the same conclusion: none of them could defeat an Elder Dragon alone.

The assault on Claw Island made the danger immediate. The island guarded the approaches to Lion's Arch, and its fall demonstrated that Zhaitan's Risen armies were capable of carrying the war far beyond Orr.

The three Orders combined their personnel, knowledge, intelligence, and military strength to form the Pact. The Sylvari scholar Trahearne became its Marshal, while the adventurer who had risen through the conflict became his field commander.

For the first time in the modern Dragonrise, Tyria possessed an organized military force created specifically to wage war against an Elder Dragon.

II

The Pact carries the war into Orr

Rather than wait for another Risen invasion, the Pact advanced south toward Orr. The campaign required more than battlefield victories. Zhaitan's strength came from an entire corrupted ecosystem of Undead servants, magical artifacts, supply lines, and specialized Champions.

Pact forces attacked those systems one by one while Trahearne pursued his own Wyld Hunt: cleansing the corruption that had consumed Orr since Zhaitan's awakening.

The campaign gradually weakened the Elder Dragon and opened a route toward Arah, the ruined City of the Gods now occupied by the Risen.

The army that entered Orr was no longer simply a coalition of three Orders. It increasingly represented the combined technology, magic, scholarship, and military traditions of several peoples.

III

Tyria kills an Elder Dragon

In 1326 AE, the Pact confronted Zhaitan above the ruins of Arah. The campaign succeeded, and the Elder Dragon of Shadow and Death was killed.

The victory changed Tyrian strategy immediately. For generations, Elder Dragons had been treated as catastrophes to survive. Zhaitan's death proved that they were physical beings that could be weakened, fought, and ultimately destroyed.

What was not yet fully understood was what happened to an Elder Dragon's magic after death. Zhaitan had consumed extraordinary amounts of magical energy, and that power did not cease to exist when the Dragon did.

Commander Q // Observation

Zhaitan's death answered the question everyone had been asking: can an Elder Dragon be killed? The harder question was the one nobody yet knew to ask: what happens to the world afterward?

IV

Scarlet Briar searches beneath the world

The period following Zhaitan's defeat did not bring lasting stability. A Sylvari engineer named Scarlet Briar began assembling unlikely alliances between hostile groups and using them to carry out attacks across Central Tyria.

Her schemes involved the Molten Alliance, Aetherblades, Toxic Alliance, Twisted Watchwork, and other forces whose cooperation made little sense until the larger purpose became visible.

Scarlet had become aware of Tyria's Ley-Line Network and was searching for a point where enormous concentrations of magic converged beneath the surface.

That search eventually led her to Lion's Arch.

V

Scarlet wakes the Jungle Dragon

Scarlet launched a full assault upon Lion's Arch and drove her massive Breachmaker into the Ley-Line Hub beneath the city. Although Scarlet herself was defeated, the machine had already delivered the magical disturbance she intended.

Deep within the Heart of Maguuma, Mordremoth awakened.

The Jungle Dragon's influence spread through roots, vines, and magical pathways far beyond its immediate territory. The attack also revealed that Elder Dragon activity was intimately connected to the movement of magic through Tyria itself.

The Pact prepared another offensive, believing the lessons learned against Zhaitan could be repeated against the newly awakened Dragon.

The Heart of Maguuma proved otherwise.

VI

The Pact breaks in the jungle

The Pact launched a massive air fleet into the Heart of Maguuma, intending to strike Mordremoth directly. The offensive collapsed almost immediately as the Jungle Dragon tore the fleet apart and scattered its survivors across the region.

The disaster revealed another truth about Mordremoth. The Sylvari were connected to the Elder Dragon through their origin, making them vulnerable to its influence and allowing Mordremoth to turn some of them into the Mordrem Guard.

Pact survivors, local allies, and the Commander's companions continued the campaign from inside the jungle. Rather than attack Mordremoth as they had attacked Zhaitan, they exploited the Dragon's connection between body and mind.

In 1328 AE, Mordremoth was killed. Marshal Trahearne died in the final stages of the battle after becoming entangled with the Dragon's remaining power.

Tyria had now killed two Elder Dragons. The magical consequences were becoming harder to ignore.

VII

The victories begin destabilizing Tyria

The magic once contained by Zhaitan and Mordremoth entered the wider magical system after their deaths. Ley Lines carried enormous quantities of energy across Tyria, and places already rich in magic became increasingly unstable.

In 1329 AE, the Maguuma Bloodstone exploded. Bloodstone Fen was transformed by the release, while magical anomalies appeared in other regions.

At the same time, the White Mantle returned to open conflict with the Krytan throne, and several apparently separate crises became connected through competition for the growing concentrations of magical power.

The Dragon strategy had reached its first serious contradiction. Killing the Dragons removed immediate threats, but their deaths were also changing the magical balance of the world.

VIII

Glint's second scion awakens

In 1329 AE, Glint's surviving egg hatched within Tarir. The young Dragon who emerged was named Aurene.

Aurene inherited the legacy of Glint, who had spent centuries preparing for a future in which the Elder Dragon Cycle might be changed rather than merely endured.

Aurene formed a bond with the Commander and began absorbing magical energy around her. Unlike the Elder Dragons Tyria had fought, she could develop through cooperation rather than conquest.

Her existence offered the first evidence that the magical role of an Elder Dragon might someday be inherited without repeating the destructive behavior of the old cycle.

Why Aurene Matters

The problem was no longer simply how to kill Elder Dragons. Tyria needed something capable of receiving, filtering, and redistributing the magic they left behind. Aurene would eventually become that answer.

IX

A former God enters the Dragon War

Another claimant to Tyria's growing concentrations of magic soon appeared. A figure presenting himself as the Mursaat Lazarus the Dire was eventually revealed to be Balthazar, the former Human God of War.

Balthazar sought the power contained within the Elder Dragons and attempted to manipulate Primordus and Jormag for his own purposes.

Research into the magical structure of Tyria revealed the danger of simply allowing the two Dragons to die. Their destruction, on top of the deaths of Zhaitan and Mordremoth, threatened catastrophic instability.

Balthazar instead turned his campaign toward Kralkatorrik and entered the Crystal Desert with his Forged army.

X

Divine power passes into Dragons

In 1330 AE, Balthazar's campaign against Kralkatorrik brought the Commander and Aurene into direct conflict with the fallen God.

Balthazar was ultimately defeated in the Domain of Vabbi. As with Abaddon centuries earlier, destroying a former God did not destroy the magic contained within him.

The released power was absorbed by both Aurene and Kralkatorrik.

Kralkatorrik had already inherited magic from the deaths of other Elder Dragons. Balthazar's power strengthened the Crystal Dragon further and expanded its ability to corrupt forms of magic that had once belonged to other beings.

Commander Q // Observation

By this point the pattern should be clear. Powerful beings die, but their power does not. Magic changes hands. Every victory therefore creates the conditions of the next problem.

XI

Elona outlives Palawa Joko

The Commander's return to Elona also reopened a conflict that had shaped the region for centuries. Palawa Joko ruled through his Awakened armies, control of history, and the deliberate cultivation of his own mythology.

Joko eventually turned his attention toward Central Tyria and prepared a new outbreak of the Scarab Plague.

The Commander and allies disrupted the campaign and carried the war back into Joko's empire. In 1331 AE, Joko was defeated.

Aurene consumed the Undead king, preventing his regeneration and absorbing the unusual magical quality that had allowed him to return from death.

That inheritance would soon become critical.

XII

Kralkatorrik begins consuming reality itself

Empowered by the magic of fallen Dragons and Balthazar, Kralkatorrik became increasingly unstable and entered the Mists.

There, the Elder Dragon began consuming fragments of Divine realms and other parts of the Mists. The threat was no longer limited to territory within Tyria.

Glint, now existing within the Mists, helped organize resistance while Aurene prepared for the confrontation her mother had anticipated long before her birth.

At Thunderhead Peaks, Aurene and her allies attempted to kill Kralkatorrik. The Elder Dragon survived the attack, while Aurene was impaled upon crystalline spikes and appeared to die.

The plan seemed to have failed completely.

XIII

Joko's magic brings Aurene back

Aurene's death was not permanent. The magic she had absorbed from Palawa Joko allowed her to recover from wounds that should have killed her.

She returned and pursued Kralkatorrik through the Mists with the Commander.

The damaged Elder Dragon eventually crashed back into Tyria, bringing fragments of the Mists with him and creating the region known as Dragonfall.

There, Kralkatorrik was finally killed.

Aurene absorbed his released power and ascended in 1332 AE as the Elder Dragon of Crystal and Light.

A Different Kind of Elder Dragon

Aurene's ascension changed the problem facing Tyria. For the first time, an Elder Dragon capable of cooperating with mortal civilizations had inherited the power released by one of the old Dragons.

XIV

Charr division opens the way for Jormag

Aurene's ascension did not end the Dragon War. In Charr territory, Bangar Ruinbringer used fear of Aurene and resentment toward outside powers to gather followers around a new campaign.

His movement divided Charr society and developed into a Charr Civil War, while Jormag manipulated events from the north.

Jormag's power relied as much upon persuasion, promises, and corruption of loyalty as upon direct physical force. The conflict eventually produced the Frost Legion and brought the Ice Dragon fully back into the war.

By 1333 AE, the Charr Civil War had ended, but Jormag had awakened into a much more dangerous position.

XV

Primordus and Jormag are turned against one another

The ancient opposition between Primordus and Jormag offered a possible solution to a problem that had once seemed impossible. Each Dragon's power could be used against the other.

Braham Eirsson became connected to Primordus through the Spirits of the Wild, while Ryland Steelcatcher became the Champion of Jormag.

The two Elder Dragons were maneuvered into direct confrontation during the Dragonstorm.

In 1334 AE, Primordus and Jormag destroyed one another.

Their Champions survived, but another enormous concentration of Dragon magic had been released. Much of that power traveled south toward Cantha.

XVI

The road returns to Cantha

In 1335 AE, the activities of the Aetherblades drew the Commander and allies across the ocean into Cantha, reconnecting Central Tyria with an Empire that had spent generations largely separated from the outside world.

Cantha itself had changed dramatically. Advances in Dragonjade technology powered cities, industry, transportation, and machinery through captured and redirected magical energy.

Beneath that technological system was a secret known to only a small number of people.

The source helping stabilize Cantha's magical system was the Elder Dragon Soo-Won.

XVII

Soo-Won reveals why the Dragons existed

Soo-Won was revealed as the Mother of the Elder Dragons. In the distant past, she had created the other Elder Dragons to help filter the chaotic magic of the world into forms capable of sustaining Tyria.

Over immense spans of time, those Dragons developed into the beings whose hunger for magic drove the destructive cycle recorded throughout this archive.

Their existence therefore contained a contradiction. The Elder Dragons helped prevent magic from collapsing into uncontrolled chaos, yet their method of regulating it repeatedly devastated mortal civilizations.

With the other Dragons dead, their accumulated magic increasingly flowed toward Soo-Won. Even she could not contain the growing imbalance alone.

The chaotic state of magic known as the Void began emerging through her.

Commander Q // Observation

This is the point where the first volume and the ninth finally meet. The Dragon Cycle was never only a story about monsters waking up. It was an imperfect system for keeping the world's magic from collapsing back into chaos.

XVIII

Aurene inherits the burden

Soo-Won was eventually overwhelmed by the magical imbalance and the spreading Dragonvoid. At the Harvest Temple, the Commander, Aurene, and their allies confronted the final crisis created by the collapse of the old Dragon Cycle.

Soo-Won died during the struggle, and the Void was separated from her and defeated before it could return Tyria's magic to a state incompatible with mortal life.

Aurene absorbed Soo-Won's remaining energy and became the sole surviving Elder Dragon, inheriting responsibility for filtering the world's magic.

The ancient system of multiple Elder Dragons awakening, consuming magic, devastating civilization, and returning to slumber had finally ended.

Aurene offered another possibility: magical balance maintained by a Dragon that could cooperate with mortal life rather than repeatedly destroy it.

After learning to manage that burden, Aurene withdrew from ordinary affairs so that she could continue filtering Tyria's magic.

The Dragon Cycle had ended. History did not.

End of Volume IX

The road continues

From the earliest Dragonrise to the death of Soo-Won, Elder Dragons had stood behind many of the greatest transformations in Tyrian history. By 1335 AE, that structure was gone.

For the first time in thousands of years, the world entered an age whose future would not be defined by waiting for another generation of Elder Dragons to wake.
