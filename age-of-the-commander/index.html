The Age of the Commander

Tyria's war against the Elder Dragons and the end of the ancient Dragon Cycle.

Compiled and Annotated By

Commander Q

Author's Preface

Tyria fights the Dragon Cycle

Earlier civilizations survived Elder Dragons through retreat, concealment, preservation, and endurance.

The peoples of the modern age chose another answer.

They attempted to kill them.

Zhaitan's death proved that an Elder Dragon could fall. Mordremoth's death revealed the larger problem: the magic carried by an Elder Dragon did not disappear with it.

The years that followed became a struggle not only to defeat the remaining Dragons, but to understand the magical system they had occupied for thousands of years.

— Commander Q

I

The Pact takes the war to Orr

The campaign against Zhaitan brought the Durmand Priory, Vigil, and Order of Whispers into the same war.

Each approached the Elder Dragons differently. The Priory sought knowledge, the Vigil prepared armies, and the Order of Whispers relied upon intelligence and infiltration.

After the fall of Claw Island, their inability to fight separately became impossible to ignore.

Under Trahearne, the three Orders formed the Pact. Trahearne became Marshal, while the adventurer who had risen through the campaign became its field commander.

The Pact entered Orr by attacking the system that supported Zhaitan.

Eyes extended the Dragon's sight. Mouths consumed magical objects and power. Risen forces recovered bodies, weapons, ships, and artifacts for the Dragon's armies.

Pact forces destroyed Champions, broke supply lines, and denied Zhaitan magical resources while advancing toward Arah.

At the Artesian Waters, Trahearne used Caladbolg to cleanse the Source of Orr.

The road to Zhaitan was open.

II

Zhaitan dies

In 1325 AE, Pact forces reached Arah and attacked Zhaitan directly.

The Elder Dragon had already been weakened by the loss of important minions, supplies, and sources of magic.

Pact airships and weapons developed from the combined knowledge of several peoples brought down the Dragon.

Zhaitan was killed.

The victory ended the belief that Elder Dragons could only be survived until they returned to sleep.

They could die.

What Tyria did not yet understand was what happened to the magic stored within them.

Commander Q // Observation

Zhaitan answered the first question. The rest of the age would be spent dealing with the answer.

III

Scarlet wakes Mordremoth

After Zhaitan's death, Scarlet Briar began investigating the magical currents running through Central Tyria.

Her alliances and attacks concealed a larger search for the network now commonly known as the Ley Lines.

Beneath Lion's Arch, Scarlet located a major concentration of Ley energy and built the Breachmaker to reach it.

Scarlet was killed during her attack on the city, but the disturbance created by the Breachmaker traveled through the magical network toward the Heart of Maguuma.

Mordremoth awakened.

In 1328 AE, the Pact launched an airship offensive into the jungle.

Mordremoth destroyed much of the fleet.

The campaign also exposed the hidden connection between Mordremoth and the Sylvari. The Elder Dragon could call to their minds, and some Sylvari became the Mordrem Guard.

The Commander eventually confronted Mordremoth through both its physical network and its mind.

Mordremoth was killed.

Trahearne died with the last remnants of the Dragon's consciousness still connected to him.

The second Elder Dragon of the modern campaign was gone.

IV

Dragon magic returns to the world

The deaths of Zhaitan and Mordremoth released enormous quantities of magic.

Ley Lines carried that energy across Tyria. Creatures, artifacts, and places already sensitive to magic began changing as ambient levels increased.

In 1329 AE, the Maguuma Bloodstone exploded and released another major concentration of power.

The White Mantle returned to open conflict in Kryta, while old Mursaat and Bloodstone histories again became part of the present.

During the same year, Glint's surviving egg hatched within Tarir.

The young Dragon was named Aurene.

Aurene bonded with the Commander and began developing differently from the Elder Dragons that had defined earlier history.

Glint and the Exalted had prepared for her as part of a plan extending far beyond Kralkatorrik alone.

V

Balthazar's war strengthens Kralkatorrik

The growing concentrations of magic drew Balthazar back into Tyrian history.

The former God of War returned stripped of much of his former power and attempted to restore himself through Elder Dragon magic.

His first efforts involved Primordus and Jormag, but research into the magical balance of Tyria revealed that another uncontrolled Dragon death could be catastrophic.

Balthazar instead pursued Kralkatorrik.

In 1330 AE, Balthazar was killed in Elona.

His released Divine power was absorbed by both Aurene and Kralkatorrik.

Aurene grew stronger.

So did the Elder Dragon she had been prepared to replace.

Kralkatorrik later entered the Mists, consuming additional magical energies from former Divine realms.

At Thunderhead Peaks, Aurene and her allies attempted to kill him.

Kralkatorrik survived.

Aurene appeared to die.

VI

Joko's magic saves Aurene

Before the battle with Kralkatorrik, Palawa Joko had attempted to spread a new Scarab Plague beyond Elona.

The campaign was stopped, and Joko was defeated in 1331 AE.

Aurene consumed the Lich and absorbed the magic associated with his ability to return from death.

That power became critical after Thunderhead Peaks.

Aurene resurrected herself.

She pursued Kralkatorrik through the Mists with the Commander until the wounded Dragon crashed back into Tyria carrying fragments of several Mist realms.

The resulting landscape became Dragonfall.

There, Kralkatorrik was killed.

Aurene absorbed the released Dragon magic and ascended as an Elder Dragon.

The old Dragon had been replaced by a successor capable of cooperation with mortal civilization.

Commander Q // Observation

Glint's plan required more than Kralkatorrik's death. Something had to survive him.

VII

Jormag and Primordus destroy one another

Aurene's ascension left Jormag and Primordus among the remaining Elder Dragons.

Jormag became involved in a Charr Civil War after Bangar Ruinbringer attempted to claim the Ice Dragon as a weapon for the Charr.

The attempt failed.

Ryland Steelcatcher became Jormag's Champion, while Braham Eirsson became connected to Primordus.

The two Elder Dragons represented opposed magical forces.

Rather than kill one and release its power into the surviving system, Tyria turned them against one another.

During Dragonstorm in 1334 AE, Primordus and Jormag destroyed each other.

Their magic remained.

Much of it moved toward Cantha.

VIII

Cantha reveals Soo-Won

In 1335 AE, the Commander and Aurene reached Cantha.

The Empire had developed a technological society built heavily upon Dragonjade, which stored magical energy for transportation, communication, machinery, and industry.

That system was connected to an Elder Dragon hidden from most of the outside world.

Her name was Soo-Won.

Soo-Won was the oldest of the Elder Dragons and the mother of the others.

She described an ancient world in which magic existed in a far more chaotic state and explained the force later called the Void.

The Elder Dragons had become part of a system that divided and contained immense magical forces.

Their cycle brought destruction to civilizations, but their existence also helped regulate the magic those civilizations depended upon.

Research Note // The Dragon Cycle

Knowledge recovered in Cantha explains parts of the Dragon Cycle that earlier civilizations could only observe. Ancient records remain valuable evidence of how the cycle behaved even though the reason behind that behavior was understood much later.

IX

The magical system begins to collapse

By the time Soo-Won revealed herself, five of the other Elder Dragons were dead.

Their magic had moved through Tyria and increasingly accumulated within the surviving system.

Soo-Won struggled to contain the different magical domains gathering within her.

The Void began emerging through that instability.

Dragonjade systems failed as Soo-Won's condition worsened, threatening the Canthan infrastructure built around her power.

Soo-Won believed Aurene's prismatic nature could receive and filter the different forms of magic without repeating the old cycle.

The final conflict was therefore no longer about killing another Elder Dragon.

It was about preventing magic itself from falling back into chaos.

X

Soo-Won dies at the Harvest Temple

At the Harvest Temple, Soo-Won was overwhelmed as the Dragonvoid manifested through the collapsing magical system.

The Commander, Aurene, and their allies confronted the crisis while attempting to separate the destructive Void from Soo-Won.

Soo-Won died.

The Dragonvoid remained and had to be confronted as a threat of its own.

When the conflict ended, Aurene absorbed the remaining Dragon magic.

She was now the sole surviving Elder Dragon.

XI

Aurene becomes the new balance

Aurene did not continue the cycle followed by the old Elder Dragons.

She could receive different magical energies, filter them, and return that power to the world without consuming magic until civilizations were forced to survive an age of scarcity.

After learning to manage that burden, Aurene withdrew from ordinary affairs to concentrate upon maintaining the world's magical balance.

The ancient cycle of awakening, consumption, magical famine, and renewed slumber had ended.

The first records of this archive described peoples who could do little more than survive until the Elder Dragons returned to sleep.

Thousands of years later, the peoples of Tyria had destroyed the old Dragons, survived the consequences of those deaths, and helped establish a different system.

Another Dragonrise was no longer waiting to repeat the same history.

Commander Q // Observation

The Seers once protected the future by hiding magic from the Dragons. The modern world protected its future by changing the role a Dragon played in magic.

End of Volume IX

The Dragon Cycle is over

The Elder Dragons had shaped the oldest surviving histories of Tyria.

Their cycle had now ended.

Aurene remained as the world's prismatic Elder Dragon, maintaining a balance unlike the one that had governed earlier ages.

With the Dragons no longer at the center of every crisis, older mysteries and powers became visible again.
