Historical Archive // Record 09

The Age of the Commander

1325 AE – 1335 AE

For centuries, Tyria survived the Elder Dragons by fleeing from them.

In 1325 AE, that changed.

Orders united. Nations cooperated. Dragons began to die.

But every victory released more magic into the world, forcing Tyria to discover that killing the dragons was only half the problem.

Archive Context

This period contains the Guild Wars 2 Personal Story, Living World Seasons 1–4, Heart of Thorns, Path of Fire, The Icebrood Saga, and End of Dragons. It follows the collapse of the traditional Elder Dragon cycle and the rise of Aurene as a new form of magical balance.

Record 01 // Zhaitan

The three Orders unite

In 1325 AE, the threat of Zhaitan forced the Durmand Priory, Order of Whispers, and Vigil into cooperation.

Their alliance became the Pact.

Trahearne became its Marshal, while the future Commander served as second-in-command.

Record 02 // Claw Island

Zhaitan attacks the gateway to Lion's Arch

Zhaitan's forces assaulted Claw Island.

The attack demonstrated that the Risen threat could no longer be contained around Orr.

It also helped force the three Orders into the cooperation that created the Pact.

Record 03 // The First Dragon Victory

Zhaitan is defeated

The Pact pushed into Orr, established a campaign against the Risen, and eventually confronted Zhaitan.

In 1326 AE, the Elder Dragon was killed.

For the first time in the modern Dragonrise, mortal civilization had defeated an Elder Dragon.

Tyria had proven the dragons could die.

Record 04 // Living World Season 1

Scarlet Briar begins tearing at Tyria

After Zhaitan's death, a sylvari named Scarlet Briar began orchestrating attacks throughout Tyria.

She created alliances between groups that normally had little reason to cooperate.

Her armies and Twisted Watchwork struck settlements across the continent.

During this period, the karka attacked Lion's Arch, Southsun Cove became known to the wider world, and the Zephyrites returned.

Record 05 // Battle for Lion's Arch

Scarlet reaches the ley lines

Scarlet launched a massive assault on Lion's Arch.

Her Breachmaker drilled into a ley-line hub beneath the city.

Scarlet was defeated, but her true objective had already been achieved.

The surge of magic awakened Mordremoth.

Record 06 // Heart of Maguuma

The jungle itself becomes the enemy

Mordremoth's influence spread across the Heart of Maguuma.

The Zephyrite sanctuary crashed in Dry Top.

The Pact gathered a major fleet to attack the Elder Dragon.

The fleet was destroyed almost immediately upon entering the jungle.

The Pact campaign had become a disaster.

Record 07 // Heart of Thorns

Mordremoth dies after forty-three days of war

Survivors regrouped inside the jungle and continued the campaign.

After approximately 43 days, Mordremoth was defeated.

Marshal Trahearne died in the aftermath.

Two Elder Dragons were now dead.

Their released magic was beginning to reshape the world.

Record 08 // Living World Season 3

Magic destabilizes Tyria

With Zhaitan and Mordremoth dead, enormous quantities of magic entered Tyria's ley lines.

In 1329 AE, the Maguuma Bloodstone exploded.

Bloodstone Fen was reshaped, and magical anomalies appeared throughout Central Tyria.

At the same time, the White Mantle returned to open conflict with the Krytan throne.

Record 09 // Aurene

Glint's second scion enters the world

In 1329 AE, Aurene, the second scion of Glint, was born.

Her existence would become central to solving a problem Tyria had only begun to understand.

Elder Dragons consumed magic, but they also helped regulate it.

Simply killing them all threatened the world in a different way.

Record 10 // Path of Fire

A human god returns to wage war

The being initially believed to be Lazarus the Dire was revealed to be Balthazar.

The former God of War sought the power of the Elder Dragons.

He moved into the Crystal Desert with his Forged army and targeted Kralkatorrik.

Record 11 // A Second God Falls

Balthazar is killed

The Commander and Aurene confronted Balthazar in the Domain of Vabbi.

Balthazar was killed.

His released divine power did not disappear.

Kralkatorrik absorbed a large portion of it.

The Elder Dragon became even more dangerous.

Record 12 // Living World Season 4

Palawa Joko turns his attention north

Palawa Joko declared war on Central Tyria.

His forces prepared to spread the Scarab Plague.

The Commander and allies carried the conflict into Joko's Elona.

In 1331 AE, Joko was defeated.

Aurene consumed the lich, ending his centuries-long reign.

Record 13 // Kralkatorrik

The Crystal Dragon tears through reality

Empowered by magic from fallen dragons and Balthazar, Kralkatorrik entered the Mists.

He began consuming parts of the realms beyond Tyria.

Glint rallied spirits against him while Aurene prepared to fulfill the role she had been born for.

Record 14 // Thunderhead Peaks

Aurene appears to die

Aurene and her allies confronted Kralkatorrik at Thunderhead Peaks.

The battle left the Elder Dragon wounded.

Aurene was impaled by crystalline spikes and appeared to have been killed.

The victory Tyria had been preparing for seemed to have collapsed.

Record 15 // Resurrection

Aurene rises again

Aurene's death was not permanent.

The magic she had absorbed from Palawa Joko allowed her to return.

She pursued Kralkatorrik into the Mists with the Commander.

Record 16 // Dragonfall

The Crystal Dragon dies

Kralkatorrik crashed into Tyria, bringing fragments of the Mists with him to form Dragonfall.

There, the final battle took place.

Kralkatorrik was killed.

Aurene absorbed his power and ascended as the Elder Dragon of Crystal and Light.

A New Possibility

Aurene demonstrated that Elder Dragon magic could potentially be inherited and regulated rather than simply released back into the world.

Record 17 // The Icebrood Saga

Bangar Ruinbringer begins another war

Following Aurene's ascension, Imperator Bangar Ruinbringer gathered the charr legions.

His campaign triggered a charr civil war.

At the same time, Jormag began stirring once more in the Far Shiverpeaks.

By 1333 AE, the civil war had ended, but the dragon threat had not.

Record 18 // Champions

Mortals become instruments of dragons

Braham Eirsson was called by the Spirits of the Wild to fulfill an old norn prophecy.

He became the champion of Primordus.

Ryland Steelcatcher became the champion of Jormag.

The two Elder Dragons were deliberately brought into conflict with one another.

Record 19 // The Dragonstorm

Jormag and Primordus destroy one another

In 1334 AE, the opposing dragons confronted one another.

Jormag and Primordus destroyed each other.

Their champions survived.

The enormous amount of released magic flowed south toward Cantha.

Record 20 // End of Dragons

Tyria returns to Cantha

In 1335 AE, the Aetherblades returned to Central Tyria.

Their actions eventually carried the Commander and allies into Cantha.

The empire reopened its borders after centuries of isolation.

There, the final Elder Dragon was revealed.

Record 21 // The First Elder Dragon

Soo-Won carries the burden of the cycle

Soo-Won was revealed as the oldest surviving Elder Dragon and the source from which the others ultimately descended.

She had spent generations helping regulate the world's magic.

But the growing imbalance created by the deaths of the other dragons overwhelmed her.

She became corrupted by the Dragonvoid.

Record 22 // End of the Dragon Cycle

The old cycle finally ends

Soo-Won was defeated.

The traditional system of multiple Elder Dragons rising, consuming magic, and returning to sleep came to an end.

Aurene remained.

She became the primary living conduit through which Tyria's magical balance could be maintained.

The Commander declared the beginning of the Age of Aurene.

The Dragon Cycle was over.

Tyria's problems were not.

Beyond the Dragon Cycle

For generations, almost every major crisis in Tyria could eventually be traced back to Elder Dragons or the magic surrounding them.

That age was over.

What came next would pull the story toward the Wizard's Tower, the Kryptis, Nayos, Janthir, Titans, and mysteries far older than the Commander's war against the dragons.
