Historical Archive // Record 09

The Age of the Commander

1325 AE – 1335 AE

For centuries, the peoples of Tyria had survived the Elder Dragons by fleeing them, hiding from them, or enduring what they destroyed.

That changed in this era.

Armies united. Dragon champions were confronted directly. The cycle that had shaped Tyria since before recorded history was finally challenged at its source.

Archive Context

This archive follows the major historical events of the Guild Wars 2 personal story, Living World Seasons 1 through 4, Heart of Thorns, Path of Fire, the Icebrood Saga, and End of Dragons. Smaller regional stories can later branch from these records without overloading the main road.

Record 01 // Zhaitan's War

The dragon threat reaches Kryta

In 1325 AE, Zhaitan's forces attacked Claw Island.

The assault made it clear that no individual order could defeat the Elder Dragon alone.

The Durmand Priory, Order of Whispers, and Vigil were forced toward cooperation.

Record 02 // The Pact

Tyria creates a unified army

The three great orders formed the Pact.

The Firstborn sylvari Trahearne became Pact Marshal.

The future Pact Commander became his second-in-command.

Fort Trinity became the center of a campaign aimed not merely at surviving Zhaitan, but killing it.

Record 03 // The First Victory

An Elder Dragon is killed

Pact forces advanced into Orr and weakened Zhaitan's ability to sustain its armies.

In 1326 AE, Zhaitan was defeated.

For the first known time in the modern Dragonrise, mortals had killed an Elder Dragon.

The Consequence

Killing an Elder Dragon did not simply remove a monster. The magic it had accumulated was released back into the world, changing the magical balance of Tyria.

Record 04 // Scarlet Briar

A new enemy follows the ley lines

After Zhaitan's death, Scarlet Briar launched attacks across Tyria through strange alliances and Twisted Watchwork.

Her campaign appeared chaotic.

It had a destination.

Lion's Arch.

Record 05 // Battle for Lion's Arch

Scarlet reaches the ley line hub

Scarlet's forces invaded Lion's Arch.

The city was devastated.

Scarlet was killed, but her Breachmaker struck the ley line network beneath the city.

The disturbance awakened Mordremoth.

Record 06 // Heart of Maguuma

The jungle itself becomes hostile

Mordremoth's influence spread through the Heart of Maguuma.

Mordrem roots tore through the landscape while dragon minions attacked settlements and supply routes.

The Pact assembled a massive fleet to invade the jungle.

Mordremoth destroyed much of it before the campaign had properly begun.

Record 07 // Heart of Thorns

The Pact wins at enormous cost

Survivors regrouped and continued the campaign through the jungle.

After weeks of fighting, Mordremoth was defeated.

Marshal Trahearne died during the final struggle.

Another Elder Dragon was gone, and another enormous concentration of magic was released into Tyria.

Record 08 // Bloodstone Fen

Magic destabilizes the world around it

In 1329 AE, the Maguuma Bloodstone exploded.

The blast reshaped Bloodstone Fen.

At the same time, the White Mantle re-emerged as an open threat to Kryta.

The world was beginning to experience the consequences of rapidly changing magical levels.

Record 09 // A God Returns

Balthazar enters the conflict

Balthazar, the former god of war, returned to Tyria.

He sought enough power to wage war against the Elder Dragons.

His campaign brought destruction to places including Lake Doric and Draconis Mons before moving toward the Crystal Desert.

Record 10 // Glint's Legacy

Aurene enters the world

During this period, Aurene, Glint's second scion, was born.

Her existence represented something different from the Elder Dragons that had dominated previous ages.

She could form bonds with mortals and potentially inherit magical power without simply repeating the old cycle.

Record 11 // Path of Fire

A god dies in the Crystal Desert

Balthazar pursued Kralkatorrik through the Crystal Desert.

His Forged army battled Awakened forces and anyone standing between him and the Elder Dragon.

Balthazar was ultimately killed in the Domain of Vabbi.

Kralkatorrik absorbed much of the released divine power.

Record 12 // The Scarab Plague

Joko tries to carry his empire into Tyria

Palawa Joko turned his attention toward Central Tyria.

His plans included the use of a renewed Scarab Plague.

In 1331 AE, Joko was defeated.

His rule over Elona finally began to collapse.

Record 13 // The Crystal Dragon

Kralkatorrik becomes a threat to more than Tyria

Empowered by Balthazar's death, Kralkatorrik entered the Mists.

The Elder Dragon began consuming magical realms and tearing apart boundaries between worlds.

The conflict could no longer be contained to the physical map of Tyria.

Record 14 // Dragonfall

Kralkatorrik dies and Aurene changes the cycle

Kralkatorrik was eventually brought down at Dragonfall.

Aurene absorbed the Elder Dragon's released power.

She ascended as the Elder Dragon of Crystal and Light.

For the first time, an Elder Dragon aligned closely with mortal civilization stood within the magical balance.

Record 15 // The Icebrood Saga

The charr fracture while Jormag stirs

Imperator Bangar Ruinbringer launched a campaign built around controlling Jormag.

His actions helped ignite a charr civil war.

Ryland Steelcatcher became increasingly tied to Jormag, while the United Legions fought Bangar's Dominion.

The civil war ended, but the Elder Dragon threat did not.

Record 16 // Two Dragons

Primordus and Jormag are forced together

Jormag and Primordus fully reawakened.

Braham Eirsson became tied to Primordus through the prophecy of the Spirits of the Wild.

Ryland stood as Jormag's champion.

The two Elder Dragons were drawn into direct confrontation.

Record 17 // Fire and Ice End Together

Two Elder Dragons destroy one another

At Anvil Rock, Jormag and Primordus were brought into direct conflict.

The two Elder Dragons annihilated one another.

The magic released by their deaths flowed south.

Only one Elder Dragon from the ancient cycle remained.

Record 18 // End of Dragons

The road leads back to Cantha

The Aetherblades returned and events carried the Commander across the ocean to Cantha.

The empire reopened contact with the outside world.

There, the final Elder Dragon was revealed: Soo-Won.

Record 19 // The End of the Cycle

Soo-Won dies

Soo-Won became corrupted by the magical forces she had spent generations helping contain.

The final confrontation ended with her death.

With Soo-Won gone, the ancient Elder Dragon cycle that had defined Tyria for millennia finally ended.

The Commander declared the beginning of the Age of Aurene.

Tyria had survived the Dragon Cycle.

Now it had to discover what came after it.
