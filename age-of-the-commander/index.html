Historical Archive // Record 09

The Age of the Commander

1325 AE – 1335 AE

For generations, Tyria survived by retreating from Elder Dragons, rebuilding around the damage they caused, and hoping their attention turned elsewhere.

In 1325 AE, that pattern changed.

The peoples of Tyria began fighting back.

Archive Context

This era covers the central story from the beginning of Guild Wars 2 through End of Dragons. It follows the rise of the Pact Commander, the deaths of the Elder Dragons, the return of Balthazar, the rise of Aurene, and the eventual end of the ancient Dragon Cycle.

Record 01 // Claw Island

Zhaitan reaches toward Lion's Arch

In 1325 AE, the Risen launched an assault against Claw Island.

The attack was led by Blightghast the Plaguebringer, one of Zhaitan's powerful champions.

The island's fall exposed how vulnerable Lion's Arch and the surrounding nations remained to the Elder Dragon.

It also forced Tyria's three great orders toward cooperation.

Record 02 // The Pact

Tyria stops fighting the dragons separately

The Durmand Priory, Order of Whispers, and Vigil joined forces.

Together they created the Pact.

The Firstborn sylvari Trahearne became Pact Marshal.

The future player hero rose to become the Pact Commander and Trahearne's second-in-command.

Historical Turning Point

Earlier civilizations had repeatedly failed to defeat Elder Dragons. The Pact represented a new idea: the major peoples of modern Tyria could combine knowledge, armies, technology, and magic against a common enemy.

Record 03 // The Death of Zhaitan

An Elder Dragon falls

The Pact established Fort Trinity and began advancing into Orr.

Pact forces fought through Zhaitan's Risen armies while Trahearne worked to cleanse the corruption of Orr.

In 1326 AE, the Pact launched its final assault aboard the Glory of Tyria.

Zhaitan was killed.

For the first time in the modern Dragonrise, mortals had successfully defeated an Elder Dragon.

Record 04 // Scarlet Briar

A different threat begins moving across Tyria

Following Zhaitan's death, Scarlet Briar began launching attacks throughout Central Tyria.

She united unlikely groups, created Twisted Watchwork armies, and manipulated events across multiple regions.

At first, her attacks appeared chaotic.

They were not.

Record 05 // Battle for Lion's Arch

Scarlet strikes the heart of the free city

In 1327 AE, Scarlet launched a massive assault against Lion's Arch.

Her enormous drill, the Breachmaker, struck into a major ley-line hub beneath the city.

Lion's Arch was devastated.

Scarlet was eventually killed.

But her machine had already accomplished its true purpose.

Record 06 // Mordremoth

The Jungle Dragon wakes

The Breachmaker's strike sent magical energy through Tyria's ley lines.

Deep within the Heart of Maguuma, Mordremoth awakened.

Mordrem vines spread across the jungle and beyond.

The Pact began preparing another campaign against an Elder Dragon.

Record 07 // Heart of Maguuma

The Pact enters the jungle

In 1328 AE, the Pact launched an enormous airship offensive into the Heart of Maguuma.

Mordremoth attacked before the fleet could establish itself.

Airships were torn from the sky by massive vines.

Pact forces were scattered across the jungle.

The campaign began as a catastrophe.

Record 08 // The Death of Mordremoth

Victory carries a cost

After a campaign lasting roughly 43 days, Pact forces and their allies reached Mordremoth.

The Elder Dragon was defeated.

But Marshal Trahearne was lost during the final confrontation.

Mordremoth's death released enormous amounts of magical energy into Tyria's ley lines.

Record 09 // Bloodstone Fen

Ancient magic becomes unstable

In 1329 AE, the Maguuma Bloodstone exploded.

The blast reshaped Bloodstone Fen and contributed to the growing instability of magic across Tyria.

The White Mantle returned openly and began moving against the Krytan throne.

At the same time, strange magical phenomena appeared across the world as excess dragon magic continued moving through the ley-line network.

Record 10 // Aurene

Glint's second scion enters the world

In 1329 AE, Aurene, the second scion of Glint, was born.

Her existence represented a possibility Glint had prepared for long before her death.

Tyria might not simply need to destroy Elder Dragons.

Something would eventually need to take their place within the magical balance of the world.

Record 11 // Krytan Civil War

The White Mantle make their final bid for Kryta

The White Mantle launched a full rebellion against Queen Jennah.

Fighting spread through Kryta, including Lake Doric.

The civil war ended with the White Mantle's defeat.

Lazarus the Dire, the last known mursaat, also died.

An ancient civilization that had survived the previous Dragonrise had finally reached its end.

Record 12 // Path of Fire

A god returns to Tyria

The god Balthazar returned to the world and began pursuing Elder Dragon magic.

His campaign moved into the Crystal Desert with his Forged army.

Balthazar sought to kill Kralkatorrik and take the dragon's power.

His actions threatened to destabilize Tyria's magical balance even further.

Record 13 // The Death of Balthazar

Another god falls

Balthazar was confronted in the Domain of Vabbi.

He was defeated and killed.

The released magical energy was absorbed by Kralkatorrik and Aurene.

Kralkatorrik became even more powerful.

Record 14 // Palawa Joko

The Scourge of Vabbi turns toward Central Tyria

Palawa Joko had ruled Elona for generations.

Following the Commander's arrival in the region, Joko turned his attention toward Central Tyria.

His forces prepared to unleash a new Scarab Plague.

In 1331 AE, Joko was defeated.

Aurene consumed the lich, ending his reign over Elona.

Record 15 // War in the Mists

Kralkatorrik begins consuming realities

Empowered by Balthazar's magic, Kralkatorrik entered the Mists.

The Elder Dragon began consuming pieces of the realms within them.

Glint gathered spirits to resist him while Aurene and the Commander's allies prepared for a confrontation in Tyria.

Record 16 // Dragonfall

The Crystal Dragon dies

Aurene and the Commander pursued Kralkatorrik through the Mists.

The Elder Dragon was brought down, and pieces of several mist realms became entangled with his body to form Dragonfall.

Kralkatorrik was finally killed.

Aurene absorbed his magic.

Record 17 // Elder Dragon of Crystal and Light

Aurene takes a place in the cycle

After Kralkatorrik's death, Aurene ascended.

She became the Elder Dragon of Crystal and Light.

Unlike the dragons that came before her, Aurene could absorb different forms of magic without immediately becoming consumed by their conflicting energies.

Glint's plan was beginning to take shape.

Record 18 // The Icebrood Saga

Bangar Ruinbringer challenges the new world

Imperator Bangar Ruinbringer gathered the charr legions after Aurene's ascension.

He feared the political power represented by a dragon allied with the Commander.

Bangar sought to claim Jormag as a weapon of his own.

His actions ignited a charr civil war.

Record 19 // Fire and Ice

Two ancient enemies return

Jormag fully reawakened.

Soon after, tremors signaled the renewed activity of Primordus.

Destroyers attacked across Tyria while the Commander and allies worked to prevent either dragon from overwhelming the world.

Braham Eirsson was called upon by the Spirits of the Wild as the norn of prophecy.

Record 20 // Dragonstorm

Fire and ice annihilate one another

Primordus and Jormag were brought into direct conflict.

Their opposing magical natures were used against one another.

In 1334 AE, the two Elder Dragons destroyed each other.

Their released magic flowed south.

Only one Elder Dragon of the ancient cycle remained unknown.

Record 21 // End of Dragons

Cantha opens to the world again

The Aetherblades returned to Central Tyria and kidnapped Gorrik.

The pursuit carried the Commander and Aurene across the ocean to Cantha.

The empire began reopening its borders after generations of isolation.

There, the Commander learned the identity of the last ancient Elder Dragon.

Record 22 // The First Elder Dragon

Soo-Won stands at the beginning of the cycle

The final Elder Dragon was Soo-Won.

She was tied to the origins of the Elder Dragon family and had spent years helping stabilize Cantha through its dragonjade technology.

But the accumulation of unstable magic corrupted her.

The ancient Dragon Cycle reached its final crisis.

Record 23 // The Age of Aurene

The old cycle ends

Soo-Won was ultimately defeated and died.

With her death, the final Elder Dragon of the ancient cycle was gone.

Aurene remained as the primary force capable of managing the magical balance left behind.

The Pact Commander declared the beginning of the Age of Aurene.

A cycle that had shaped Tyria for tens of thousands of years had finally ended.
