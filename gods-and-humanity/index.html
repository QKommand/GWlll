Historical Archive // Record 03

The Gods and Humanity

Ancient Era – 1 BE

Divine Era Early Human History

Tyria was already ancient when the gods arrived.

Elder Dragons had already risen, ancient civilizations had already fought for survival, and the world carried scars from ages humanity had never witnessed.

Then divine beings entered from the Mists, brought humanity into Tyria, and began reshaping parts of the world according to their own vision.

Archive Context

The Human Gods did not create Tyria. The world, the Elder Dragons, and several ancient civilizations existed before their arrival.

The gods brought humanity into Tyria, but the location and circumstances of humanity's first arrival remain unknown. The earliest firmly dated human settlement appears in Cantha in 786 BE.

Orr would later become the great center of divine presence in Tyria, with Arah built above the sacred Artesian Waters.

Record 01 // From the Mists

The gods enter an ancient world

Sometime after the previous Dragonrise, divine beings entered Tyria from the Mists.

According to surviving Orrian history, Dwayna was the first to step forth.

She was followed by Balthazar, Melandru, Lyssa, and Abaddon.

These gods did not create the world.

Instead, they entered a Tyria that already possessed ancient magical systems, Elder Dragons, and civilizations thousands of years old.

An Existing World

The gods reshaped portions of Tyria after their arrival. Their influence was enormous, but it was imposed upon a world that already had its own history.

Record 02 // Humanity

The gods bring humanity into Tyria

Humanity did not originate on the world of Tyria.

The Human Gods brought humans into the world from elsewhere.

The precise location of humanity's original homeland remains unknown.

The location where humanity first entered Tyria is also not securely established.

Later evidence suggests early human populations moved northward from lands farther south than the regions familiar to modern Tyrians.

Important Distinction

The arrival of the gods at Orr should not be treated as proof that humanity first entered Tyria there.

The gods' arrival, humanity's arrival, and the later construction of Arah are related parts of early history, but the complete sequence remains unknown.

Record 03 // Orr

The gods establish themselves at the Artesian Waters

In Orr, the gods became associated with the immensely powerful Artesian Waters.

The waters lay beneath what would become the great city of Arah.

Arah became the center of divine presence in Tyria and the place most closely associated with the gods living openly among mortals.

Ancient Orr itself was described as fertile and verdant, with its natural beauty closely associated with Melandru.

The gods transformed and cultivated parts of the world around them, but they did not create the land upon which Arah stood.

Record 04 // 786 BE

Humanity appears in the dated record

In 786 BE, humans are firmly recorded along the northern coastline of Cantha.

This is the earliest securely dated human presence currently preserved in known history.

It does not necessarily mark humanity's first arrival on the world of Tyria.

Human populations would spread throughout Cantha, including regions later associated with the Echovald Forest and Jade Sea.

Record 05 // Cantha

The Empire of the Dragon rises

Human settlement in Cantha eventually developed into a unified empire.

In 510 BE, Lord Emperor Kaineng Tah united the human clans and established the Empire of the Dragon.

This event also marked the beginning of the Canthan Calendar.

Record 06 // 205 BE

Humans arrive in Orr and Elona

In 205 BE, human populations arrived by sea in Orr, Istan, and the southern valleys of Elona.

The surviving accounts describe these settlers as arriving from unknown lands.

Their exact connection to the earlier human populations of Cantha is not fully preserved.

Record 07 // Elona

The Primeval Kings establish Elona

Around 200 BE, the first Primeval Kings ruled from Fahranur, the First City.

The Primeval Dynasty began, together with the Dynastic Reckoning calendar.

Humanity was no longer simply migrating.

It was building kingdoms.

Record 08 // The Margonites

A maritime human civilization dominates the sea

By 175 BE, the human Margonite civilization was already established along the coasts north and west of Elona.

Their ships dominated much of the Unending Ocean.

The Margonites were originally human.

Their later supernatural form resulted from their corruption and transformation through Abaddon.

Record 09 // Ascalon

Humans push into Charr territory

By approximately 100 BE, humans had established themselves in Ascalon.

The land was not empty.

The Charr already controlled territory throughout the region.

Human expansion pushed the Charr north, beginning a conflict that would endure for more than a thousand years.

Record 10 // Divine Orr

The gods live among their followers

Orr became the spiritual center of human civilization.

The gods lived for a time among their followers in and around Arah.

Temples, settlements, and sacred sites spread throughout the kingdom.

The Cathedral of Silence became the first known temple dedicated to Grenth.

But the pantheon itself was not yet exactly the Six remembered in later human religion.

Record 11 // Grenth

A new god of death rises

Grenth was the child of Dwayna and a mortal sculptor.

In approximately 48 BE, Grenth and seven mortal heroes challenged Dhuum, the existing god of death.

Dhuum was defeated at the Cathedral of Eternal Radiance in Orr.

Grenth assumed his place within the pantheon.

From this point, the pantheon increasingly resembled the Six Gods remembered throughout later human history: Dwayna, Balthazar, Melandru, Lyssa, Abaddon, and Grenth.

Record 12 // Malchor

The gods begin withdrawing from mortal sight

The gods did not remain openly among humanity forever.

Their divine radiance could overwhelm mortal eyes, and over time the gods increasingly withdrew from direct public contact.

Dwayna commissioned the Orrian sculptor Malchor to carve great statues preserving the likenesses of the gods.

As each statue was completed, the god it represented withdrew deeper into Arah.

The statues became the enduring images through which generations of humans would know them.

Malchor's Fate

Exposure to divine radiance ultimately left Malchor blind.

After completing his work and realizing he could no longer look upon Dwayna, whom he deeply loved, Malchor threw himself into the sea.

Record 13 // The Retreat

Arah becomes a city behind closed doors

By the end of this gradual withdrawal, the gods were no longer freely walking among humanity as they once had.

They remained in Arah, above the same Artesian Waters associated with their presence in Orr.

This was not yet the Exodus.

The gods were still physically present within Tyria, but their relationship with mortal civilization had already changed.

Arah had become less an open city of gods and mortals, and increasingly the secluded seat of the pantheon.

Two Different Withdrawals

The retreat into Arah happened before Abaddon's rebellion.

The later Exodus of the Gods in 0 AE was a separate event, when the remaining gods left Tyria for the Mists.

Record 14 // Human Expansion

Humanity becomes a dominant force

Human civilizations had established themselves across Cantha, Elona, Orr, Ascalon, and Kryta.

Kingdoms rose. Borders expanded. Rivalries formed.

Humanity had transformed from newcomers to Tyria into one of the dominant political forces of the age.

Record 15 // The Turning Point

The balance of the world is about to change

The gods had reshaped parts of Tyria and guided human civilization for centuries.

They had also increasingly withdrawn from direct mortal contact, remaining secluded within Arah.

Then, in 1 BE, Abaddon dramatically expanded mortal access to magic.

The gift would transform civilization.

It would also begin the crisis that fractured the pantheon itself.

Era Summary

The world the gods shaped is about to turn against them

Tyria existed long before the Human Gods arrived.

They entered an ancient world, reshaped parts of it, brought humanity into it, and established Arah as the center of their presence.

Humanity spread across continents and built powerful civilizations.

The pantheon itself changed when Grenth replaced Dhuum as the god of death.

The gods eventually withdrew from daily mortal life and secluded themselves within Arah, leaving statues and temples as the most visible reminders of their presence.

Then Abaddon made a decision that would transform magic, humanity, and the gods forever.

Continue the Road Through Time

The Gift of Magic

In 1 BE, Abaddon dramatically expanded mortal access to magic.

What followed was not an age of enlightenment.

Magic became another weapon.

King Doric would travel to Arah and ask the gods to intervene, leading them to repurpose an artifact created thousands of years earlier by the Seers: the Bloodstone.
