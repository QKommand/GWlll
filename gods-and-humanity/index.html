YOU ARE HERE // A New Age Begins

Historical Archive // Record 03

The Gods and Humanity

Before 786 BE – 1 BE

The elder civilizations had inherited Tyria from ages shaped by the Elder Dragons.

Then beings from the Mists arrived, and with them came a people whose kingdoms would eventually dominate much of recorded history.

Humanity had entered Tyria.

Archive Context

This record follows the Six Gods and the expansion of humanity. The ancient races remain important, but their individual histories are covered in the previous archive. The story here is about the new civilizations that began replacing them as the dominant powers in the historical record.

Record 01 // From the Mists

The Six Gods enter Tyria

At an uncertain point in the ancient past, the beings later known as the Six Gods arrived in Tyria from the Mists.

Their first known point of arrival was the Artesian Waters beneath Orr.

They became central to human religion and to the development of several civilizations that followed.

Record 02 // The World of the Gods

The landscape changes around them

Human and charr traditions both remember the gods as beings capable of reshaping the environment around them.

They established Arah in Orr and became associated with the restoration and cultivation of lands touched by earlier destruction.

Orr would eventually become the spiritual center of human civilization on the Tyrian continent.

Record 03 // Cantha

Humanity enters the historical record

The oldest surviving human records date to 786 BE.

Humans appeared along the northern shores of Cantha.

Other human populations later settled the Jade Sea and Echovald Forest from lands whose locations have been lost to history.

Cantha became the earliest known center of recorded human civilization.

Record 04 // Kaineng Tah

Cantha becomes an empire

In 510 BE, the warlord Kaineng Tah united the human clans of Cantha.

He declared himself the first Lord Emperor of the Dragon.

The unification created the Empire of the Dragon and established the Canthan Calendar.

Human rule in Cantha had become organized, imperial, and increasingly powerful.

Record 05 // The Vassal Clans

Imperial unity does not mean peace

The Luxons and Kurzicks both attempted to break from imperial rule.

Emperor Yian Zho responded violently.

Both cultures were eventually forced into becoming vassal states of the Canthan Empire while retaining distinct identities and rivalries of their own.

Record 06 // Across the Seas

Human civilization spreads west

In 205 BE, humans arrived by sea on the shores of Orr, Istan, and the southern valleys of Elona.

Their original homeland remains unknown.

These settlers formed the foundations of new human nations separate from the Canthan Empire.

Record 07 // Elona

The Primeval Dynasty rises

In 200 BE, the first of the Primeval Kings ruled Elona from Fahranur in Istan.

This marked the beginning of both the Primeval Dynasty and the Dynastic Reckoning calendar.

Along the coast, the Margonites developed into a major maritime civilization whose ships dominated parts of the Unending Ocean.

Record 08 // Ascalon

Human expansion reaches charr lands

Human settlement expanded northward into the high plains later known as Ascalon.

In 100 BE, humans drove the charr north from lands the charr had previously controlled.

Doric was crowned king of the human lands of continental Tyria.

The conflict over Ascalon would outlive nearly every ruler who participated in it.

Long Memory

To later humans, Ascalon was an ancestral homeland. To the charr, it was territory that humanity had taken from them. Both histories shaped the wars that followed.

Record 09 // Human Ascendancy

A new political world takes shape

By the final century before the Exodus, human civilizations occupied large portions of the known world.

Cantha had an empire. Elona had dynasties. Orr stood near the City of the Gods. Human settlements spread across Kryta and Ascalon.

Many older peoples remained, but the written record increasingly centered on human kingdoms, rulers, religions, and conflicts.

Record 10 // The Edge of a Magical Age

Power is about to change hands

Humanity had spread across the known continents under the influence of gods, kings, emperors, and expanding civilizations.

Then one member of the pantheon made a decision that affected far more than humanity.

In 1 BE, Abaddon gave the peoples of Tyria far greater access to magic.

The result would immediately change the balance of power across the world.
