Historical Archive // Record 03

The Gods and Humanity

Before 786 BE – 1 BE

Tyria had already survived Dragonrises, ancient wars, and the decline of civilizations before humanity entered the historical record.

Then beings from the Mists arrived, and with them came a people whose kingdoms would reshape much of the known world.

Archive Context

The origins of both the Six Gods and humanity extend beyond Tyria's surviving historical record. What is known is that the gods came from the Mists and humanity arrived in Tyria through routes that later generations no longer remembered.

Record 01 // The Artesian Waters

The gods arrive from the Mists

At an unknown point in the distant past, the Six Gods came to Tyria from the Mists.

Their arrival is associated with the Artesian Waters in Orr, a place that would later become deeply tied to the gods and the city of Arah.

Dwayna, Balthazar, Melandru, Lyssa, Grenth, and Abaddon would become the central divine figures of human civilization.

Record 02 // A Changed World

The gods begin reshaping Tyria

Human and charr traditions both describe the gods as beings who transformed the lands they walked through.

They restored life to places scarred by earlier Dragonrises and established Arah in Orr as the City of the Gods.

Their presence marked the beginning of a new age in Tyrian history.

Record 03 // The First Human Records

Humanity reaches Cantha

The oldest surviving human records place humanity on the northern shores of Cantha in 786 BE.

These settlers established communities along the coast and eventually spread farther across the continent.

Other groups later settled the Echovald Forest and Jade Sea. Their exact origins were already lost to history, although Canthan traditions remembered that humanity had come from lands across the ocean.

Record 04 // The Empire of the Dragon

Kaineng Tah unites the Canthan clans

In 510 BE, the warlord Kaineng Tah united the human clans of Cantha.

He declared himself the first Lord Emperor of the Dragon and began construction of the first Raisu Palace.

The Canthan Calendar begins from this moment.

Historical Turning Point

Humanity had moved from scattered settlements to one of Tyria's earliest enduring human empires.

Record 05 // The Empire Fractures

Luxons and Kurzicks seek independence

After Kaineng Tah's death, his son Yian Zho inherited the empire.

The Luxon clans formally broke away in 462 BE, while the Kurzicks followed in 459 BE.

Yian Zho responded with brutal military campaigns. Both peoples were eventually forced into becoming vassals of the empire, but their hostility toward one another and toward imperial rule endured.

Record 06 // Humanity Crosses the Seas

New settlements appear in Orr and Elona

In 205 BE, human settlers arrived by ship on the shores of Orr, Istan, and the southern valleys of Elona.

Their homeland has been lost to history.

These humans did not appear to have come from Cantha, suggesting that multiple human migrations reached different parts of the world.

Record 07 // The Primeval Kings

Elona gains its first great dynasty

In 200 BE, the first of the Primeval Kings ruled Elona from the city of Fahranur in Istan.

This marked the beginning of the Primeval Dynasty and the Dynastic Reckoning calendar.

Over time, the Primeval Kings expanded their influence into Vabbi, the Desolation, and the lands surrounding the Crystal Sea.

Record 08 // The Margonites

A maritime civilization controls the ocean

By 175 BE, Margonite settlements existed along the coastlines north and west of Elona.

Their ships dominated the Unending Ocean, making the Margonites one of the most powerful maritime civilizations of the age.

Their future would become inseparable from the god Abaddon.

Record 09 // Ascalon

Humanity pushes north

In 100 BE, humans expanded into the lands later known as Ascalon.

The charr were driven north from territories they had previously conquered.

Doric was crowned king of the Tyrian human lands, creating a political center for humanity on the continent.

The conflict between humans and charr had begun.

Record 10 // Divine Presence

Mortals still speak directly with gods

The centuries before the Exodus contain accounts of the gods directly interacting with humanity.

Dwayna answered Doric during a long war. Balthazar aided a human army. Melandru punished those who rejected her teachings. Lyssa tested the people of Wren. Grenth gathered his earliest followers.

To humanity of this era, the gods were not distant figures of faith.

They were part of the world.

Record 11 // The Gift

Abaddon gives magic to the world

In 1 BE, Abaddon gave the knowledge and power of magic to the sentient races of Tyria.

Magic itself had existed before this moment, but the gift dramatically increased the amount of magical power available to mortal civilizations.

The consequences were immediate.

Races turned the gift against one another, and widespread magical wars erupted across the world.

Humanity stood on the edge of another catastrophe.
