Historical Archive // Record 03

The Gods and Humanity

Ancient Era – 1 BE

Divine Era Early Human History

The dragons slept. The elder civilizations had faded.

Then something new entered the world.

Powerful beings crossed from the Mists, and humanity was brought with them into Tyria.

The gods did not create Tyria. They entered an ancient world, reshaped parts of it, established Arah, and guided humanity according to their own divine visions.

Archive Context

Humanity did not originate on the world of Tyria. The Six Gods brought humanity to Tyria from somewhere beyond the world. The full story of humanity's original homeland remains unknown.

The gods themselves arrived after the previous rise of the Elder Dragons. They therefore entered a world that already possessed ancient civilizations, landscapes, magic, and history.

Their first known arrival is associated with the Artesian Waters in Orr, while the earliest firmly dated human presence appears in Cantha in 786 BE.

Record 01 // From the Mists

The Six Gods enter an ancient Tyria

Sometime before 786 BE, powerful divine beings arrived in the world of Tyria.

They would become known collectively as the Six Human Gods.

According to Orrian history, Dwayna was the first to step forth from the Mists.

She was followed by Balthazar, Melandru, Lyssa, Abaddon, and later Grenth.

The gods did not create Tyria.

The world, the Elder Dragons, and civilizations such as the Seers, Mursaat, Forgotten, Dwarves, and Jotun already possessed histories stretching far into the past.

An Important Shift

The arrival of the gods was not the beginning of Tyria.

It was the beginning of a new age within a much older world.

Record 02 // Orr

The gods arrive at the Artesian Waters

The earliest known divine presence on Tyria is associated with the Artesian Waters beneath Orr.

The waters formed a vast subterranean magical spring system beneath the region.

The gods were drawn to this powerful source of magic.

Orr would eventually become the spiritual center of their presence in Tyria.

Record 03 // The City of the Gods

Arah rises above the Artesian Waters

The gods established Arah above the Artesian Waters.

The city became the center of divine life in Tyria and would later be remembered as the place where the gods themselves walked.

Ancient Orr was not the dead wasteland encountered in the modern age.

It was remembered as a verdant land, particularly associated with Melandru, goddess of earth and nature.

The gods reshaped portions of the world they had entered, leaving their influence upon the landscape, cultures, and civilizations that developed around them.

The Gods Did Not Build an Empty World

Arah was constructed in a world already shaped by Elder Dragons, ancient races, and powerful magical forces.

The divine age layered a new civilization over a much older history.

Record 04 // Humanity

Humanity is brought into Tyria

Humanity did not originate on the world of Tyria.

The Six Gods brought humans into Tyria from somewhere beyond the world.

The precise location of humanity's original homeland and the exact route by which humans entered Tyria remain unresolved.

What survives is a scattered record of humanity appearing in different regions over the centuries that followed.

Important Distinction

The arrival of the gods and the earliest firmly dated human civilizations are not the same event.

The gods entered Tyria sometime before the first securely dated human presence in Cantha. The missing centuries between these events remain one of the unresolved areas of early human history.

Record 05 // 786 BE

Humanity appears in Cantha

In 786 BE, humans arrived along the northern coastline of Cantha.

This is the earliest firmly dated appearance of humanity preserved in the known historical record.

Human populations spread throughout the continent, establishing settlements across the region.

Cantha would become the site of one of humanity's oldest known enduring civilizations.

Record 06 // Cantha

The Empire of the Dragon rises

Human settlement in Cantha eventually developed into a unified empire.

In 510 BE, Lord Emperor Kaineng Tah unified the human clans and established the Empire of the Dragon.

This event also marked the beginning of the Canthan Calendar.

Record 07 // 205 BE

Humans arrive in Orr and Elona

In 205 BE, human tribes arrived by sea in Orr, Istan, and the southern valleys of Elona.

The surviving accounts describe these settlers as coming from unknown lands.

Their precise connection to the earlier humans of Cantha is not clearly preserved in the historical record.

From these migrations would eventually emerge some of the most powerful human civilizations in Tyria.

Record 08 // Elona

The Primeval Kings establish Elona

Around 200 BE, the first of the Primeval Kings ruled from Fahranur, the First City in Istan.

This began the Primeval Dynasty and the Dynastic Reckoning calendar.

Human civilization was no longer simply spreading.

It was building kingdoms.

Record 09 // The Margonites

Humans take to the Unending Ocean

By 175 BE, Margonite settlements had appeared along coastlines north and west of Elona.

Their ships came to dominate much of the Unending Ocean.

The Margonites were originally a human civilization.

Their later demonic appearance resulted from the corruption and transformation associated with Abaddon.

Their civilization would eventually become deeply intertwined with the god of knowledge and water.

Record 10 // Ascalon

Humans push into charr territory

By approximately 100 BE, humanity had established itself in Ascalon.

The land was not empty.

The charr had already established their own power in the region.

Human expansion drove the charr northward from territory they had previously conquered.

Some humans who remained in the contested lands were enslaved by the charr.

The struggle over Ascalon would continue for centuries and eventually become one of the defining conflicts of human and charr history.

Record 11 // 48 BE

Melandru binds a human tribe to the land

The Scriptures of Melandru preserve an account dated to 48 BE in which Melandru directly intervened in the lives of a human tribe.

According to the scripture, the tribe wandered the land while destroying the natural world wherever they established camp.

Their leader, Ewan, belonged to a people who rejected magic and threatened death to anyone among them who practiced it.

When the tribe attempted to move on, thorny branches suddenly grew from the earth and blocked their path.

A great tree then rose, revealing the form of Melandru.

She identified herself as the Mother of earth and nature and bound the tribe to the land they had harmed.

Branches grew from their limbs, and the blood within them became like the sap of trees.

Ewan and his people were transformed into stewards of nature.

Why This Matters

The account is dated only 48 years before the Exodus.

It preserves a tradition in which one of the gods still directly appeared, spoke to mortals, manipulated the natural world, and permanently transformed human beings.

The gods may have become increasingly distant from ordinary life, but the divine age had not yet ended.

Human Expansion

Humanity spreads across the known world

Human civilizations established themselves across Cantha, Elona, Orr, Ascalon, and Kryta.

Kingdoms rose. Borders expanded. Rivalries formed.

Humanity had transformed from a people brought into an ancient world into one of its dominant political forces.

Record 12 // The Turning Point

The world stands on the edge of change

Humanity had spread widely across Tyria.

The gods remained deeply involved in mortal civilization.

Their temples, teachings, interventions, and magical influence had shaped human societies for generations.

But the balance of the world was about to change.

In 1 BE, Abaddon would grant widespread access to magic.

The gift would transform civilization.

It would also begin a chain of events that would eventually end the age when the gods openly inhabited Tyria.

Era Summary

A new world has taken shape

The gods entered an ancient world whose history stretched back long before humanity.

They established themselves around the Artesian Waters, built Arah, reshaped parts of the world, and brought humanity into Tyria.

Humanity then spread across multiple continents and established kingdoms, empires, religions, and rivalries of its own.

The gods remained capable of directly intervening in mortal life, as traditions such as the Scriptures of Melandru continued to record.

But the divine age was approaching its breaking point.

One god's decision about the nature of magic would transform the relationship between mortals, magic, and the divine forever.

Continue the Road Through Time

The Gift of Magic

In 1 BE, Abaddon gave widespread access to magic.

What followed was not an age of simple enlightenment.

Magic became another weapon.

King Doric would eventually travel to Arah and beg the gods to intervene, leading them back to an artifact created thousands of years earlier by the Seers: the Bloodstone.
