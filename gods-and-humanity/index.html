Historical Archive // Record 03

The Gods and Humanity

Ancient Era – 1 BE

Divine Era Early Human History

The dragons slept. The elder civilizations had faded.

Then something new entered the world.

Powerful beings crossed from the Mists, and humanity was brought with them into Tyria.

The age that followed would reshape the world almost as dramatically as the dragons had before them.

Archive Context

Humanity did not originate on the world of Tyria. Human tradition and surviving records describe a homeland somewhere within the Mists, from which the Six Gods brought humanity into Tyria.

The exact sequence of humanity's earliest migrations is incomplete. The gods' first known arrival is associated with the Artesian Waters in Orr, while the earliest firmly dated human presence appears in Cantha in 786 BE.

Record 01 // From the Mists

The Six Gods enter Tyria

Sometime before 786 BE, powerful divine beings arrived in Tyria from the Mists.

They would become known collectively as the Six Human Gods.

According to human tradition, Dwayna was the first of the gods to appear in the world.

Their arrival marked the beginning of an entirely new chapter in Tyrian history.

Record 02 // Orr

The gods first set foot at the Artesian Waters

The gods were drawn to a powerful source of magic within the Artesian Waters of Orr.

Human scriptures identify this spring system as the place where the gods first set foot upon Tyria.

They later built Arah above the subterranean waters, creating a city that became the center of divine presence in the world.

Orr's connection to the gods therefore reaches back to the beginning of their known presence in Tyria.

Record 03 // Humanity

Humanity is brought into Tyria

Humanity originated on another world within the Mists.

The Six Gods brought humans from that distant homeland into the world of Tyria.

The surviving record strongly connects humanity's arrival with the gods and their early presence around Orr, but does not preserve a complete chronology of every migration that followed.

Whatever civilization existed on humanity's original homeworld has largely disappeared from surviving history.

Important Distinction

The Artesian Waters are identified as the place where the gods first entered Tyria. Humanity was brought to Tyria by those gods, but the earliest firmly dated human settlement in the surviving record is found centuries later in Cantha.

Record 04 // 786 BE

Humanity appears in Cantha

In 786 BE, humans arrived along the northern coastline of Cantha.

This is the earliest firmly dated appearance of humanity preserved in the known historical record.

Human populations would spread through the continent, including regions that later became associated with the peoples of the Echovald Forest and the Jade Sea.

Cantha would become the site of one of humanity's oldest known enduring civilizations.

Record 05 // Cantha

The Empire of the Dragon rises

Human settlement in Cantha eventually developed into a unified empire.

In 510 BE, Lord Emperor Kaineng Tah unified the human clans and established the Empire of the Dragon.

This event also marked the beginning of the Canthan Calendar.

Record 06 // 205 BE

Humans arrive in Orr and Elona

In 205 BE, human settlers arrived by sea in Orr, Istan, and the southern valleys of Elona.

The surviving accounts describe these settlers as coming from unknown lands.

Their precise connection to the earlier humans of Cantha is not clearly preserved in the historical record.

From these migrations would eventually emerge some of the most powerful human civilizations in Tyria.

Record 07 // Elona

The Primeval Kings establish Elona

Around 200 BE, the first of the Primeval Kings ruled from Fahranur, the First City in Istan.

This began the Primeval Dynasty and the Dynastic Reckoning calendar.

Human civilization was no longer simply spreading.

It was building kingdoms.

Record 08 // The Margonites

Humans take to the Unending Ocean

By 175 BE, Margonite settlements had appeared along coastlines north and west of Elona.

Their ships came to dominate much of the Unending Ocean.

The Margonites would eventually become closely associated with Abaddon, the god of knowledge and water.

Record 09 // Ascalon

Humans push into charr territory

By approximately 100 BE, humanity had settled Ascalon.

The land was not empty.

Human expansion drove the charr north from territory they had previously conquered.

The struggle over Ascalon would outlive kingdoms, dynasties, and generations.

Record 10 // Human Expansion

Humanity spreads across the known world

Human civilizations established themselves across Cantha, Elona, Orr, Ascalon, and Kryta.

Kingdoms rose. Borders expanded. Rivalries formed.

Humanity had transformed from a people brought from beyond Tyria into one of the dominant political forces of the age.

Record 11 // The Turning Point

The world stands on the edge of change

Humanity had spread widely across Tyria.

The gods remained deeply involved in mortal affairs.

But the balance of the world was about to change.

In 1 BE, Abaddon would grant widespread access to magic.

The gift would transform civilization.

It would also begin a chain of events that would eventually drive the gods themselves from Tyria.

Era Summary

A new world has taken shape

The world inherited from the ancient Dragonrise was no longer dominated by elder civilizations.

Humanity had been brought from a distant world within the Mists, spread across multiple continents, and established powerful kingdoms.

The Six Gods had become central figures in human life.

Then one god made a decision that would change the relationship between mortals, magic, and the divine forever.

Continue the Road Through Time

The Gift of Magic

In 1 BE, Abaddon gave magic freely to the peoples of Tyria.

What followed was not an age of enlightenment.

Magic became another weapon.

King Doric would eventually travel to Arah and beg the gods to intervene, leading them back to an artifact created thousands of years earlier by the Seers: the Bloodstone.
