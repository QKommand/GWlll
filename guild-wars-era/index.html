The Guild Wars Era

1070 AE – 1080 AE

For generations, the human kingdoms had fought one another. Then the charr came south in force.

Within only a few years, Ascalon was scorched, Orr was drowned, Kryta fell under the White Mantle, Shiro returned to Cantha, and Abaddon's ancient plan threatened the world through Nightfall.

Archive Context

This era contains the central events of the original Guild Wars campaigns and their immediate aftermath: Prophecies, Factions, Nightfall, Eye of the North, and Guild Wars Beyond.

Record 01 // The Charr Invasion

The Guild Wars end beneath a larger threat

In 1070 AE, the charr launched a massive invasion south into the human kingdoms.

Ascalon, Kryta, and Orr had been weakened by the Third Guild War.

The invasion forced the human kingdoms to abandon their struggle with one another and turn toward the enemy approaching from the north.

The Guild Wars ended.

The disasters that followed were worse.

Record 02 // The Searing

Ascalon burns

The charr used powerful magical Searing Cauldrons in a ritual that brought fire and destruction across Ascalon.

The event became known as the Searing.

Cities were destroyed, the landscape was scorched, and the Great Northern Wall was breached.

Ascalon survived, but the kingdom would never fully recover.

Hidden Influence

The charr's Titan gods and the magical power behind the Searing were ultimately connected to Abaddon's agents.

Record 03 // Orr Falls

An entire kingdom sinks beneath the sea

Charr forces moved toward Orr after devastating Ascalon.

Vizier Khilbron turned to a forbidden scroll in an effort tied to the defense of the kingdom.

The resulting spell caused the Cataclysm of Orr.

The peninsula sank beneath the sea and nearly every person within Orr died.

Khilbron survived only by becoming undead.

Abaddon's Shadow

Khilbron was a follower of Abaddon, tying Orr's destruction to the fallen god's long attempt to influence Tyria.

Record 04 // Kryta

The Unseen Ones save a kingdom

Kryta faced its own charr invasion.

King Jadon fled, leaving the kingdom without effective leadership.

Saul D'Alessio and the White Mantle stepped forward.

With the help of the mursaat, worshiped as the Unseen Ones, the White Mantle defeated the invading charr.

Kryta survived.

But salvation came with a hidden cost.

Record 05 // The Flameseeker Prophecies

The prophecy begins to fulfill itself

Ascalonian refugees crossed the Shiverpeaks toward Kryta under Prince Rurik.

Rurik died protecting the refugees from the Stone Summit.

In Kryta, heroes discovered that the White Mantle were sacrificing innocents to power soul batteries.

Those sacrifices helped sustain the mursaat's defense against Glint's Flameseeker Prophecies.

The Shining Blade rebellion and the actions of Tyrian heroes ultimately brought the prophecy toward completion.

Record 06 // The Titans

The mursaat fall

The Flameseeker Prophecies led heroes to the Ring of Fire.

The surviving mursaat were devastated, and Khilbron, now the Lich Lord, opened the Door of Komalie.

Titans poured into Tyria.

Khilbron was defeated, and the Titans were eventually destroyed or driven back.

The mursaat survived only in tiny numbers.

Record 07 // Factions

The Jade Wind's architect returns

In Cantha, the spirit of Shiro Tagachi returned more than two centuries after his death.

He unleashed the Affliction, transforming victims into violent, corrupted creatures.

Shiro also created armies of Shiro'ken.

The crisis spread from Shing Jea Island into Kaineng City, the Echovald Forest, and the Jade Sea.

Record 08 // Shiro's Second Fall

Cantha survives the Affliction

Canthan heroes joined with allies from the Kurzicks and Luxons against Shiro.

The dragon Kuunavang was freed from corruption and joined the struggle.

Shiro was defeated again, ending the immediate threat.

The Afflicted, however, remained throughout Cantha and would take years to eliminate.

Record 09 // Nightfall

Abaddon's plan reaches Elona

In 1075 AE, Warmarshal Varesh Ossa attempted to bring about Nightfall.

Nightfall would merge parts of Tyria with the Realm of Torment, allowing Abaddon to escape.

Demons entered Elona while the landscape itself began to twist under the influence of the fallen god.

The Sunspears and Order of Whispers united against Varesh and Abaddon.

Record 10 // An Old Enemy Returns

Palawa Joko leaves his prison

Crossing the Desolation required the help of a familiar enemy.

Palawa Joko was released from the prison where Turai Ossa's forces had sealed him centuries earlier.

Joko aided the heroes with knowledge of the Desolation and used his Awakened forces against the Kournans.

Once the immediate crisis ended, however, nothing prevented Joko from rebuilding his own power.

Record 11 // The Death of a God

Abaddon's thousand-year plan ends

Heroes entered the Realm of Torment and confronted Abaddon.

The fallen god was defeated and killed.

But divine power could not simply disappear.

The released magical energy threatened to destroy Tyria.

Kormir entered the unstable power and emerged as the new Goddess of Truth.

The Pantheon Changes

Abaddon was replaced by Kormir, preserving the balance of the divine power that had been released.

Record 12 // The Earth Shakes

Something ancient stirs below Tyria

In 1078 AE, earthquakes opened passages throughout Tyria.

Creatures of stone and fire known as Destroyers emerged from the depths.

The crisis brought several peoples into greater contact: the asura were driven toward the surface, the norn became known to southern races, and the dwarves faced an ancient prophecy.

Record 13 // The Great Destroyer

The dwarves sacrifice their old future

The Destroyer invasion fulfilled ancient dwarven legends concerning the Great Destroyer.

King Jalis Ironhammer invoked the Rite of the Great Dwarf.

Dwarves began transforming into beings of living stone.

They descended into the Depths to wage a war against Primordus and the Destroyers.

The transformation marked the beginning of the end of dwarven civilization as Tyria had known it.

Record 14 // The First Dragon Stirs

The ancient cycle begins again

Beneath Tyria, the Elder Dragon Primordus began to stir.

The Great Destroyer was defeated before Primordus could fully awaken.

The dragon remained dormant.

But the age of Elder Dragons was returning.

Record 15 // War in Kryta

The White Mantle lose the throne

The White Mantle continued ruling Kryta even after the devastation of the mursaat.

The Shining Blade rallied around Princess Salma.

In 1079 AE, the Krytan civil war ended with the defeat of the White Mantle government.

Salma was crowned, and the Seraph were established.

Record 16 // Winds of Change

Cantha purges the Afflicted

The Ministry of Purity, formally established by Reiko Murakami in 1077 AE, rose to power during the effort to eliminate the remaining Afflicted.

By 1079 AE, the organization had begun its final campaign against the Affliction.

Reiko's growing authoritarian movement was eventually defeated, but the political direction of Cantha had begun to change.

Record 17 // The End of an Era

The world prepares for the centuries ahead

In 1080 AE, King Adelbern recalled the Ebon Vanguard from the north.

They fortified Ebonhawke, establishing what would become one of humanity's last major strongholds in Ascalon.

The great crises of the Guild Wars campaigns had passed.

But beneath the surface, Elder Dragons were beginning to awaken.
