Historical Archive // Record 07

The Guild Wars Era

1070 AE – 1080 AE

The human kingdoms had spent generations fighting one another.

Then the charr came south.

In a single decade, kingdoms collapsed, ancient prophecies were fulfilled, forgotten gods returned to the center of history, and the first signs of a far older threat began shaking Tyria from below.

Archive Context

This era covers the central events of Guild Wars: Prophecies, Factions, Nightfall, Eye of the North, and Guild Wars Beyond. The long build-up to these events belongs to the previous archive. Here, the focus is on what actually breaks the old world apart.

Record 01 // The Charr Invasion

The Third Guild War ends in fire

In 1070 AE, the charr launched a massive invasion south into Ascalon.

Their assault ended the Third Guild War almost overnight.

Human armies that had spent years fighting each other were suddenly forced to defend their own borders.

The first great blow fell on Ascalon.

Record 02 // The Searing

Ascalon is transformed

The charr used Searing Cauldrons and rituals tied to their Titan gods to call down fire upon Ascalon.

The destruction scorched the plains, shattered settlements, and breached the Great Northern Wall.

Ascalon survived as a kingdom, but it would never return to what it had been before.

Turning Point

The Searing marks the beginning of the playable story of Guild Wars: Prophecies and the collapse of the old balance between the human kingdoms.

Record 03 // The Charr Move South

The invasion spreads beyond Ascalon

Charr forces continued west and south toward Kryta and Orr.

Kryta survived through the intervention of the White Mantle and the mursaat they worshiped as the Unseen Ones.

Orr met a far darker fate.

Record 04 // The Cataclysm

Orr destroys itself to stop the charr

As the charr advanced across Orr, Vizier Khilbron used a forbidden spell.

The resulting Cataclysm sank the Orrian peninsula beneath the sea.

Nearly everyone still in Orr died.

Khilbron survived in a changed form, becoming the Undead Lich.

Record 05 // Prophecies

The mursaat try to prevent their own destruction

The events of Guild Wars: Prophecies brought the Flameseeker Prophecies toward fulfillment.

The White Mantle sacrificed Chosen humans to power soul batteries used by the mursaat.

Their goal was to keep the Door of Komalie sealed and prevent the Titans from entering Tyria.

The effort failed.

Record 06 // The Door of Komalie

The prophecy comes true

The Undead Lich attempted to use the Scepter of Orr to open the Door of Komalie.

The mursaat were nearly wiped out as the Flameseeker Prophecies reached their conclusion.

Titans poured into Tyria.

The Lich was defeated, and the invading Titans were eventually hunted down and destroyed.

Record 07 // Factions

Cantha faces the return of Shiro Tagachi

In late 1072 AE, Shiro Tagachi returned as an Envoy.

His presence unleashed the Affliction, a plague that twisted living beings into grotesque creatures.

The crisis spread from Shing Jea into Kaineng City, the Echovald Forest, and the Jade Sea.

Shiro was ultimately defeated again.

Record 08 // Nightfall

Abaddon's old plan reaches Elona

In 1075 AE, Warmarshal Varesh Ossa worked to bring about Nightfall.

The Realm of Torment began bleeding into Elona.

Demons entered the physical world, and the landscape itself began changing under Abaddon's influence.

The imprisoned god was attempting to return.

Record 09 // The Death of a God

Abaddon is finally destroyed

Varesh was defeated, and the campaign continued into the Realm of Torment.

There, Abaddon was killed.

His released power threatened to destroy Tyria if left uncontrolled.

Kormir absorbed that power and ascended, becoming the new Goddess of Truth.

The pantheon had changed.

Record 10 // An Old Enemy Returns

Palawa Joko walks free

The campaign against Nightfall also resulted in the release of Palawa Joko from his prison.

He assisted the Sunspears when their interests temporarily aligned.

Once the crisis ended, Joko was free to rebuild his Awakened armies and resume plans that would reshape Elona generations later.

Record 11 // Eye of the North

A deeper threat begins moving beneath Tyria

In 1078 AE, earthquakes erupted across Tyria.

Underground passages opened and creatures known as Destroyers began appearing.

The crisis forced the asura toward the surface and brought southern peoples into greater contact with the norn.

The threat came from far below the conflicts of human kingdoms.

Record 12 // The Great Destroyer

The dwarves fulfill an ancient prophecy

The Destroyer invasion culminated in battle against the Great Destroyer.

The dwarves underwent the Rite of the Great Dwarf, transforming themselves into stone.

They descended into the depths to wage war against the Destroyers.

Their civilization would never return to the surface in its old form.

Record 13 // The Dragon Beneath

The Destroyers have a master

The Great Destroyer was only a champion.

Beneath Tyria rested the Elder Dragon Primordus.

The dragon stirred but did not fully awaken.

The immediate threat was stopped.

The deeper cycle had already begun moving again.

Record 14 // War in Kryta

The White Mantle finally lose control

In 1079 AE, the Krytan civil war reached its conclusion.

The Shining Blade and their allies defeated the White Mantle leadership.

Princess Salma was crowned, restoring the royal line.

The Seraph were established as part of the new Krytan order.

Record 15 // Cantha After Shiro

The Ministry of Purity rises

In Cantha, the Ministry of Purity expanded its campaign against the remaining Afflicted.

Minister Reiko Murakami turned that campaign into a broader political movement.

Reiko was eventually killed, but the Ministry remained powerful enough to influence the direction of Cantha long afterward.

Record 16 // The End of the Guild Wars Era

Tyria survives, but the next age is already arriving

By 1080 AE, the great crises of the Guild Wars era had been contained.

Abaddon was dead. The Titans had been defeated. Shiro was gone. Kryta had broken the White Mantle's rule.

But beneath Tyria, Primordus had stirred.

The world had spent generations worrying about kingdoms, gods, guilds, and prophecy.

Soon, Elder Dragons would become the defining threat.
