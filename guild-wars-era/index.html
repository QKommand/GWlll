Volume VII // The Guild Wars Era

The Guild Wars Era

The collapse of old kingdoms, the great crises of 1070–1080 AE, and the first signs of the returning Dragon Cycle.

Author's Preface

The world changes quickly

The Third Guild War had consumed the northern Human kingdoms for decades. Then the Charr invaded.

Within ten years, Ascalon was devastated, Orr was destroyed, the White Mantle lost control of Kryta, Shiro Tagachi returned in Cantha, Abaddon died in the Realm of Torment, Kormir ascended, and the Destroyers revealed the approach of an Elder Dragon.

The histories of kingdoms were beginning to collide with powers much older than the kingdoms themselves.

— Commander Q

I

The Third Guild War reaches its end

The Third Guild War continued for more than half a century after its beginning in 1013 AE.

Ascalon and Kryta remained deeply involved, while Orr eventually entered the conflict in an unsuccessful attempt to restore peace.

During the closing years of the war, Saul D'Alessio encountered the ancient Mursaat and founded the White Mantle, believing his new patrons to be Divine beings known as the Unseen Ones.

The Mursaat were survivors of the ancient world, returning to Human politics after thousands of years.

II

The Charr invade

In 1070 AE, the Charr launched a major invasion of the Human kingdoms.

At the Great Northern Wall, Charr forces used magical cauldrons supplied through the Flame Legion's new religion to perform the Ritual of the Searing.

Fire devastated Ascalon, destroying settlements, farmland, forests, and much of the kingdom's ability to sustain itself.

The Charr invasion forced the Human kingdoms away from their long war with one another. The Third Guild War ended.

Kryta also faced Charr attack, but Saul D'Alessio and the White Mantle fought them with Mursaat assistance. The order that helped preserve Kryta soon became one of its dominant political powers.

Commander Q // Observation

The Guild War did not end with peace. It ended because another war replaced it.

III

Orr is destroyed

In 1071 AE, the Charr invasion reached Orr.

Vizier Khilbron turned to forbidden magic in an attempt to stop the invading army.

The resulting Cataclysm destroyed the Charr forces and Orr with them.

Much of the peninsula sank beneath the sea. Cities collapsed, most of the population died, and Arah disappeared beneath the waters along with temples, vaults, relics, and collections preserved since the Divine Age.

Khilbron survived as an Undead Lich.

Historical Caution // Khilbron

Khilbron's role in the Cataclysm is established, but the surviving record complicates how much he understood about the spell and its consequences before using it.

IV

The Flameseeker Prophecies are fulfilled

In 1072 AE, refugees fleeing ruined Ascalon entered a Kryta increasingly controlled by the White Mantle.

Humans identified as Chosen were secretly taken to the Maguuma Bloodstone and sacrificed. Their souls helped maintain the magical system surrounding the Door of Komalie.

The Mursaat feared what would happen if the Door opened. Glint's Flameseeker Prophecies foretold their destruction through the release of the Titans.

The Shining Blade exposed the Mantle's sacrifices, and the conflict eventually led through the Crystal Desert, Ascension, and an encounter with Glint.

At the Door of Komalie, Khilbron manipulated events toward opening the portal. The Titans emerged, the Mursaat guarding the Door were devastated, and the Lich was defeated.

The prophecy recorded centuries earlier had reached its fulfillment.

V

Shiro returns to Cantha

During the same year, Cantha faced the return of Shiro Tagachi.

The former Imperial bodyguard returned as a spirit and spread the supernatural Affliction across the Empire.

He also corrupted the dragon Kuunavang, who was later freed and joined the fight against him.

Imperial forces, Kurzicks, Luxons, and foreign allies eventually defeated Shiro before he could complete his return to mortal life.

The Affliction survived him.

In 1073 AE, Reiko Murakami began building support around the campaign to eliminate the remaining Afflicted. By 1077 AE, the Ministry of Purity had been formally established.

VI

Abaddon dies and Kormir ascends

In 1075 AE, followers of Abaddon attempted to bring Nightfall to Elona.

Under Varesh Ossa, the boundary between Tyria and the Realm of Torment weakened as demonic forces entered the physical world.

The conflict also led to the release of Palawa Joko, who had remained imprisoned since his defeat centuries earlier.

Abaddon was eventually confronted inside the Realm of Torment and killed.

His Divine power remained.

Kormir, former Spearmarshal of the Sunspears, absorbed that power and ascended as the Goddess of Truth.

The place Abaddon had once occupied among the Human Gods was filled again.

Commander Q // Observation

Abaddon's death showed that killing a God and destroying Divine power were not the same thing.

VII

The Destroyers rise beneath Tyria

In 1078 AE, earthquakes opened new passages into the world beneath Tyria.

The Destroyers were spreading through the Depths, devastating underground settlements and forcing many Asura toward the surface.

Humans, Asura, Norn, Dwarves, and Charr rebels became involved in the same expanding conflict.

To continue their ancient struggle against the Destroyers, King Jalis Ironhammer invoked the Rite of the Great Dwarf. The Dwarves transformed into beings of living stone bound to the war beneath Tyria.

The campaign culminated in the defeat of the Great Destroyer.

The victory revealed the greater threat behind it: the Elder Dragon Primordus.

Primordus had not yet fully awakened. The destruction of its champion delayed that awakening until 1120 AE.

Commander Q // Observation

The Great Destroyer was not the return of the Dragon Cycle. It was the first clear warning that the return had already begun.

VIII

Kryta and Cantha settle unfinished wars

The years after the major campaigns closed several conflicts that had remained unresolved.

In 1079 AE, the Krytan Civil War reached its decisive stage. Princess Salma, the Shining Blade, and their allies defeated the White Mantle's open rule at the Battle for Lion's Arch.

Salma became Queen of Kryta, establishing a royal line that would continue into later centuries.

In Cantha, the Ministry of Purity expanded its campaign beyond the Afflicted and increasingly targeted groups it considered threats to Canthan society.

Opposition formed against Reiko Murakami, who was eventually defeated. The Ministry survived under Ashu, and Cantha continued moving toward a more inward and nationalistic political order.

IX

Ebonhawke and the approaching Dragon Cycle

In 1080 AE, the Ebon Vanguard moved south and fortified Ebonhawke.

The stronghold became an important Human position as Charr control expanded through much of former Ascalon.

By then, the immediate crises of the Guild Wars Era had largely reached their conclusions.

The Flameseeker Prophecies had been fulfilled. Shiro had fallen. Abaddon was dead. Kormir had ascended. Kryta had broken White Mantle rule. The Great Destroyer had been defeated.

But the discovery beneath Tyria changed the scale of everything that followed.

Primordus was an Elder Dragon.

The ancient Dragonrises were no longer only records of a distant world.

Another cycle was beginning.

End of Volume VII

The Elder Dragons begin to wake

The wars of kingdoms and fallen Gods had dominated the previous decade.

The next age would be shaped by something older.
