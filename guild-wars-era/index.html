The Guild Wars Era

1013 AE – 1080 AE

The age began with humans fighting humans.

It ended with fallen kingdoms, a dead god, shattered faiths, transformed dwarves, and the first warning that something far older was waking beneath Tyria.

These are the events of the original Guild Wars.

Archive Context

The Third Guild War began in 1013 AE, but the major campaigns of the original Guild Wars games occur primarily between 1067 and 1080 AE. The opening records preserve the final decades of conflict so the historical road remains continuous.

Record 01 // The Third Guild War

The human kingdoms turn on one another

In 1013 AE, the Third Guild War began.

The conflict drew the human kingdoms of continental Tyria into another generation of warfare.

Ascalon and Kryta fought for power. Orr eventually entered the conflict as well.

The fighting weakened the kingdoms at exactly the wrong moment in history.

Record 02 // Saul D'Alessio

A new faith appears in Kryta

In 1067 AE, the exiled Saul D'Alessio encountered the mursaat.

He came to worship them as divine beings known as the Unseen Ones.

From this faith grew the White Mantle.

In 1069 AE, Saul brought the White Mantle to Janthir, strengthening the order's connection to the mursaat and the Eye of Janthir.

Record 03 // The Charr Invasion

A greater war ends the Guild Wars

In 1070 AE, charr armies descended upon Ascalon.

At the Great Northern Wall, they performed the Ritual of the Searing.

Searing Cauldrons summoned magical fire across the kingdom.

Cities burned. The countryside was devastated. The Great Northern Wall was breached.

The charr invasion abruptly ended the Third Guild War.

Turning Point

Ascalon, Kryta, and Orr had spent decades weakening one another. The charr invasion forced the kingdoms to abandon their war against each other and defend themselves.

Record 04 // Kryta

The Unseen Ones save a kingdom

Kryta lacked the strength to defeat the charr invasion on its own.

Saul D'Alessio returned with the White Mantle.

Backed by the mursaat, they defeated the invading charr.

King Jadon fled.

With the monarchy absent, the White Mantle eventually took control of Kryta's government.

The people initially remembered them as saviors.

Record 05 // Orr

A kingdom sinks beneath the sea

In 1071 AE, the charr invaded Orr while much of its army was still away from home.

They advanced deep into the kingdom but never reached Arah.

Vizier Khilbron invoked forbidden magic.

The resulting Cataclysm sank Orr beneath the sea.

Nearly everyone within the kingdom died. Khilbron survived as the Undead Lich.

Historical Uncertainty

Surviving accounts disagree on whether Khilbron intended to destroy Orr, acted desperately to stop the charr, or understood the full consequences of the magic he unleashed.

Record 06 // A Changing World

Other long conflicts finally close

The same year saw several major changes beyond continental Tyria.

After 191 years, the Tengu Wars in Cantha ended.

Formal trade between Cantha and the Tyrian kingdoms was restored through the Second Treaty of Lion's Arch.

The Bay of Sirens increasingly became known as the Sea of Sorrows.

Record 07 // Guild Wars Prophecies

Ascalon's survivors leave home

By 1072 AE, Ascalon's position had become desperate.

Prince Rurik believed the surviving population should flee west, rebuild, and someday return.

King Adelbern rejected the plan.

After Rin fell, Rurik led refugees across the Shiverpeaks toward Kryta.

He died protecting them during the crossing.

Record 08 // The Flameseeker Prophecies

Glint's centuries-old prophecy comes due

The Undead Lich sought the Scepter of Orr while events moved toward fulfillment of the Flameseeker Prophecies.

The White Mantle had been sacrificing Chosen humans to power soul batteries.

Resistance from the Shining Blade grew into open rebellion.

The prophecy was ultimately fulfilled.

The mursaat were nearly destroyed, White Mantle power was broken, and the Door of Komalie opened.

Record 09 // The Door of Komalie

The prophecy releases another threat

Opening the Door of Komalie released Titans into Tyria.

The Titans were the same type of beings that portions of charr society had once worshiped as gods.

The Undead Lich was defeated, and the Titan threat was eventually contained.

Record 10 // Guild Wars Factions

Shiro returns to Cantha

Across the ocean, another old catastrophe returned.

In 1072 AE, Shiro Tagachi returned after two centuries.

He unleashed the Affliction, a plague that transformed its victims into grotesque creatures.

The disease spread from Shing Jea through Kaineng City and into the Echovald Forest and Jade Sea.

Record 11 // Cantha Fights Back

Kuunavang joins the mortal resistance

The dragon Kuunavang was freed from Shiro's corruption and allied with mortal heroes.

Shiro was eventually defeated again.

His death ended the immediate crisis, but the Afflicted remained throughout Cantha.

Their presence would shape Canthan politics for years afterward.

Record 12 // Ministry of Purity

The fight against the Afflicted becomes political

In 1073 AE, Reiko Murakami began rallying Canthans against the Afflicted.

By 1077 AE, she formally established the Ministry of Purity.

What began as a movement against a plague would grow into a powerful political force.

Record 13 // Guild Wars Nightfall

Abaddon's thousand-year plan reaches its end

In 1075 AE, Warmarshal Varesh Ossa attempted to bring about Nightfall.

The barriers between Tyria and the Realm of Torment weakened.

Demons entered Elona, while the land itself began changing under Abaddon's influence.

During the campaign, Palawa Joko was freed from his ancient imprisonment.

Record 14 // A God Falls

Kormir inherits Abaddon's power

Abaddon was finally confronted within the Realm of Torment.

The fallen god was killed.

His divine power could not simply disappear.

Kormir absorbed the released energy and ascended into the pantheon as the Goddess of Truth and Knowledge.

More than a thousand years after the Exodus, Abaddon's rebellion had finally ended.

Record 15 // Charr Rebellion

The Flame Legion's religious order begins to fracture

In 1076 AE, Pyre Fierceshot led his warband in rebellion against the Flame Legion's shaman caste.

The Titans worshiped as charr gods had already been revealed as false.

Resistance to the shaman caste was becoming organized.

Record 16 // Eye of the North

The ground begins to move

In 1078 AE, earthquakes spread across Tyria.

The source was the Great Destroyer and its armies.

The crisis exposed forgotten underground passages and brought several peoples into much closer contact.

Asura

Driven to the Surface

Destroyer attacks forced asuran populations out of their underground civilization.

Norn

Southern Contact

The norn became increasingly involved with the peoples farther south.

Charr

Rebellion

Pyre Fierceshot's resistance developed into open conflict with the shaman caste.

Dwarves

Transformation

The dwarves underwent the Transformation to fight the Destroyers as living stone.

Record 17 // Beneath the Destroyers

A far older enemy is revealed

The Great Destroyer was defeated.

But it had not been the true source of the crisis.

Beneath Tyria, Primordus was beginning to stir.

The Elder Dragon did not fully awaken in 1078 AE.

The world had only been given a warning of what was coming.

Record 18 // Guild Wars Beyond

Kryta finally breaks the White Mantle

In 1079 AE, the Krytan civil war reached its conclusion.

Salma reclaimed Kryta.

The White Mantle's open rule ended.

The last known living Seer died during this period after providing the Shining Blade with the weapon that gave the order its name.

Events surrounding Gwen, Keiran Thackeray, and the Ebon Vanguard also unfolded during Hearts of the North.

Record 19 // Cantha

The Ministry of Purity moves against the Afflicted

In Cantha, the Ministry of Purity began the final stage of eliminating the Afflicted.

Their campaign brought increasing influence and authority to the Ministry.

The later events of Winds of Change continued into 1080 AE.

Ashu eventually assumed leadership of the Ministry of Purity.

Record 20 // End of an Era

The Ebon Vanguard falls back to Ebonhawke

In 1080 AE, the Ebon Vanguard withdrew from the north and fortified Ebonhawke.

The great campaigns of the Guild Wars era were coming to an end.

Tyria had survived the Searing, the Cataclysm, the Flameseeker Prophecies, Shiro, Nightfall, and the Destroyers.

But the warning from beneath the earth remained.

Primordus had stirred.

The age of the Elder Dragons was returning.

Continue the Road Through Time

The Dragons Awaken

The heroes of the Guild Wars era defeated a fallen god, ancient prophecies, undead armies, Titans, and the Great Destroyer.

They could not stop the larger cycle.

Over the next two and a half centuries, Elder Dragons would awaken one by one, entire peoples would be displaced, kingdoms would change, and Tyria would become the world encountered at the beginning of Guild Wars 2.
