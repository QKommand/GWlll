The Current Era

1338 AE – 1339 AE

Present Edge of the Archive

Tyria has survived gods, dragons, undead kings, wars between nations, invasions from the Mists, and threats older than human history.

Now the road through time bends back toward its beginning.

The latest journey leads to Castora, where ancient Seer history and forgotten magical knowledge are once again becoming part of the present.

Current Archive Status

This record covers the beginning of Visions of Eternity. The current archive chronology reaches 1339 AE.

Record 01 // Visions of Eternity

The Inquest travels to Castora

In 1338 AE, an Inquest expedition traveled to the island of Castora.

The expedition was commanded by Director Vloxx.

Their objective was the search for something known as Ancora. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Record 02 // A Distant Island

Castora becomes the center of the new mystery

Castora had existed on the edges of Tyrian knowledge for generations.

The expedition brought renewed attention to the island and the histories buried there.

The modern story was no longer simply exploring the consequences of the Dragon Cycle.

It was uncovering pieces of the world that had remained outside ordinary Tyrian history.

Record 03 // Ancient Seers

Seer history returns to the present

Ancient Seer ruins on Castora became central to the developing conflict.

The Seers had once been one of the great elder civilizations that resisted the Elder Dragons.

Their civilization was nearly destroyed during their ancient conflict with the mursaat.

Now remnants of that civilization were becoming relevant thousands of years later. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Historical Connection

The final previously known Seer died in 1079 AE. Castoran discoveries should therefore be understood as ancient remains, artifacts, memories, or previously unknown survivals unless later evidence establishes otherwise. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

Record 04 // Forgotten Knowledge

Ancient magic becomes relevant again

The Castora story begins uncovering Seer magical knowledge.

The archive specifically connects the discoveries to ancient magical technologies and ascension rituals. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

This creates a direct bridge between the oldest chapters of Tyrian history and its present day.

Record 05 // A Circle Through History

Tyria returns to its oldest questions

The Road Through Time began with Elder Dragons, the Seers, mursaat, Forgotten, dwarves, and jotun.

Thousands of years later, the modern story is once again centered on the ruins and magical knowledge of those ancient peoples.

History has not simply moved forward.

It has begun folding back upon itself.

Record 06 // Present Day

1339 AE

This is the current edge of the archive.

The traditional Elder Dragon Cycle has ended.

Aurene regulates the world's magical balance.

The Tyrian Alliance represents increasing cooperation between the world's nations.

Relations with the Mists have expanded far beyond what previous generations knew.

And on Castora, the mysteries of the Seers are becoming part of Tyria's living history again. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
