Tyria: Then to Now

Ancient Past – 1339 AE

Current Historical Record

You have reached the end of the Road Through Time.

Behind this point are thousands of years of surviving history: Dragonrises, forgotten civilizations, gods from the Mists, kingdoms, magical wars, migrations, catastrophes, and the eventual end of the Elder Dragon cycle.

This is the Tyria all of those events created.

Archive Context

Unlike the previous historical entries, this page does not represent one isolated era. It is the final overlook of the library's main timeline. Each record below summarizes an earlier chapter without repeating its full history. Open that archive when you want to follow the branch in detail.

The View From the Present

Tyria has never been one story

The oldest surviving histories describe civilizations trying to survive powers they barely understood.

Later ages shifted the center of history toward gods, humans, kingdoms, guilds, and nations.

Then the Elder Dragons returned and reminded the world that mortal politics existed inside a much older cycle.

The modern age finally broke that cycle.

What followed revealed something important: the dragons were never the limits of Tyria's history.

Present Day

1339 AE

The Elder Dragon cycle is over.

Aurene has stepped away from ordinary affairs to maintain the world's magical balance.

Cantha has reopened connections with the wider world. The Tyrian Alliance represents a growing attempt to bring nations together against dangers that do not respect borders.

The existence of the Astral Ward and Wizard's Court has changed how the Commander understands Tyria's place within the Mists.

Janthir has shown that histories once treated as ancient curiosities can still shape events thousands of years later.

And now the Inquest expedition toward Castora, seeking Ancora, carries the record into Visions of Eternity.
