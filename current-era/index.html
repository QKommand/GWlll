THE CASTORA EXPEDITION

Volume XI // The Current Era

The Current Era

On Castora, Ancora, Divitium Nava, and the return of Seer history to the present age.

Compiled and Annotated By

Commander Q

Captain Commander of the Vanguard Knights
Researcher of Tyrian History and the Mists

Author's Preface

The present reaches Castora

Janthir returned several ancient histories to active events. Mursaat ruins became places of investigation, Titans returned to Tyria, and modern forces followed their trail into the Realm of Torment.

Castora reaches farther back.

Here the Seers survive not only through the Bloodstone or the testimony of their enemies, but through places where they lived, worked, argued, performed rituals, and attempted to transform themselves through magic.

At the center of the current conflict stands Ancora, an ancient Seer staff connected to the rite known as Divitium Nava.

Director Vloxx came to Castora seeking that power. What the expedition uncovered was a larger piece of Seer history.

— Commander Q

I

The Inquest sail for Castora

In 1338 AE, Inquest forces under Director Vloxx sailed toward Castora.

Vloxx was searching for a specific Seer artifact: Ancora.

By the time the Commander and allies began their own voyage across the Unending Ocean, the Inquest already possessed the lead.

Reaching Castora presented its own difficulties. Dangerous seas and an unusual fog had helped keep the island isolated from the wider world.

The expedition nevertheless reached its shores.

What waited there was not an empty island containing one valuable relic.

Castora held the remains of an entire Seer world.

Commander Q // Observation

Vloxx went looking for a staff. He accidentally led the rest of Tyria into an archaeological crisis.

II

Castora preserves a Seer civilization

Castora had appeared in scattered records long before the modern expedition reached it.

Ancient Margonite sailing accounts preserved some of the earliest surviving references to the island, while later rumors and incomplete records kept its name alive without explaining what existed there.

The expedition found basilicas, temples, ritual grounds, vaults, statues, workshops, and magical defenses belonging to the Seers.

Among the most striking survivals were the Seneschals, magically altered Castoran creatures bound to protect the island and its secrets.

Other sites preserved writings, memorials, enchanted mechanisms, constructs, and evidence of dangerous magical experimentation.

Together they reveal something earlier records could only suggest.

The Seers were more than the creators of the Bloodstone. They possessed councils, ritual specialists, craftsmen, sacred places, internal laws, ambitions, disagreements, and formal traditions of magical transformation.

Historical Caution // Castora

The widespread presence of Seer ruins and artifacts does not establish that every magical feature of Castora was created by them. The island should be read from the evidence that survives rather than assigned wholesale to one civilization.

III

Isgarren returns to his own history

Castora placed Isgarren within a Seer society that had existed long before the modern Wizard's Court.

His relationship with that society had not ended peacefully.

Isgarren came into conflict with other Seers and was eventually brought before a council to determine his fate.

The council divided.

Sidony cast the deciding judgment that resulted in his banishment from Castora.

The punishment extended beyond ordinary exile. A curse tied Isgarren to the island's magical defenses, prevented him from safely wielding Ancora, and ensured that returning to Castora would expose him again to an ancient sentence.

Isgarren had not simply abandoned his people.

He had been cast out by them.

Commander Q // Observation

Castora makes Isgarren smaller in the best possible way. Here he is not the ancient wizard above Tyria. He is one Seer among other Seers, still carrying the consequences of a judgment made by his own people.

IV

Ancora and the Rite of Divinity

Ancora was not merely a powerful Seer staff.

It was connected to Divitium Nava, the ancient Rite of Divinity.

The ritual was performed at a particular source of power on Castora. Through Ancora and the magic of that place, chosen candidates could undergo a profound transformation.

Successful candidates emerged with extraordinary magical ability and longevity.

Failure could be fatal.

Castoran memorials and surviving records preserve individuals who died or were permanently harmed while attempting the rite.

Isgarren himself had undergone Divitium Nava in the ancient Seer era.

The later transformations used within the Wizard's Court drew upon knowledge connected to that tradition, but should not be treated as identical to the older rite.

Research Note // Ascension

Tyrian history uses "Ascension" for several different transformations. Divitium Nava should remain distinct from the Forgotten trials in the Crystal Desert and from the later process used to create members of the Wizard's Court.

V

Vloxx wanted the rite for himself

Director Vloxx had not come to Castora simply to add another relic to the Inquest's collection.

He intended to perform Divitium Nava.

Vloxx argued that Tyria had become too dependent upon powerful individuals and institutions deciding who should possess dangerous knowledge.

In his view, figures such as Isgarren and institutions such as the Tyrian Alliance risked concentrating authority in another small group of guardians.

He claimed the power he sought could prepare Tyria for dangers still ahead.

His methods made those claims difficult to accept without suspicion.

The Inquest stripped knowledge from Castoran ruins, disturbed ancient sites, and experimented with magical systems whose consequences were not fully understood.

The historical question surrounding Vloxx therefore became more complicated than whether he wanted power.

It became what he intended to do once he had it.

Historical Caution // Vloxx

Vloxx has stated beliefs concerning Tyria's future and the concentration of power, but his complete purpose remains unresolved. His explanations should be preserved as his stated motives rather than proof of what he ultimately intends.

VI

Vloxx completes Divitium Nava

In 1339 AE, Vloxx reached the Castoran ritual grounds with Ancora.

The Commander and allies attempted to stop him before the ancient process could be completed.

They were too late.

Vloxx completed Divitium Nava.

The rite transformed him and granted extraordinary magical power rooted directly in the ancient Seer tradition.

His relationship with the Inquest changed immediately.

When Kuda asserted control over the organization, Vloxx relinquished his position.

Kuda became Director of the Inquest.

Vloxx left Castora with the power he had crossed the ocean to obtain.

Commander Q // Observation

This is the moment the ruins stop being ruins. Ancient Seer knowledge has become present-day power in the hands of someone whose next move is still unknown.

VII

Sidony's judgment finally ends

Vloxx's ascension did not resolve the oldest conflict still active on Castora.

Sidony's curse continued to bind Isgarren to a punishment imposed by his own people ages earlier.

Breaking it required understanding the Seer magic that sustained it rather than simply attempting to overpower the enchantment.

The ancient All Seer construct remained connected to Castora's defenses. After its defeat, the curse could finally be confronted directly.

The surviving language of the enchantment was interpreted and altered through ritual.

The punishment laid upon Isgarren was broken.

The effects reached beyond him.

The fog surrounding Castora cleared, and ancient bindings associated with the island's guardians were brought to an end.

A judgment made within Seer society had survived the society that created it.

In 1339 AE, it finally ended.

VIII

The record remains open

The archive presently reaches 1339 AE.

Castora has moved from scattered rumor into explored geography.

Ancora has been recovered and used. Divitium Nava has been performed in the modern age.

Vloxx has ascended and relinquished control of the Inquest to Kuda.

Isgarren's exile has been placed within the history of his own people, while the curse imposed by Sidony has finally been broken.

Most importantly, Castora has changed the surviving picture of the Seers themselves.

They are no longer visible only through the Bloodstone, their war with the Mursaat, or isolated survivors.

Their ruins now preserve a society with laws, rituals, ambitions, failures, sacred places, magical specialists, political judgments, and competing ideas about what their people should become.

The remaining question is no longer what Vloxx was searching for.

He found it.

The question is what he intends to do next.

Current Archive Position

1339 AE // Record Ongoing

This volume remains open. Later events should be added as they become part of the historical record rather than treated as a separate completed age.

Commander Q // Observation

The Road Through Time began by trying to reconstruct the Seers from what they left behind. It has now reached a point where Seer history is creating new history in front of us.

Volume XI // Current Record

History is still being written

The Road Through Time began with civilizations facing an ancient Dragonrise and followed their histories through the Elder Races, the Gods, Human kingdoms, the Guild Wars, the modern Dragon Cycle, and the world that followed it.

Castora has brought that road back into direct contact with one of the peoples near its beginning.

There is no next completed volume yet.

The record has reached the present.
