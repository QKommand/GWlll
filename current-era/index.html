YOU ARE HERE // The Castora Expedition

Historical Archive // Record 11

The Current Era

1338 AE – 1339 AE

Current Archive

The fall of the Elder Dragons opened Tyria to histories that had remained hidden behind older wars and forgotten civilizations.

The road now reaches Castora, where the legacy of the Seers, ancient magical traditions, and Director Vloxx's ambitions have become part of the present conflict.

Archive Context

This volume follows the events of Visions of Eternity, beginning with the expedition to Castora in 1338 AE and continuing into 1339 AE.

New story chapters can be added to this volume in chronological order as the Castora conflict develops.

Record 01 // Visions of Eternity

Director Vloxx leads an expedition to Castora

In 1338 AE, an Inquest expedition led by Director Vloxx traveled toward the distant island of Castora.

Vloxx was searching for an ancient artifact known as Ancora, drawing the Tyrian Alliance into pursuit.

The expedition brought modern Tyria into a region that had remained largely outside the known historical record.

Record 02 // Castora

A distant island reveals an ancient past

Castora proved to be saturated with powerful magical energy and filled with ruins belonging to the Seers.

Ancient defenses, ritual structures, and magical systems remained active long after the civilization that created them had faded.

The island offered something rare: direct evidence of Seer society rather than scattered remnants preserved through other civilizations.

Record 03 // Ancient Seers

Seer civilization comes back into focus

Earlier records preserved the Seers through their role in the ancient Dragonrise, their creation of the Bloodstone, and their war with the Mursaat.

Castora revealed more of the civilization itself, including its rituals, magical practices, defenses, and internal traditions.

A people once known mainly through the artifacts they left behind were becoming easier to understand on their own terms.

Archive Connection

The Seers first appear near the beginning of the Road Through Time. Castora returns the archive to that ancient history without repeating the full story of the Dragonrise.

Record 04 // Isgarren

Isgarren's history reaches back to the Seers

The Castoran discoveries connected Isgarren directly to the ancient Seer civilization.

Before becoming the Wizard known to the modern Astral Ward, Isgarren had once belonged to the Seers.

His transformation was associated with an ancient ceremony called the Rite of Divinity, performed using the artifact known as Ancora.

Record 05 // Ancora

The purpose of Vloxx's search becomes clear

Ancora was more than a surviving Seer artifact.

The staff was tied to the Rite of Divinity, a magical transformation capable of elevating those who underwent it.

Its defenses showed that the Seers understood how dangerous such knowledge could become in the wrong hands.

Director Vloxx intended to use that knowledge for himself.

Record 06 // The Pursuit

The Alliance and Inquest race through the ruins

The search for Ancora carried both sides deeper into Castora's surviving Seer structures.

Each ruin revealed more about the civilization that had built it, while Vloxx demonstrated enough understanding of Seer traditions to remain ahead of the Alliance.

Exploration and conflict became inseparable. Whoever understood the ruins first gained the advantage.

Record 07 // Ancora Lost

Vloxx escapes with the Seer artifact

Although the Commander reached Ancora, Director Vloxx and the Inquest ultimately escaped with it.

The conflict then moved deeper into Castora and toward Eternity's Garden.

What had begun as a search for an ancient artifact became a struggle over what the Seers' knowledge could be used to accomplish.

Record 08 // Vloxx's Ambition

Vloxx seeks the power of the Seers

Vloxx confirmed that he intended to use Ancora in a form of the Rite of Divinity.

His objective extended beyond acquiring another powerful artifact. He sought transformation through the same ancient traditions connected to Isgarren.

Vloxx argued that Tyria had repeatedly depended on exceptional individuals and powerful institutions to determine its future.

His solution was to change who had access to that kind of power.

Historical Caution

Vloxx's complete objective remains only partly understood. His stated ambitions should not be treated as proof of what his final plan will ultimately accomplish.

Record 09 // 1339 AE

The Castora conflict enters a new year

By 1339 AE, the pursuit of Vloxx and Ancora had moved beyond the expedition's original search.

Vloxx's transformation, Isgarren's ancient history, and the purpose behind the Rite of Divinity had become central to the developing conflict.

The events surrounding So It Is Written mark the current chronological point of this volume.

Current Position

1339 AE

The Castora expedition has brought ancient Seer history directly into modern Tyrian affairs.

Director Vloxx now possesses knowledge and power connected to the Rite of Divinity, while the consequences of his larger plan remain unresolved.

Future records in this volume will continue from this point.
