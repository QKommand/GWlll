Tyria: Then to Now

Ancient Tyria – 1339 AE

Current Historical Record

You have reached the present.

Behind this point lies more than ten thousand years of surviving history: civilizations swallowed by Dragonrises, gods arriving from the Mists, human kingdoms rising and falling, wars over magic, and generations spent trying to survive the Elder Dragons.

This is the world all of those stories created.

Reading This Archive

This page is the final overlook on the Road Through Time. Instead of focusing on a single event, it looks back across the major ages of Tyria and shows how each one leads into the next. Select any era below to return to its full archive.

The Big Picture

Tyria has always been changing

The oldest surviving history is not about humans or kingdoms. It is about survival.

Ancient civilizations lived beneath recurring Dragonrises. Some were destroyed. Some hid. Some sacrificed their magic. Others vanished into the Mists.

Humanity arrived much later and built civilizations over a world whose oldest ruins they did not fully understand. Gods, magic, guilds, kingdoms, and empires became the center of recorded history.

Then the Elder Dragons returned.

The modern age became the first known period in which the peoples of Tyria did more than simply survive the Dragon Cycle. They ended it.

Patterns Across History

The same questions keep returning

Magic and Power

From the Seers' Bloodstone to Abaddon's gift, dragon magic, ley lines, and modern artifacts, magic repeatedly changes who holds power and what they are willing to do with it.

Survival and Adaptation

Elder races hid from dragons. Humans built kingdoms. Norn migrated south. Asura fled the depths. Modern Tyria survives because its peoples continually adapt.

History Never Fully Disappears

The mursaat, Titans, Bloodstones, Scepter of Orr, Janthir, and ancient dragon knowledge repeatedly return to influence events centuries or millennia later.

Cooperation Changes the Outcome

Earlier civilizations often fractured under pressure. The modern era increasingly survives through alliances, shared knowledge, and cooperation across nations and races.

Where Tyria Stands Now

1339 AE

The ancient Elder Dragon Cycle is over.

Aurene has taken her place as a conduit for the world's magical balance. Cantha has reopened to the wider world. The Tyrian Alliance has begun creating stronger ties between nations and peoples that spent much of history divided.

The Wizard's Court and Astral Ward have revealed that Tyria has been connected to conflicts in the Mists for far longer than most of its inhabitants knew.

Janthir has reopened some of the oldest threads in recorded history, linking the present back to the mursaat, Seers, Titans, and ancient magic.

And now the Inquest expedition toward Castora and the search for Ancora carries the historical record into Visions of Eternity.
