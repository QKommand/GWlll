Author's Preface

There is no ending to write yet

Every previous volume in this archive has had one advantage that this record does not. I knew how the story ended.

The Seers could be followed from the ancient Dragonrise to the Bloodstone. The Human Gods could be followed from their arrival to the Exodus. The Guild Wars became history, and even the Elder Dragon Cycle eventually reached its conclusion.

Castora offers no such distance. The expedition there belongs to the present, and discoveries are arriving faster than historians can comfortably place them. Ancient names that once occupied only a few surviving records now have ruins, rituals, artifacts, and consequences attached to them.

For that reason, this volume must remain different from those before it. I can record what has been discovered. I can connect those discoveries to the older archive. I cannot pretend that an unfinished event already has a conclusion.

History is still happening.

— Commander Q

I

Director Vloxx searches for Castora

In 1338 AE, the Inquest launched an expedition toward Castora, a distant island continent long known through sailors' stories but regarded by many Tyrians as inaccessible, or perhaps not real at all.

The expedition was led by Director Vloxx, the latest leader of the Inquest. His forces were not traveling to Castora simply to chart an unexplored shore.

Vloxx was searching for an ancient artifact known as Ancora.

The Tyrian Alliance pursued the expedition, carrying the Commander and several allies into a region that had remained outside ordinary Tyrian politics for generations.

The journey immediately proved difficult. Castora's waters, magical defenses, and natural hazards damaged the Inquest fleet before its members could establish themselves securely on the island.

II

An island thought to be legend becomes geography

Castora proved to be far more than an isolated island. Its landscape was saturated with Ley Energy, and ancient magic had altered the creatures and environments that developed there.

Modern visitors were not the first civilization to recognize the island's importance. Ruins scattered throughout Castora pointed toward occupation and construction by the Seers, one of the Elder Races whose history reached back to the previous Dragonrise.

The island was protected by magical systems designed to survive long after their creators. Enchanted guardians, arcane barriers, and powerful creatures bound into service continued enforcing instructions left behind thousands of years earlier.

Castora therefore presented a rare historical problem. It was not merely a ruined civilization whose remains could be excavated. Parts of the civilization's defensive infrastructure were still functioning.

Commander Q // Observation

A ruin usually tells us what a civilization left behind. Castora is stranger. Some of what the Seers left behind is still following orders.

III

The Seers become more than fragments in other histories

Earlier volumes encountered the Seers mainly through the things they left behind. They created the original Bloodstone, fought the Mursaat, developed defenses against Spectral Agony, and were eventually reduced to only a handful of survivors.

Castora changed the nature of that evidence. Seer ruins, writings, magical systems, and ritual structures preserved information about the civilization itself rather than merely the role it played in someone else's story.

These discoveries showed a society with its own hierarchy, traditions, disagreements, and understanding of magical transformation.

The Seers were no longer simply the people who created the Bloodstone before disappearing from history. Castora was beginning to show how they lived and what they believed.

Archive Connection

The Road Through Time first encountered the Seers near the beginning of the archive, during the ancient Dragonrise. Castora brings their civilization back into focus thousands of years later without requiring the earlier history to be repeated here.

IV

Isgarren was once one of the Seers

The Castoran discoveries also changed the historical understanding of Isgarren, the ancient Wizard whose influence had shaped the Wizard's Court long before the Commander discovered it.

Isgarren had once belonged to the Seers.

His history reached back to the civilization whose ruins now covered parts of Castora, making his extraordinary age and magical knowledge part of a much older story than the modern Astral Ward.

Among the traditions preserved by the Seers was a transformative ceremony known as the Rite of Divinity.

Isgarren had undergone that rite in the ancient past. The process used the artifact known as Ancora.

The same object Vloxx had crossed the ocean to find was therefore connected directly to the origin of one of the most powerful Wizards known to modern Tyria.

V

Ancora was built for transformation

Ancora was not simply a powerful Seer weapon. It was a ritual staff tied to the Rite of Divinity, a process capable of elevating those who underwent it beyond their previous mortal condition.

The Seers themselves understood that such power could not be allowed to fall casually into other hands. Ancient accounts associated with the artifact show that its preservation was treated as a dangerous necessity rather than an ordinary act of safekeeping.

The artifact was concealed behind defenses involving Seer magic, traps, guardians, and Shadowstone.

Director Vloxx sought Ancora because he intended to use the Seers' knowledge of ascension for himself.

The mystery was no longer what the Inquest had come to Castora to find.

The question became what Vloxx intended to do once he possessed it.

VI

The Alliance and Inquest race through Seer history

The pursuit of Ancora carried both the Alliance and the Inquest deeper into Castora's surviving Seer structures. Exploration became inseparable from the conflict because each new discovery revealed another part of the route toward the artifact.

The Commander and allies reached Seer ritual sites, studied the defenses surrounding them, and learned more about the culture responsible for building Castora's ancient magical network.

Director Vloxx remained ahead often enough to make those discoveries dangerous. He was not looting ruins at random. His actions showed that he understood enough of the Seers' traditions to identify the pieces needed for the Rite of Divinity.

The expedition had therefore become a race between two forms of historical research.

One side wanted to understand the past before using it. The other believed understanding was valuable mainly because it created the possibility of action.

VII

Ancora falls into Inquest hands

The Commander eventually reached Ancora, but possession of the artifact was brief. Vloxx and his forces intervened and escaped with the Seer staff.

From that point onward, the problem changed again. Castora no longer held the artifact everyone was seeking.

Vloxx did.

His ability to move through Castora's surviving magical defenses also demonstrated that the Inquest expedition had learned far more about the island than its damaged arrival initially suggested.

The Alliance continued the pursuit deeper into Eternity's Garden, where the surviving Seer legacy and Vloxx's ambitions increasingly overlapped.

VIII

Vloxx intends to become something greater

Vloxx eventually confirmed what the search for Ancora had implied. He intended to perform a form of the Seer Rite of Divinity and elevate himself through its power.

His stated ambitions reached farther than personal immortality or another Inquest attempt to collect magical weapons.

Vloxx argued that Tyria had repeatedly placed its future into the hands of exceptional beings, institutions, rulers, Wizards, Dragons, and organizations capable of deciding what ordinary people were allowed to become.

He presented his own plans as an attempt to return control of Tyria's future to its people and create another generation capable of accomplishing what individuals such as the Commander had achieved.

Whether his solution would actually accomplish that remained far less certain than his confidence in it.

Commander Q // Observation

I have learned to be suspicious whenever someone announces that they intend to give everyone else freedom after first making themselves powerful enough to decide what freedom should mean.

IX

The problem is no longer only Ancora

As the pursuit continued, Vloxx's language suggested that the Rite of Divinity was not the final purpose of his plan.

Ascension would provide him with power, but power was a means rather than an ending.

Vloxx increasingly described Tyria's repeated failures as symptoms of something deeper. Dragons had been defeated. Gods had been challenged. Tyrants had fallen, only for new institutions, conflicts, and concentrations of power to emerge afterward.

His conclusion was that repairing individual crises would never be enough if the underlying structure that allowed those crises to recur remained unchanged.

Whatever Vloxx intends to alter, his objective appears to have moved beyond changing a government or defeating another enemy.

He believes there is something wrong at the foundation.

Historical Caution

Vloxx's complete objective has not yet been revealed. This archive therefore records the direction of his stated intentions without treating speculation about his final plan as established history.

X

The record enters 1339 AE

By 1339 AE, the pursuit of Director Vloxx and Ancora had carried the story beyond the events first recorded during the Castora expedition.

The historical record has now reached the events surrounding So It Is Written, with Vloxx's transformation, the Seer legacy, and his intended changes to Tyria still unresolved.

Isgarren's own ancient history has become increasingly important to understanding what the Rite of Divinity can accomplish and what Vloxx may now be capable of.

For the first time in this archive, the chapter being written is not safely behind us.

It is the present.

XI // Present Record

This is where the archive currently ends

The year is 1339 AE.

The traditional Elder Dragon Cycle is over, while Aurene remains responsible for helping maintain Tyria's magical balance.

The Tyrian Alliance has created a political framework for cooperation that earlier generations could scarcely have imagined.

The Astral Ward and Wizard's Court have brought the politics of the Mists into ordinary Tyrian awareness.

The history of the Mursaat and Seers, once confined largely to ancient ruins and incomplete testimony, has become relevant to current events again.

Director Vloxx possesses knowledge and power derived from Seer traditions, while the consequences of his larger plan remain unknown.

The Road Through Time began with the Seers trying to preserve a world dominated by Elder Dragons.

Thousands of years later, the road has returned to them.

Not because history repeated itself exactly, but because something they understood about magic, transformation, and the structure of the world was never completely buried.

Commander Q // Present Entry

There is a temptation at the edge of an archive to invent a conclusion simply because every book feels cleaner with one. I would rather leave the last page unfinished. When the next event becomes history, there will be room to write it properly.

Living Archive // Open Record

History is still being written

Unlike the earlier volumes, this archive does not conclude with the end of an era.

It ends at the point where documented history meets events that are still unfolding.

Future discoveries concerning Castora, Ancora, Director Vloxx, Isgarren, the Seers, and the wider consequences of the current crisis can be added here without rewriting the historical road that came before.
