The Current Era

Visions of Eternity // 1338 AE – 1339 AE

Current Historical Record

The road that began beneath the shadow of ancient dragons has reached the present.

The Titan crisis is over. The Elder Dragon cycle is history. Tyria now looks beyond the borders that once defined its world, toward places that remain largely unknown.

One of those roads leads to Castora.

Living Archive

Unlike earlier sections of the Road Through Time, this record describes the current era of Guild Wars 2. The history here is still being written. This archive can expand as new events become part of Tyria's established historical record.

Record 01 // After Janthir

Another old threat is closed away

The renewed Titan threat had reached back into some of Tyria's oldest conflicts.

In 1338 AE, the Titans were defeated and the portal to the Foundry of Failed Creations was closed.

The crisis ended another thread stretching all the way back to Abaddon and the events surrounding the original Guild Wars.

Record 02 // An Artifact Disappears

The Scepter of Orr enters the Mists

The Scepter of Orr was lost to the Mists.

Few artifacts have touched as many layers of Tyrian history.

Orr, the mursaat, ancient magic, the Flameseeker Prophecies, and later conflicts all became part of its story.

Its disappearance left another historical thread unresolved.

Open Thread

The Scepter has not simply been destroyed. Its trail now extends into the Mists.

Record 03 // Visions of Eternity

Attention turns toward Castora

A new expedition begins.

This time, the force driving it is not the Pact, the Wizard's Court, or one of Tyria's great nations.

It is the Inquest.

Their destination is Castora.

Record 04 // Director Vloxx

The Inquest takes the lead

The expedition to Castora is led by Director Vloxx.

The Inquest has long pursued magical power, experimental technologies, lost civilizations, and knowledge regardless of the consequences.

Now that curiosity carries them toward another largely unexplored chapter of the world.

Record 05 // Ancora

The expedition has a target

The Inquest expedition travels to Castora in search of Ancora.

Within the current record gathered by this library, this search marks the central mystery leading Tyria into the events of Visions of Eternity.

The road has moved far beyond the struggles that once defined the world.

There is no Elder Dragon at the end of this trail.

Record 06 // A Different Kind of Frontier

Tyria is no longer looking inward

Much of Tyrian history was shaped by survival.

Humans fought for kingdoms. Elder races fought extinction. Nations fought one another. Generations fought the Elder Dragons.

The modern world now finds itself in a different position.

The greatest known cycle of destruction has ended, cooperation between nations has expanded, and paths through the Mists have become increasingly important.

Exploration itself is becoming one of the forces shaping history.

Record 07 // Present Day

History catches up with the reader

The calendar has reached 1339 AE.

Everything before this point can be approached as history.

From the disappearance of the Giganticus Lupicus, through the Elder Races, the arrival of humanity, the Exodus, the Guild Wars, the Dragonrise, the Commander, and the mysteries of Janthir, every road has eventually led here.

Beyond this point, the archive is no longer looking backward.

Record 08 // An Open Page

The story is still happening

The Guild Wars Living Lore Library ends its main historical road here for now.

Not because Tyria's history has ended.

Because this is the point where history becomes the present.
