The Guild Wars Living Lore Library organizes the major history of Tyria into connected volumes so that each era can be read on its own or followed as one continuous road through time.

Guild Wars Living Lore Library is an unofficial fan lore project. Guild Wars, Guild Wars 2, ArenaNet, NCSOFT, and related properties belong to their respective owners.

Archive Notice

This library contains major story spoilers.

The Guild Wars Living Lore Library follows the complete historical record across Guild Wars, Guild Wars 2, expansions, Living World releases, and the current story.

Major characters, deaths, locations, revelations, historical mysteries, and story outcomes are discussed openly.
