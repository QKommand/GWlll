Historical Archive // Record 08

The Dragons Awaken

1078 AE – 1320 AE

For thousands of years, the Elder Dragons had existed mostly as ruins, legends, and warnings from peoples whose civilizations had already faded.

Then the earth shook.

Over the next two centuries, the ancient cycle returned and reshaped nearly every corner of Tyria.

Archive Context

This era bridges Guild Wars and Guild Wars 2. The world encountered in Guild Wars 2 exists because these awakenings displaced entire peoples, destroyed cities, changed coastlines, and forced old enemies into new relationships.

Record 01 // The First Warning

Primordus begins to stir

During the events surrounding the Great Destroyer in 1078 AE, the Elder Dragon Primordus began stirring beneath Tyria.

The Great Destroyer was defeated before Primordus could fully awaken.

The victory bought Tyria time.

It did not stop the Dragonrise.

Record 02 // The Foefire

Ascalon's last king refuses to surrender

In 1090 AE, the charr High Legions finally broke into Ascalon City.

King Adelbern unleashed the Foefire.

The spell killed the remaining human defenders and transformed many of them into ghosts bound to Ascalon.

The charr reclaimed the city.

But the war left a supernatural scar across the land.

Record 03 // A New Order

Institutions rise from the old world

In 1105 AE, the Durmand Priory was established in the Shiverpeaks.

New Krytan became a central written language.

In 1112 AE, the charr constructed the Black Citadel over the ruins of Rin.

In 1116 AE, Kalla Scorchrazor led the rebellion that broke the Flame Legion shaman caste's control over charr society.

Record 04 // Primordus

The first Elder Dragon fully awakens

In 1120 AE, Primordus awakened.

Destroyers spread throughout the Depths of Tyria.

Underground civilizations were forced toward the surface.

The asura lost their great subterranean cities, and both asura and skritt began appearing throughout the surface world.

The transformed dwarves continued their war against Primordus beneath the earth.

Record 05 // The Last Dwarves

An ancient civilization leaves the surface world

By 1125 AE, the last known dwarves had undergone the transformation into stone.

The dwarves continued fighting Destroyers in the depths, but their old kingdoms and ordinary society disappeared.

One of the elder races that had survived the previous Dragonrise had now transformed itself to face the next one.

The Cycle Returns

The same ancient race that remembered earlier Dragonrises became one of the first civilizations consumed by the new cycle.

Record 06 // Distant Nations Change

Tyria begins losing contact with the wider world

In 1127 AE, Emperor Usoku began campaigns to fully unify Cantha under imperial rule.

Kurzick and Luxon independence was crushed, while non-humans and political enemies were purged.

In 1135 AE, Palawa Joko began his successful conquest of Elona.

He redirected the River Elon and expanded his Awakened forces.

Record 07 // Jormag

Winter consumes the north

In 1165 AE, Jormag, the Elder Dragon of Ice, awakened.

A massive snowstorm continued for four years.

Jormag's Icebrood expanded across the Far Shiverpeaks while several Spirits of the Wild disappeared.

Norn and kodan settlements were devastated.

Record 08 // The Norn Exodus

Hoelbrak becomes a new home

After years of losses against Jormag's forces, the norn abandoned the Far Shiverpeaks.

They migrated south into the Shiverpeak Mountains.

In 1169 AE, they established Hoelbrak.

What later appeared to outsiders as the traditional norn homeland was actually a refuge created by the awakening of an Elder Dragon.

Record 09 // The Fall of Elona

Palawa Joko finishes what he began centuries earlier

In 1175 AE, Elona fell to Palawa Joko.

The Order of the Sunspears was largely destroyed.

Joko's Awakened regime took control of the nation and would remain in power for generations.

Elona effectively disappeared from the political world known to Central Tyria.

Record 10 // A Quiet Legacy

Ventari leaves behind an idea

In 1180 AE, the centaur Ventari died beside the Pale Tree.

He left behind the Ventari Tablet, containing principles of peace and coexistence.

At the time, this seemed like a small event compared with the Dragonrise.

More than a century later, an entirely new people would build much of their culture around those teachings.

Record 11 // Zhaitan

Orr rises from the sea

In 1219 AE, Zhaitan awakened beneath the sunken kingdom of Orr.

The entire peninsula rose from the depths of the Sea of Sorrows.

The resulting tsunami destroyed old Lion's Arch and devastated coastlines across the region.

Zhaitan's Risen began spreading from Orr across nearby waters and settlements.

Ancient Echo

Thousands of years earlier, the elder races had attempted to destroy Zhaitan and failed. Now the same dragon had returned beneath the ruins of one of humanity's greatest kingdoms.

Record 12 // The Ocean Changes

The dragon's awakening reaches Cantha

Zhaitan's Great Tsunami crossed the ocean and destroyed Old Kaineng.

Kuunavang called upon Soo-Won to leave the depths and defend Cantha.

Around this period, tengu fought their way through Risen forces and established the Dominion of Winds.

Record 13 // Rebuilding

Survivors build around the disasters

In 1220 AE, Divinity's Reach was founded in Kryta.

Over the following decade, pirates and corsairs occupied the drying ruins of old Lion's Arch.

Cobiah Marriner eventually helped rebuild the settlement as a free city.

The modern political map of Central Tyria was beginning to emerge.

Record 14 // Displacement

Dragonrise becomes migration

By 1270 AE, danger in the deep ocean had driven peoples such as the quaggan and krait toward the shores of Tyria.

Refugees from conquered Elona also settled in Divinity's Reach.

The Dragonrise was no longer something happening in distant lands.

It was changing who lived where, who became neighbors, and which peoples were forced into conflict.

Record 15 // A New People

The sylvari appear

In 1302 AE, the first sylvari awakened from the Pale Tree.

They emerged into a world already shaped by Elder Dragon awakenings, ancient wars, and refugee migrations.

Guided by the Pale Tree and the teachings of Ventari's Tablet, the sylvari quickly became one of Central Tyria's major peoples.

Record 16 // Destiny's Edge

Champions begin fighting the dragons directly

In 1319 AE, Destiny's Edge formed.

The group defeated several powerful dragon champions, including Jormag's Dragonspawn.

For a brief time, they represented the possibility that small groups of exceptional fighters could strike directly at the Elder Dragon threat.

Record 17 // Kralkatorrik

The Crystal Dragon crosses Tyria

In 1320 AE, Kralkatorrik awakened.

The Elder Dragon flew south across Ascalon toward the Crystal Desert.

Everything caught beneath its path was transformed by its corruption, creating the enormous scar known as the Dragonbrand.

Destiny's Edge attempted to confront Kralkatorrik with the help of Glint.

Glint was killed.

Record 18 // The Eve of Guild Wars 2

Tyria enters the modern Dragonrise

By 1320 AE, multiple Elder Dragons were awake.

Primordus ruled the depths. Jormag held much of the north. Zhaitan commanded Orr. Kralkatorrik had carved a corrupted path across the continent.

Nations had survived largely by adapting around the dragons, not defeating them.

Five years later, a new generation of heroes would begin changing that equation.
