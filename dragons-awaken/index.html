YOU ARE HERE // The Old World Begins to Change

Historical Archive // Record 08

The Dragons Awaken

1080 AE – 1325 AE

The Guild Wars era ended with Primordus stirring beneath Tyria.

Over the next two and a half centuries, that warning became a new age.

Elder Dragons awakened, civilizations were displaced, old kingdoms disappeared, new cities rose, and the world slowly became the Tyria encountered at the beginning of Guild Wars 2.

Archive Context

Eye of the North already revealed the Destroyers and Primordus beginning to stir. This archive begins after that warning and follows the transition from the old Guild Wars world into the political, cultural, and geographical landscape of 1325 AE.

Record 01 // After the Guild Wars

The old conflicts continue

The great campaigns of the Guild Wars era had ended, but many mortal conflicts remained unresolved.

In 1080 AE, the Ebon Vanguard withdrew from the north and fortified Ebonhawke.

The fortress became one of humanity's last major footholds in Ascalon.

Record 02 // The Foefire

Human Ascalon ends in fire and ghosts

In 1090 AE, the charr finally took Ascalon City.

Facing defeat, King Adelbern unleashed the Foefire.

The spell killed many of the remaining humans and transformed them into spectral defenders bound to Ascalon.

Human rule over most of Ascalon was effectively over.

Record 03 // A Changing Civilization

New powers replace the old world

In 1105 AE, the Durmand Priory was established in the Shiverpeaks.

New Krytan became a widely used written language.

In 1112 AE, the charr constructed the Black Citadel over the ruins of Rin.

By 1116 AE, Kalla Scorchrazor was leading the rebellion that would help break the Flame Legion's control over charr society.

Record 04 // Primordus

The warning beneath Tyria becomes an awakening

Primordus had stirred during the events of Eye of the North.

By 1120 AE, the Elder Dragon's activity had transformed the underground world.

Destroyers overran subterranean settlements.

The great underground cities of the asura fell, forcing asura and skritt populations toward the surface.

Timeline Note

Primordus stirring in 1078 AE and the major awakening period beginning around 1120 AE are separate points in the same developing Dragonrise.

Record 05 // The Dwarves

An ancient civilization leaves the surface

The transformation begun during the Destroyer crisis eventually reached the remaining dwarves.

By 1125 AE, the last known dwarves had become stone.

They continued fighting Primordus and the Destroyers below Tyria, but dwarven civilization largely disappeared from the surface.

Record 06 // Cantha

The Empire turns inward

In 1127 AE, Emperor Usoku began his campaign against the Luxons and Kurzicks.

Cantha was forcibly reunified.

Political enemies and non-human populations were increasingly targeted as imperial control strengthened.

The empire became progressively isolated from the wider world.

Record 07 // Joko's Return

Elona falls to the Scourge of Vabbi

In 1135 AE, Palawa Joko began a renewed invasion of Elona.

He redirected the River Elon, dramatically changing the landscape and controlling access to water.

By 1175 AE, Elona had fallen under Joko's rule.

The Sunspears were hunted down, destroyed, converted, or forced into hiding.

Record 08 // Jormag

The north freezes

In 1165 AE, Jormag awakened in the Far Shiverpeaks.

A massive snowstorm lasted for years.

Norn settlements were overwhelmed and several Spirits of the Wild disappeared.

By 1169 AE, surviving norn had migrated south and established Hoelbrak.

Record 09 // New Generations

Tyria changes between the disasters

Not every transformation of this age came from destruction.

The Pale Tree continued growing on the Tarnished Coast.

In 1302 AE, the first sylvari awakened.

The Secondborn followed in 1304 AE.

A completely new people had entered the historical record.

Record 10 // Zhaitan

Orr rises from the sea

In 1219 AE, Zhaitan awakened beneath Orr.

The sunken peninsula rose from the Sea of Sorrows.

The event caused enormous waves that destroyed old Lion's Arch.

Zhaitan's Risen spread through nearby waters and coastlines, making travel increasingly dangerous.

Record 11 // Across the Ocean

Zhaitan's awakening reaches Cantha

The tsunami caused by Orr's rising crossed the ocean.

Old Kaineng was devastated.

Maritime routes became more dangerous as dragon activity spread through the seas.

Political isolation and physical danger increasingly separated the continents.

Record 12 // Rebuilding

Civilization adapts to the new world

In 1220 AE, Divinity's Reach was founded in Kryta.

Pirates and sailors later occupied the drying ruins around old Lion's Arch.

Cobiah Marriner helped transform the settlement into a new multiracial city.

The modern Lion's Arch began taking shape.

Record 13 // Displacement

Entire peoples are forced into new homes

Dangers in the Unending Ocean pushed quaggans and krait toward the shores of Central Tyria.

Refugees fleeing Joko's Elona settled in Kryta, including Divinity's Reach.

Modern Tyria was increasingly becoming a world shaped by migration.

Record 14 // A New Generation

The modern world begins to take shape

In Cantha, Yu Joon began experimenting with dragonjade around 1310 AE.

Those experiments became the foundation of later jade technology.

Meanwhile, heroes from several races were beginning to cooperate against Elder Dragon champions.

Record 15 // Destiny's Edge

Champions from different peoples fight together

In 1319 AE, Destiny's Edge formed.

The group defeated several powerful dragon champions, including Jormag's Dragonspawn.

Their victories suggested that Elder Dragon forces could be confronted directly rather than merely endured.

Record 16 // Kralkatorrik

The Crystal Dragon crosses Ascalon

In 1320 AE, Kralkatorrik awakened.

Its flight south carved the Dragonbrand across Ascalon toward the Crystal Desert.

Creatures and landscapes caught within the Brand were transformed into crystalline servants.

Record 17 // The Breaking of Destiny's Edge

Glint dies fighting her former master

Destiny's Edge confronted Kralkatorrik with the help of Glint.

The plan failed.

Glint was killed.

Destiny's Edge fractured in the aftermath.

Tyria lost one of its greatest potential weapons against the Elder Dragons.

Record 18 // The Last Quiet Years

Tyria prepares without knowing what comes next

The years after Glint's death did not bring another immediate Elder Dragon victory.

Instead, the peoples of Tyria continued adapting to a world already surrounded by dragon threats.

Lion's Arch expanded its role as a multiracial center, while travel and infrastructure increasingly connected the major peoples of Central Tyria.

Between 1320 and 1325 AE, five asura gates were established at Fort Marriner, creating fixed access points connected to travel into the Mists.

Record 19 // Human and Charr Relations

Centuries of war begin bending toward diplomacy

The conflict between humans and charr had defined Ascalon for generations.

By the years immediately before 1325 AE, cooperation against common threats had created opportunities for diplomacy.

The retrieval of the Claw of the Khan-Ur helped support peace negotiations between Kryta and the charr High Legions.

One of Tyria's oldest mortal wars was beginning to cool just as a much larger war approached.

Record 20 // The Edge of the Commander Era

The world reaches 1325 AE

By 1325 AE, Tyria had become a world fundamentally different from the one left behind by Guild Wars.

The charr controlled most of Ascalon. Humans had consolidated around Divinity's Reach. The norn lived around Hoelbrak. The asura had rebuilt on the surface. The sylvari were entering the world for the first time.

Lion's Arch stood as a multiracial city connecting them.

Around them, Elder Dragons and their minions threatened nearly every region.

The events of the Guild Wars 2 Personal Story now begin.

The next age belongs to the Commander.
