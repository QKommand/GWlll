YOU ARE HERE // The Old World Begins to Change

Historical Archive // Record 08

The Dragons Awaken

1080 AE – 1320 AE

The heroes of the Guild Wars era had defeated gods, liches, Titans, and ancient prophecies.

None of those victories stopped what was waking beneath them.

Over the next two and a half centuries, the Elder Dragons returned one by one and remade the map of Tyria.

Archive Context

Eye of the North already revealed the Destroyers and Primordus beginning to stir. This archive starts after that warning. Its focus is the long transformation between Guild Wars and Guild Wars 2: migrations, destroyed civilizations, new cities, and the full return of the Elder Dragon cycle.

Record 01 // A World in Transition

The old conflicts have not disappeared yet

The years immediately after the Guild Wars era were still shaped by unresolved mortal conflicts.

In Ascalon, humans and charr continued fighting.

The Ebon Vanguard withdrew south and fortified Ebonhawke.

In 1090 AE, the charr finally captured Ascalon City.

Record 02 // The Foefire

Ascalon survives as ghosts

Facing defeat, King Adelbern unleashed the Foefire.

The spell killed the remaining humans within Ascalon City and transformed many of them into spectral defenders.

Human rule over Ascalon had effectively ended.

The charr reclaimed the region, but inherited a land haunted by the war that had consumed it.

Record 03 // The Charr Rebuild

A new charr civilization rises

In 1112 AE, the charr erected the Black Citadel over the ruins of Rin.

Four years later, Kalla Scorchrazor led a rebellion against the Flame Legion's shaman caste.

The rebellion helped break the religious control that had dominated charr society.

The modern High Legions began taking shape.

Record 04 // Primordus

The first Elder Dragon fully awakens

In 1120 AE, Primordus awakened beneath Tyria.

Destroyers spread through the underground world.

The great subterranean cities of the asura fell.

Asura and skritt populations were forced toward the surface.

The dragon's movements also increased volcanic activity around Mount Maelstrom and surrounding regions.

Record 05 // The Dwarves

The last dwarves become stone

The transformation begun during the Destroyer crisis eventually reached the remaining dwarves.

By 1125 AE, the last known dwarves had become stone.

Their people continued fighting Primordus below the surface, but dwarven civilization largely disappeared from the world above.

Record 06 // Emperor Usoku

Cantha is forcibly unified

In 1127 AE, Emperor Usoku began a campaign to bring the Luxons and Kurzicks fully under imperial control.

The empire was reunified through force.

Political enemies and non-human populations were increasingly targeted as Cantha turned inward.

Contact with the wider world became more difficult.

Record 07 // Joko's Elona

The Scourge of Vabbi returns

In 1135 AE, Palawa Joko began another campaign against Elona.

He redirected the River Elon, transforming the landscape of the Crystal Desert and cutting regions off from water.

By 1175 AE, Elona had fallen under his rule.

The Sunspears were hunted down or forced into hiding.

Record 08 // Jormag

The north freezes

In 1165 AE, Jormag awakened in the Far Shiverpeaks.

A massive blizzard lasted for years.

Norn settlements were overwhelmed, and several Spirits of the Wild disappeared during the conflict.

By 1169 AE, surviving norn had migrated south and established Hoelbrak.

Record 09 // New Generations

The world keeps growing between disasters

Not every major change came from destruction.

The Pale Tree continued growing along the Tarnished Coast.

In 1302 AE, the first sylvari awakened.

Two years later, the Secondborn followed.

A completely new people had entered Tyrian history.

Record 10 // Zhaitan

Orr rises from the sea

In 1219 AE, Zhaitan awakened beneath Orr.

The sunken peninsula rose from the Sea of Sorrows.

The event caused enormous waves that destroyed old Lion's Arch.

Zhaitan's Risen spread across nearby waters and coastlines, turning travel through the region into a constant danger.

Record 11 // The Great Tsunami

Zhaitan's awakening reaches across the ocean

The tsunami caused by Orr's rising did not stop at Central Tyria.

It reached Cantha and destroyed Old Kaineng.

Maritime routes became increasingly dangerous.

Combined with political isolation and Dragon activity, contact between continents continued to weaken.

Record 12 // Rebuilding

Survivors build around the new world

In 1220 AE, Divinity's Reach was founded in Kryta.

A decade later, pirates and sailors began settling the ruins around old Lion's Arch.

Cobiah Marriner helped rebuild the city into the multiracial Lion's Arch known in the modern age.

Record 13 // Displacement

Dragon activity moves entire peoples

Threats in the Unending Ocean forced quaggans and krait toward Central Tyria.

Refugees from Joko's Elona settled in Divinity's Reach.

The modern population of Central Tyria was increasingly shaped by peoples who had been pushed from their original homes.

The World of Guild Wars 2

Many races encountered throughout Guild Wars 2 are living where they do because the Dragonrise displaced them.

Record 14 // Destiny's Edge

A new generation begins fighting back

In 1319 AE, Destiny's Edge formed.

The group defeated several powerful dragon champions, including Jormag's champion known as the Dragonspawn.

For the first time in generations, a small group of heroes demonstrated that Elder Dragon forces could be challenged directly.

Record 15 // Kralkatorrik

The Crystal Dragon crosses Ascalon

In 1320 AE, Kralkatorrik awakened.

Its flight south carved the Dragonbrand across Ascalon and toward the Crystal Desert.

Living creatures and landscapes caught within the Brand were transformed into crystalline servants.

Record 16 // The Breaking of Destiny's Edge

Glint dies fighting her former master

Destiny's Edge confronted Kralkatorrik with the help of Glint.

The battle failed.

Glint was killed.

Destiny's Edge fractured in the aftermath.

Two years later, Tyria would still be living under the shadow of multiple awakened Elder Dragons.

Then a new generation of heroes would enter the story.
