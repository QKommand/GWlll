Historical Archive // Record 10

Beyond the Dragon Cycle

1336 AE – 1338 AE

For tens of thousands of years, the Elder Dragons had defined the rhythm of Tyria's history.

Their cycle was over.

What came next was not peace, but discovery. Old barriers weakened, hidden powers stepped into view, and Tyria learned that the dragons had never been the only danger beyond the edges of the known world.

Archive Context

This era begins after the death of Soo-Won and the end of the ancient Elder Dragon cycle. The focus shifts away from dragons and toward the Mists, the Wizard's Court, Kryptis society, ancient secrets around Janthir, and threats that had remained hidden while Tyria was occupied with the Dragon Cycle.

Record 01 // After Soo-Won

Aurene takes responsibility for the balance

With Soo-Won gone, the ancient Elder Dragon cycle had ended.

Aurene remained as the great magical stabilizing force left behind.

In 1336 AE, she withdrew from ordinary affairs and took her place as a conduit for the world's magical balance.

Tyria entered an age where mortals could no longer measure every great threat against the Elder Dragons.

Record 02 // Secrets of the Obscure

The world above Tyria reveals itself

A hidden struggle involving the Wizard's Court and the Astral Ward became impossible to ignore.

For centuries, these groups had defended Tyria from threats beyond the ordinary awareness of its nations.

Their work centered on the Wizard's Tower, the skies above Tyria, and dangers moving through the Mists.

Record 03 // The Kryptis Invasion

Creatures from the Mists cross into Tyria

Kryptis forces managed to breach into Tyria from the Mists.

The invasion destabilized the Wizard's Court and allowed Kryptis forces to take control of important locations, including parts of Amnytas and the Wizard's Tower.

Tyria was suddenly facing an enemy whose homeland did not lie across an ocean or beneath the earth.

It lay in another realm.

Record 04 // Mabon

An ancient guardian is lost

During the Kryptis crisis, Mabon died.

His death weakened an organization already struggling against a threat that had breached defenses built over centuries.

It also marked the loss of another figure tied to ancient histories that much of Tyria had never known existed.

Record 05 // Janthir Stirs

Old protections begin to fail

As the conflict unfolded, the wards around Bava Nisos weakened.

The ancient mursaat city lay in Janthir, a region already tied to the mursaat, Seers, Titans, Bloodstones, and some of Tyria's oldest unresolved mysteries.

Ancient Connection

Janthir reconnects the modern story to the same elder civilizations introduced near the beginning of the Road Through Time.

Record 06 // Peitha

The Kryptis are not united

The Kryptis threat was not a simple invasion by a single unified people.

Peitha allied with the Wizard's Court and began organizing resistance against Eparch.

The conflict became a rebellion within Kryptis society itself.

The road forward would lead into their homeland: Inner Nayos.

Record 07 // Inner Nayos

The war crosses fully into another realm

In 1337 AE, Peitha's rebellion pushed deeper into Kryptis territory.

The alliance breached Eparch's defenses and carried the conflict into the structure of Kryptis society itself.

Rather than simply defending Tyria, the Commander and allies were now helping determine who would rule another realm.

Record 08 // The Fall of Eparch

The Midnight King rises

Peitha and her allies eventually defeated Eparch.

With his fall, the old rule over Nayos ended.

Peitha became the Midnight King.

The immediate Kryptis crisis was resolved, but Tyria had now established direct relationships with powers and societies beyond its own world.

Record 09 // Janthir Wilds

Tyria begins acting as a wider alliance

In 1337 AE, the Tyrian Alliance was formed.

The great Dragon campaigns had already forced nations and peoples to cooperate repeatedly.

Now that cooperation began taking a more permanent political form.

The world was moving from emergency coalitions toward a broader international structure.

Record 10 // The Titans

An enemy from the Guild Wars era returns

The Titans resurfaced.

These creatures had once served Abaddon and had poured through the Door of Komalie during the events surrounding the Flameseeker Prophecies.

Their return tied the modern age directly back to the conflicts of the original Guild Wars era.

Once again, history that seemed finished had found a way back into the present.

Record 11 // The Foundry Connection

The Titan threat is cut off

In 1338 AE, the Titans were defeated.

The portal leading to the Foundry of Failed Creations was closed.

A threat linked to Abaddon, the Mists, and events more than two centuries earlier was once again contained.

Record 12 // The Scepter of Orr

An ancient artifact disappears into the Mists

During the events surrounding the Titan crisis, the Scepter of Orr was lost to the Mists.

The artifact had already passed through some of the most important conflicts in Tyrian history.

Now its trail extended beyond the physical world.

Unresolved Thread

The Scepter's disappearance leaves one of Tyria's ancient magical artifacts connected directly to whatever may come next.

Record 13 // The New Frontier

The map has become larger than Tyria

The Elder Dragons had once made the world feel impossibly large.

Now the scale had changed.

Tyria knew of hidden magical orders above its skies, societies within the Mists, ancient powers in Janthir, and pathways connecting realms that had once been treated as legends.

The Dragon Cycle was over.

The age of the unknown had begun.
