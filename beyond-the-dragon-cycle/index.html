YOU ARE HERE // The Dragon Cycle Is Over

Historical Archive // Record 10

Beyond the Dragon Cycle

1336 AE – 1338 AE

For thousands of years, the Elder Dragons were the great rhythm beneath Tyrian history.

By 1335 AE, that rhythm had ended.

What came next was not peace so much as a wider view of the world. With the Dragon Cycle gone, hidden powers, distant realms, and forgotten histories could finally move into the foreground.

Archive Context

This archive covers the period following End of Dragons: Aurene's departure from ordinary affairs, Secrets of the Obscure, the Kryptis conflict, the rise of Peitha, the formation of the Tyrian Alliance, Janthir, and the renewed Titan threat. Earlier Elder Dragon history is not repeated here.

Record 01 // A Different World

Tyria enters an age without the old cycle

The death of Soo-Won brought the ancient Elder Dragon cycle to an end.

Aurene remained as the great conduit through which the world's magical balance could be maintained.

Yet her role was not to rule Tyria.

The peoples of the world were increasingly left to decide their own future.

Record 02 // Aurene's Departure

The Elder Dragon of Crystal and Light steps away

In 1336 AE, Aurene departed from ordinary Tyrian affairs.

Her purpose shifted toward serving as a conduit for the magical balance of the world.

The Commander and the nations of Tyria could no longer rely upon an Elder Dragon to stand at the center of every new crisis.

Record 03 // Secrets of the Obscure

Another layer of Tyria steps into view

The Commander encountered an organization unknown to most of the world: the Astral Ward.

The Ward had spent centuries defending Tyria against threats that ordinary nations rarely knew existed.

Their history was connected to the Wizard's Court and the mysterious Wizard's Tower.

A Change in Perspective

The Commander had spent years fighting dangers everyone could see. The Astral Ward revealed how many threats had been kept outside ordinary history entirely.

Record 04 // The Kryptis

Invaders cross from the Mists

Creatures known as the Kryptis breached Tyria from the Mists.

Their invasion struck the Wizard's Tower and Amnytas.

The Wizard's Court lost control of important positions, forcing the Astral Ward into open conflict.

Tyria was now facing an enemy whose homeland lay beyond the physical world.

Record 05 // Mabon

One of the Wizard's Court is lost

During the Kryptis invasion, Mabon died.

His death weakened an already strained defense against the creatures pressing through the Mists.

At the same time, old protections around Bava Nisos began to weaken.

Ancient history and the new crisis were starting to overlap.

Record 06 // Peitha

Not every Kryptis serves the same master

The Kryptis were not a single united enemy.

Peitha opposed the rule of Eparch.

She allied with the Wizard's Court and the Commander's forces, turning the conflict from a defense of Tyria into a rebellion within Kryptis society itself.

Record 07 // Inner Nayos

The conflict crosses completely into another realm

Peitha's rebellion carried the war into Inner Nayos.

Instead of waiting for the Kryptis to breach Tyria again, the alliance moved against Eparch within his own domain.

The Commander was no longer simply defending a continent.

Tyrian conflicts had become directly entangled with civilizations of the Mists.

Record 08 // The Midnight King

Peitha takes the throne of Nayos

In 1337 AE, Peitha's rebellion breached Eparch's defenses.

Eparch was defeated.

Peitha became the Midnight King of Nayos.

The Kryptis crisis ended not through destruction of their people, but through a change in who ruled them.

Record 09 // The Tyrian Alliance

Tyria begins organizing for a wider world

In 1337 AE, the Tyrian Alliance was formed.

The alliance reflected how much the political landscape had changed.

Nations that once acted primarily through their own borders were increasingly facing problems that required permanent cooperation.

The future of Tyria was becoming more interconnected.

Record 10 // Janthir Wilds

The road turns toward an old mystery

Attention shifted toward Janthir.

The region carried connections to some of the oldest threads in Tyrian history, including the mursaat and Seers.

What had once seemed like distant ancient history was now becoming part of the present again.

Record 11 // An Ancient Threat Returns

The Titans reappear

Titans resurfaced during the events surrounding Janthir.

These beings had once been tied to some of the great crises of the original Guild Wars era.

Their return showed how easily unresolved pieces of the past could survive long enough to become modern threats.

The Road Loops Back

The Dragon Cycle may have ended, but ancient history did not. The post-dragon era increasingly uncovers problems whose roots reach back hundreds or thousands of years.

Record 12 // The Foundry

The Titans are defeated

In 1338 AE, the renewed Titan threat was defeated.

The portal to the Foundry of Failed Creations was closed.

Another ancient danger had reached the modern world and been contained.

Record 13 // An Open Thread

The Scepter of Orr disappears into the Mists

During the closing events of this era, the Scepter of Orr was lost to the Mists.

The artifact had already passed through several major chapters of Tyrian history.

Its disappearance leaves another road open between the past and whatever comes next.

By 1338 AE, Tyria had moved beyond the Elder Dragon cycle.

It had not moved beyond its history.
