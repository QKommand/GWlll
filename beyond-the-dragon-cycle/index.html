Historical Archive // Record 10

Beyond the Dragon Cycle

1336 AE – 1338 AE

For thousands of years, Elder Dragons stood behind the greatest changes in Tyria.

Then the cycle ended.

With the dragons no longer defining every crisis, another layer of history came into view: hidden wizards, civilizations of the Mists, ancient secrets, and dangers that had existed outside ordinary Tyrian awareness.

Archive Context

This record covers Secrets of the Obscure and Janthir Wilds. It begins after the end of the traditional Elder Dragon cycle in 1335 AE and ends in 1338 AE, immediately before the events of Visions of Eternity.

Record 01 // The Age of Aurene

Aurene steps away from mortal affairs

With the Dragon Cycle ended, Aurene took on a different role.

She withdrew from ordinary Tyrian affairs to help regulate the world's magic.

Tyria had entered an age in which its nations would increasingly have to face new threats without an Elder Dragon standing beside them.

Record 02 // Hidden Defenders

The Commander discovers a secret war

The Commander encountered the Astral Ward, an organization largely unknown to the ordinary nations of Tyria.

The Ward was connected to the Wizard's Court and the mysterious Wizard's Tower.

Their existence revealed that powerful defenders had been confronting threats beyond ordinary mortal awareness for centuries.

Older Than the Modern Crisis

The PDF places the formal founding of the Astral Ward in 604 AE under Warden Taryn, while the Wizard's Court itself reaches even farther into ancient history.

Record 03 // Secrets of the Obscure

The Kryptis cross from the Mists

Creatures known as the Kryptis breached Tyria from the Mists.

Their assault compromised both the Wizard's Tower and Amnytas.

The conflict was fundamentally different from the Dragon campaigns.

Tyria was now confronting a civilization from another realm.

Record 04 // Mabon

The Wizard's Court loses one of its own

During the Kryptis crisis, Mabon died.

His death struck the Wizard's Court while its defenses were already under pressure.

The conflict also began pulling long-hidden pieces of Tyrian history into the open.

Record 05 // Peitha

The Kryptis are not one enemy

The invasion revealed that Kryptis society was itself divided.

Peitha opposed Eparch and began organizing a rebellion against his rule.

She allied with the forces defending Tyria.

The war changed from simply repelling an invasion to supporting a revolution inside Nayos.

Record 06 // Nayos

The war crosses fully into the Mists

Peitha's rebellion carried the conflict into Inner Nayos.

Instead of waiting for another breach, the alliance took the war into Eparch's own realm.

This marked a major shift in the scale of the Commander's conflicts.

Tyria was no longer only interacting with the Mists through portals, spirits, or isolated expeditions.

It was becoming directly involved in the politics of another world.

Record 07 // Midnight King

Peitha defeats Eparch

In 1337 AE, Peitha's rebellion broke through Eparch's defenses.

Eparch was defeated.

Peitha became the Midnight King of Nayos.

The Kryptis crisis ended through a change in their leadership rather than the destruction of their civilization.

Record 08 // Janthir Wilds

The Tyrian Alliance forms

In 1337 AE, the Tyrian Alliance was formed.

The new organization reflected how much the political world had changed since the Pact's creation.

Cooperation was becoming less of a temporary response to disaster and more of a permanent feature of Tyrian politics.

Record 09 // The Isles of Janthir

Modern Tyria returns to ancient ground

The story shifted toward Janthir, a region already woven deeply into older Tyrian history.

The mursaat had long-standing connections to the area.

Saul D'Alessio had brought the White Mantle to Janthir centuries earlier.

Now the modern story was returning to mysteries connected to the mursaat, Seers, and some of Tyria's oldest magical conflicts.

Record 10 // An Old Enemy

The Titans return

Titans resurfaced during the events in Janthir.

These creatures were not new to Tyria.

They had already played major roles in the original Guild Wars era, including the events surrounding the Flameseeker Prophecies.

Their return connected the present directly back to conflicts more than two centuries old.

Record 11 // Mursaat and Seers

The oldest races return to the center of history

Janthir brought ancient mursaat and Seer history back into the modern story.

These were not newly arrived peoples.

Their conflict reached back to the ancient Dragonrise thousands of years before humanity became dominant in Tyria.

The modern story was beginning to loop back toward the beginning of the Road Through Time.

Record 12 // The Foundry

The Titan crisis ends

In 1338 AE, the renewed Titan threat was defeated.

The portal to the Foundry of Failed Creations was closed.

Another ancient enemy had reached the modern age and been pushed back.

Record 13 // The Scepter of Orr

An ancient artifact disappears into the Mists

The Scepter of Orr was lost to the Mists.

The artifact had already passed through some of the most important events of Tyrian history.

Its disappearance left another unresolved connection between the ancient world and the present.

The Dragon Cycle was over.

But the oldest parts of Tyria's history were becoming relevant again.
