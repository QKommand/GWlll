Historical Archive // Record 05

The Exodus of the Gods

0 AE

The crisis over magic had already changed Tyria. The Bloodstone was divided. Mortal power was restrained. The wars that followed Abaddon's gift were beginning to end.

But among the gods themselves, the conflict had only begun.

Archive Context

The Exodus marks one of the great dividing lines in Tyrian history. The Mouvelian Calendar uses this event as its center: years before it are recorded as BE, Before the Exodus, and years after it as AE, After the Exodus.

Record 01 // The Dispute

Abaddon refuses the new order

Abaddon had given greater magical power to the peoples of Tyria.

When the other gods chose to limit that gift using the Bloodstone, Abaddon opposed them.

The disagreement was no longer about mortals alone.

It became a conflict among gods.

Record 02 // The Margonites

A civilization follows Abaddon

The Margonites were already one of the great maritime peoples of the age.

Their settlements stretched along the coastlines north and west of Elona, and their ships once dominated the Unending Ocean.

During Abaddon's rebellion, he began transforming his followers into powerful ethereal and demonic beings.

One of the earliest known was Jadoth.

Collapse

The divine war would ultimately destroy Margonite civilization as it had previously existed.

Record 03 // The Divine War

Abaddon turns against the gods

Abaddon openly declared war on the other members of the pantheon.

His forces assaulted the Gates of Heaven.

Abaddon was powerful enough to fight multiple gods, but he could not defeat the combined strength of the other five.

The fighting spilled across the world and left permanent scars upon Tyria.

Record 04 // The Crystal Sea

A sea becomes a desert

The battle that defeated Abaddon caused catastrophic changes across southern Tyria.

The floor of the Crystal Sea rose.

Waters vanished, exposing vast stretches of land that became the Crystal Desert.

Farther south, once-fertile regions were transformed into the sulfurous wasteland later known as the Desolation.

World Changed

The Crystal Desert was not always a desert. Its landscape is one of the physical scars left behind by the war between gods.

Record 05 // The Fall of Abaddon

The god of knowledge is cast down

Abaddon was eventually defeated by the other five gods.

He and his followers were imprisoned within the Realm of Torment.

The Mouth of Torment became the place where the fallen god's realm touched the world.

His defeat did not destroy him.

It only imprisoned him.

Record 06 // The Forgotten God

History is deliberately erased

After Abaddon's defeat, the remaining gods attempted to remove knowledge of him from Tyria.

Records were destroyed.

His Orrian temple was sunk, and the Temple of the Six was pulled into the Realm of Torment with him.

Later generations would remember the pantheon as though Abaddon had never belonged to it.

Historical Irony

Abaddon became a forgotten god not because no one had once known him, but because powerful beings deliberately tried to erase him from history.

Record 07 // The Exodus

The gods leave Tyria

The divine war had demonstrated the damage that could occur when gods fought directly within the mortal world.

After Abaddon's imprisonment, the remaining Five Gods withdrew from Tyria and departed into the Mists.

Legends later claimed that they went to create new worlds.

Whatever their destination, the age of gods openly walking alongside mortals was over.

Record 08 // Year Zero

History gains a dividing line

The Exodus became important enough that Tyrian historians used it to divide their calendar.

Events before the gods' departure became BE, Before the Exodus.

Events after it became AE, After the Exodus.

The old divine age had ended.

The age of mortal kingdoms continued without the gods standing beside them.

Record 09 // The Prisoner Still Watches

The rebellion was not truly over

Abaddon remained trapped within the Realm of Torment.

But imprisonment did not end his influence.

Over the centuries that followed, his servants and agents would influence events throughout Tyria.

The Searing of Ascalon, the Cataclysm of Orr, Shiro Tagachi's fall, and eventually Nightfall would all become connected to the fallen god's efforts to return.

His war had ended.

His plan had not.
