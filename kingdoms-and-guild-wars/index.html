Historical Archive // Record 06

Kingdoms and Guild Wars

1 AE – 1069 AE

The gods had left the world, but the civilizations they helped shape remained behind.

Human kingdoms expanded. Trade crossed oceans. Dynasties rose and collapsed. Guilds became powerful enough to challenge kings. And by the end of the age, the nations of continental Tyria were exhausting themselves in war.

Archive Context

This era covers more than a thousand years. Rather than treating every event as equal, this archive follows the major political changes that created the world encountered at the beginning of the original Guild Wars campaigns.

Record 01 // After the Exodus

Mortals inherit the world

The departure of the gods did not end human civilization.

Instead, human nations entered an age where their kingdoms, rulers, guilds, and institutions would increasingly determine the course of history.

In 1 AE, Prince Chang Hai became the first Ascendant emperor of Cantha.

In 2 AE, Orr became an independent nation.

Record 02 // Expanding Kingdoms

Humanity spreads across the known world

Human civilizations continued expanding throughout Tyria, Elona, and Cantha.

The Primeval Kings extended their influence into Vabbi and the Desolation.

Cantha developed deeper trade relationships with Tyria and Elona.

The Forgotten withdrew from the human world and settled within the Crystal Desert, where they became caretakers of Glint and guardians of Ascension.

Record 03 // The Volcano Erupts

The Bloodstones return to the world

Roughly a century after the Exodus, Abaddon's Mouth erupted.

The five major Bloodstones were thrown from the volcano and scattered across Tyria.

Magical energy seeped into the surrounding lands.

The stones that had once been created to limit magical power became objects of desire.

Historical Connection

The struggle over the Bloodstones helped give rise to the conflicts later remembered as the Guild Wars.

Record 04 // The Rise of the Guilds

Power moves beyond the throne

Guilds had originally grown as influential organizations within human society.

Over time, they became powerful military and political forces.

In some regions, guild armies became strong enough that kings could no longer fully control them.

Their competing interests eventually turned into open conflict.

The first of the three Guild Wars began during this broad period.

Record 05 // A Connected World

Trade grows while borders harden

In 221 AE, Cantha formally expanded trade with Tyria, Elona, and northern lands.

In 300 AE, Kryta became an official colony of Elona.

Human expansion also intensified conflicts with older peoples, including the centaurs whose lands were increasingly occupied.

In 358 AE, Kryta gained independence and became a kingdom in its own right.

Record 06 // Elona in Crisis

Plague ends a royal line

In 452 AE, the Scarab Plague swept through Elona.

Istan was abandoned and the royal family of the Primeval Kings was wiped out.

The Great Dynasty eventually replaced them, only to collapse centuries later.

The Pretender Wars and Shattered Dynasty Era followed.

By 640 AE, Elona had emerged as three allied provinces: Istan, Kourna, and Vabbi.

Record 07 // Cantha Changes

Emperors rise, and Shiro changes everything

Cantha passed through generations of emperors, internal conflicts, and changing political eras.

In 872 AE, the imperial bodyguard Shiro Tagachi assassinated Emperor Angsiyan during the Harvest Ceremony.

Shiro was killed by Saint Viktor, Archemorus, and Vizu.

His death released the Jade Wind, petrifying the Echovald Forest and turning the Jade Sea solid.

A Land Frozen in a Moment

Cantha would live with the physical consequences of Shiro's death for centuries.

Record 08 // Lord Odran

A mortal opens the Rift

In 851 AE, the arcanist Lord Odran became the first recorded living mortal to enter the Rift.

Through sacrifices and powerful rituals, he opened permanent portals into the Mists.

After his death, the protections hiding those portals failed, giving mortals greater access to places such as the Hall of Heroes.

Record 09 // The Scourge of Vabbi

Palawa Joko rises from the Desolation

In 860 AE, the undead lich Palawa Joko invaded Elona.

His Awakened army conquered much of Vabbi before facing resistance led by Turai Ossa.

In 862 AE, Turai defeated Joko in single combat during the Battle of Jahai.

Joko could not be killed, so he was imprisoned.

The Order of Whispers was formed to ensure that imprisonment endured.

Record 10 // Walls and Isolation

The kingdoms prepare for threats they can see

In 898 AE, the Great Northern Wall was constructed in Ascalon.

Cantha increasingly withdrew from foreign contact.

Long-standing trade agreements with the Tyrian kingdoms were dissolved in 902 AE, and Canthan embassies were later closed.

The known world was becoming less connected just as larger dangers were beginning to gather.

Record 11 // The Third Guild War

Human kingdoms turn on one another again

In 1013 AE, the Third Guild War began.

Ascalon, Kryta, and Orr became consumed by another struggle between powerful guilds and national interests.

Unlike earlier wars, Orr eventually became directly involved.

The conflict was costly enough to weaken the three kingdoms at exactly the wrong moment.

Record 12 // The Unseen Ones

A forgotten people enters human politics

In 1067 AE, the Krytan exile Saul D'Alessio encountered the mursaat.

Believing them to be divine beings, he founded the White Mantle and taught followers to worship the mursaat as the Unseen Ones.

Within only a few years, this new faith would become one of the most powerful political forces in Kryta.

Record 13 // The Edge of Disaster

The kingdoms do not see what is coming

By 1069 AE, the human nations of continental Tyria remained locked in conflict.

Ascalon had fortified itself against northern threats. Kryta was increasingly influenced by the White Mantle. Orr remained powerful but would soon be dragged directly into war.

The Third Guild War continued.

Then the charr moved south.

The age of kingdoms fighting one another was about to end because all three would face something worse.
