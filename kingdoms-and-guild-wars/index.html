Historical Archive // Record 06

Kingdoms and Guild Wars

2 AE – 1013 AE

The gods had left.

The world did not become quiet.

Kings, guilds, dynasties, traders, prophets, and conquerors now shaped the age.

Over the next thousand years, mortal civilizations would build kingdoms, cross continents, reach into the Mists, and create conflicts powerful enough to reshape history.

Archive Context

This is a long transitional era rather than a single war. After the Exodus, human kingdoms expanded and international trade flourished. Guilds grew from economic organizations into political and military powers capable of operating across national borders.

When the Bloodstones were scattered across Tyria, competition for their power helped ignite the conflicts that became known as the Guild Wars.

Record 01 // Orr

Orr becomes an independent nation

In 2 AE, Orr became an independent human nation.

The kingdom possessed a particularly strong relationship with magic and remained closely associated with Arah, the former city of the gods.

Orr would become one of the three great human kingdoms of continental Tyria alongside Ascalon and Kryta.

Record 02 // The Ring of Fire

The Bloodstones return to the world

Roughly a century after the Exodus, the volcano known as Abaddon's Mouth erupted.

The Bloodstones hidden within the Ring of Fire were scattered across Tyria.

Their magical influence spread into the lands surrounding them.

Mortals soon realized the enormous power associated with the stones.

Competition for that power would help ignite a new kind of conflict.

Important Boundary

The Bloodstones were placed within Abaddon's Mouth before the Exodus. Their later scattering was caused by the volcano's eruption roughly a century afterward.

Record 03 // Rise of the Guilds

Organizations begin rivaling kings

The centuries after the Exodus brought prosperity, invention, and expanding trade.

Guilds originally developed largely as economic organizations.

Merchants used them to exchange goods, establish prices, and build networks that crossed political borders.

But the idea spread.

Military, religious, magical, and social guilds appeared.

Some operated across several kingdoms at the same time.

Eventually military guilds became powerful enough to enforce laws and influence governments.

Kings still ruled. But they no longer held all the power.

Record 04 // The Forgotten

Human expansion changes an ancient world

Long before humanity dominated continental Tyria, the Forgotten had inhabited large portions of the world.

As human populations expanded, the Forgotten gradually withdrew toward the Crystal Desert.

There they became closely associated with Glint and the trials surrounding Ascension.

Their retreat was part of a larger transformation: humanity was becoming one of the dominant civilizations of the known world.

Record 05 // International Trade

The continents become more connected

In 221 AE, Cantha entered the Age of the Hedgehog.

Formal trade expanded between Cantha, Tyria, and Elona.

Goods, people, ideas, and political influence increasingly crossed continental borders.

The known human world was becoming more interconnected.

Record 06 // Glint

Glint records a prophecy for a distant future

In 272 AE, Glint foresaw the events remembered as the Flameseeker Prophecies.

The prophecies concerned future catastrophes that would threaten humanity and transform Tyria.

Their meaning would not become fully apparent for centuries.

Eventually they would become central to the fall of the Mursaat, the opening of the Door of Komalie, and the events of the first Guild Wars campaign.

Record 07 // Kryta

A colony becomes a kingdom

In 300 AE, Kryta was established as a colony of Elona.

Human expansion through the region intensified conflict with local centaur populations.

In 358 AE, Kryta gained its independence.

It joined Ascalon and Orr as one of the major human powers of continental Tyria.

Record 08 // Elona

The Primeval Dynasty falls

In 452 AE, the Scarab Plague began spreading through Elona.

The plague devastated Istan, killed enormous numbers of people, and wiped out the royal family of the Primeval Kings.

Istan was abandoned.

By 456 AE, the plague years had ended, but the political order they destroyed could not simply be restored.

Record 09 // Shattered Dynasties

Elona survives political collapse

The fall of the Primeval Kings was followed by new rulers and eventually further political conflict.

The Pretender Wars helped usher in a period remembered as the Shattered Dynasty Era.

In time, Elona developed into the three provinces familiar in later history: Istan, Kourna, and Vabbi.

The old unified dynastic order had given way to a more divided political landscape.

Record 10 // Palawa Joko

An undead conqueror challenges Elona

By 757 AE, the undead lord Palawa Joko had established his Bone Palace within the Desolation.

In 860 AE, Joko invaded Elona and captured much of the Northern Province, earning the title Scourge of Vabbi.

For two years, his forces threatened the Elonian provinces.

In 862 AE, the Kournan general Turai Ossa defeated Joko at the Battle of Jahai.

The Order of Whispers, originating from Turai's intelligence network, helped seal the defeated lich away and guarded his prison.

In 868 AE, Turai led the Great Pilgrimage into the Crystal Desert seeking Ascension.

Record 11 // Lord Odran

A mortal forces open the way into the Mists

In 851 AE, the powerful arcanist Lord Odran used his own magic and the sacrifice of many souls to open a portal into the Mists.

He eventually reached the Rift and traveled through the Hall of Heroes.

Over time, Odran created hidden portals linking the Rift to other worlds.

When the spirits of the Hall eventually killed him, the enchantments hiding those portals failed.

Routes once known only to Odran became accessible to other mortals.

Record 12 // Cantha

Shiro Tagachi changes an entire landscape

In 872 AE, imperial bodyguard Shiro Tagachi assassinated Emperor Angsiyan at the Harvest Temple.

Shiro was killed by Saint Viktor, Archemorus, and the assassin Vizu.

But his death released an enormous magical shockwave remembered as the Jade Wind.

The Jade Sea solidified.

The Echovald Forest was petrified.

Cantha entered the Age of the Dragon.

Record 13 // Isolation and Trade

Connections between nations weaken

In 902 AE, Cantha dissolved its long-standing trade agreements with the northern human kingdoms by imperial decree.

By 940 AE, Cantha's embassy in Ascalon and its diplomatic presence in Elona had formally closed.

Political connections weakened, while piracy further disrupted trade.

The connections between continents had not vanished, but they had become less stable.

Record 14 // The Guild Wars

Competition for power becomes war

The eruption of Abaddon's Mouth scattered the Bloodstones across Tyria.

Once their power became known, guilds began competing to control them.

That competition escalated into the First Guild War.

Ascalon and Kryta were eventually dragged into the fighting.

A Second Guild War followed at an uncertain date, again involving Ascalon and Kryta.

By this point, conflicts that began between guilds had become wars capable of consuming kingdoms.

Why They Were Called Guild Wars

The conflicts were not simply wars between Ascalon and Kryta. Powerful guilds were at the center of the struggle, competing for Bloodstones, territory, political influence, and military power.

Record 15 // The Third Guild War

The bloodiest Guild War begins

In 1013 AE, the Third Guild War began.

Ascalon and Kryta again became consumed by conflict.

Orr initially remained outside the war.

Eventually, King Reza sent Orrian forces into the conflict hoping to end it.

Instead, Orr's entry made the war even more destructive.

The Third Guild War would continue for decades.

And while the human kingdoms fought one another, the Charr were preparing to reclaim lands humanity had taken from them.
