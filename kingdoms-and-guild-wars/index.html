Historical Archive // Record 06

Kingdoms and Guild Wars

1 AE – 1069 AE

The gods had withdrawn.

What followed was not an empty age, but a mortal one. Kingdoms expanded, dynasties collapsed, trade crossed continents, guilds became armies, and old rivalries hardened into generations of conflict.

By the end of this era, the human kingdoms of continental Tyria were powerful, divided, and dangerously exhausted.

Archive Context

This record follows the long period between the Exodus and the eve of the original Guild Wars campaigns. Its purpose is to show how mortal civilizations developed and why the human kingdoms were already weakened before the disasters beginning in 1070 AE.

Record 01 // After the Exodus

Mortal nations move forward without the gods

The Exodus changed the relationship between mortals and the divine, but it did not halt human civilization.

In 1 AE, Prince Chang Hai became the first Ascendant emperor of Cantha.

In 2 AE, Orr became an independent nation.

The political world was becoming more divided, regional, and increasingly controlled by mortal rulers.

Record 02 // Expansion

Human influence spreads across the known world

The Primeval Kings expanded their influence into Vabbi and the Desolation.

Human settlements continued growing across continental Tyria.

By 174 AE, the expansion of humanity had pushed the Forgotten farther into the Crystal Desert.

There they increasingly became associated with Glint and the trials later used by mortals seeking Ascension.

Record 03 // The Volcano Erupts

The Bloodstones return to the world

Around 100 AE, Abaddon's Mouth erupted.

The major Bloodstones that had been hidden within the volcano were scattered across Tyria.

Their magical influence seeped into the surrounding lands.

The stones became objects of power that ambitious groups wanted to control.

A Different Bloodstone Story

In the previous age, the Bloodstones had been safeguards. Once scattered, they became prizes whose power helped fuel mortal competition.

Record 04 // The Guilds

Organizations begin rivaling kings

Powerful guilds grew within the human nations.

These were not merely social organizations.

Guilds possessed fighters, wealth, influence, and the ability to enforce laws or ignore them depending on their interests.

As their influence spread across borders, disputes that once belonged to kings increasingly became conflicts between guilds.

Record 05 // A Connected World

Trade ties distant civilizations together

In 221 AE, Cantha began formal trade with Tyria, Elona, and northern regions.

Kryta became an official colony of Elona in 300 AE.

It gained independence in 358 AE.

The known world was increasingly connected by merchants, diplomats, migrations, and political agreements.

Record 06 // Elona Changes

Plague breaks an ancient dynasty

In 452 AE, the Scarab Plague devastated Elona.

The royal house of the Primeval Kings was wiped out, and Istan was abandoned.

New dynasties rose and later collapsed during the Shattered Dynasty Era.

By 640 AE, Elona had emerged as three allied provinces: Istan, Kourna, and Vabbi.

Record 07 // Palawa Joko

An undead conqueror nearly takes Elona

Palawa Joko emerged from the Desolation and built his Bone Palace.

In 860 AE, he invaded Elona and conquered much of Vabbi.

Two years later, Turai Ossa defeated him at the Battle of Jahai.

Joko could not be permanently killed, so he was imprisoned, and the Order of Whispers was established to watch over his tomb.

Record 08 // Lord Odran

A mortal finds a way into the Mists

In 851 AE, the arcanist Lord Odran became the first recorded living mortal to enter the Rift.

Through dangerous rituals and sacrifices, he opened permanent portals between Tyria and regions of the Mists.

His death eventually left those pathways exposed.

Mortals now had access to places once thought to belong only to spirits and gods.

Record 09 // Shiro Tagachi

Cantha is frozen in a single moment

In 872 AE, Shiro Tagachi assassinated Emperor Angsiyan during the Harvest Ceremony.

Shiro was killed by Saint Viktor, Archemorus, and Vizu.

His death released the Jade Wind.

The Jade Sea solidified and the Echovald Forest was petrified, permanently changing the landscape of Cantha.

Record 10 // Growing Isolation

Cantha turns increasingly inward

Conflict with the tengu grew into the Tengu Wars.

Trade agreements with Ascalon, Kryta, and Orr were dissolved in 902 AE.

Canthan embassies eventually closed, and contact with the northern kingdoms weakened.

Informal trade resumed in 1022 AE, but relations remained more limited than before.

Record 11 // The Third Guild War

Human rivalry consumes continental Tyria

There had already been two earlier Guild Wars, though their exact dates remain uncertain.

In 1013 AE, the Third Guild War began.

Guilds tied to the great human kingdoms fought for influence and supremacy.

Ascalon, Kryta, and eventually Orr were pulled into a conflict that consumed wealth, soldiers, and political attention.

Record 12 // Saul D'Alessio

Kryta gains a new faith

Around 1067 AE, the exile Saul D'Alessio encountered the mursaat.

He returned to Kryta and founded the White Mantle.

The mursaat became known to their followers as the Unseen Ones.

What began as a religious movement would soon become central to the survival and later rule of Kryta.

Record 13 // The Eve of Disaster

The kingdoms are powerful, but exhausted

By 1069 AE, the human kingdoms of continental Tyria had spent generations fighting among themselves.

Ascalon remained locked in rivalry with its neighbors. Kryta was undergoing religious and political change. Orr had largely remained outside the worst of the fighting, but could not ignore the conflict forever.

None of them knew that the Guild Wars were about to become the smaller problem.

North of Ascalon, the charr were preparing to march south.
