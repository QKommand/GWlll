Historical Archive // Record 06

Kingdoms and Guild Wars

2 AE – 1013 AE

The gods had left.

The world did not become quiet.

Kings, guilds, dynasties, traders, prophets, and conquerors now shaped the age.

Over the next thousand years, mortal civilization would grow powerful enough to create its own catastrophes.

Archive Context

This is a long transitional era rather than a single war. It follows the rise and decline of kingdoms after the Exodus, the increasing power of guilds, the scattering of the Bloodstones, and the conflicts that eventually lead into the Guild Wars era.

Record 01 // Orr

Orr becomes an independent nation

In 2 AE, Orr became an independent human nation.

The kingdom possessed a particularly strong relationship with magic and remained closely associated with Arah, the former city of the gods.

Orr would eventually become one of the three great human kingdoms of continental Tyria.

Record 02 // The Ring of Fire

The Bloodstones return to the world

About a century after the Exodus, Abaddon's Mouth erupted.

The five great Bloodstones were scattered across Tyria.

Magic seeped into the surrounding land and atmosphere.

What had been hidden to prevent another magical catastrophe was now spread throughout the world again.

Important Boundary

The Bloodstones were placed in Abaddon's Mouth before the Exodus. They were scattered later by the volcanic eruption, not during the Exodus itself.

Record 03 // Rise of the Guilds

Organizations begin rivaling kings

During the century after the Exodus, guilds became increasingly important social, military, and political organizations.

They did more than organize craftsmen or merchants.

Powerful guilds enforced laws, fielded armed forces, and acted according to their own interests.

As their influence overlapped, competition increasingly became conflict.

Record 04 // The Forgotten

Human expansion pushes the Forgotten away

By 174 AE, continued human expansion had pushed the Forgotten into the Crystal Desert.

There they became caretakers of Glint and guardians of the trials associated with Ascension.

One of Tyria's oldest peoples had largely withdrawn from the growing human world.

Record 05 // International Trade

The continents become more connected

In 221 AE, Cantha entered the Age of the Hedgehog.

Formal trade expanded between Cantha, Tyria, and Elona.

Goods, people, ideas, and political influence increasingly crossed continental borders.

Record 06 // Glint

Glint records a prophecy for a distant future

In 272 AE, Glint compiled the Flameseeker Prophecies.

Their importance would not become clear for centuries.

Eventually they would become central to the downfall of the mursaat and the events surrounding the Door of Komalie.

Record 07 // Kryta

A colony becomes a kingdom

In 300 AE, Kryta was formally recognized as a colony of Elona.

Human expansion through the region intensified conflict with local centaur populations.

In 358 AE, Kryta became independent.

It joined Ascalon and Orr as one of the major human powers of continental Tyria.

Record 08 // Elona

The Primeval Dynasty falls

In 452 AE, the Scarab Plague swept through Elona.

The plague devastated Istan, killed enormous numbers of people, and destroyed the royal family of the Primeval Kings.

Istan was abandoned.

By 456 AE, the plague years had ended and a new dynasty began.

Record 09 // Shattered Dynasties

Elona survives political collapse

The Great Dynasty eventually collapsed.

The resulting Pretender Wars began a period remembered as the Shattered Dynasty Era.

By 640 AE, Elona emerged as three allied provinces: Istan, Kourna, and Vabbi.

Record 10 // Palawa Joko

An undead conqueror challenges Elona

Palawa Joko emerged from the Desolation and built his power over generations.

In 860 AE, his invasion captured much of Vabbi.

In 862 AE, Warmarshal Turai Ossa defeated Joko at the Battle of Jahai.

The Order of Whispers was established to help keep the defeated lich imprisoned.

In 868 AE, Turai led the Great Pilgrimage into the Crystal Desert.

Record 11 // Lord Odran

A mortal opens the way into the Mists

In 851 AE, Lord Odran used sacrifices and powerful rituals to open portals into the Mists.

He became the first recorded living mortal to enter the Rift.

His death eventually left those hidden routes accessible to others.

Mortal access to the Mists had changed forever.

Record 12 // Cantha

Shiro Tagachi changes an entire landscape

In 872 AE, Shiro Tagachi assassinated Emperor Angsiyan.

Saint Viktor, Archemorus, and Vizu killed Shiro.

His death released the Jade Wind.

The Jade Sea solidified, while the Echovald Forest was petrified.

Cantha entered the Age of the Dragon.

Record 13 // Isolation and Trade

Connections between nations weaken

In 902 AE, Cantha formally dissolved long-standing trade agreements with the northern human kingdoms.

Diplomatic connections weakened further over time.

Informal trade eventually resumed, but the world was becoming politically fragmented.

Record 14 // Guild Wars

Guilds turn their power against one another

There were three major Guild Wars.

The exact dates of the first two remain uncertain.

Ascalon and Kryta were major participants, while Orr initially attempted to remain outside the early conflicts.

Guild armies had become powerful enough that kings could no longer reliably control them.

Record 15 // The Third Guild War

The final Guild War begins

In 1013 AE, the Third Guild War began.

Ascalon, Kryta, and eventually Orr became drawn into increasingly destructive conflict.

Orr entered hoping to end the war.

Instead, the fighting became even more severe.

The kingdoms were still fighting one another when a far older enemy moved south.

The Guild Wars Era

By the eleventh century AE, the great human kingdoms were exhausted by generations of rivalry.

Then the charr invaded.

The Searing, the Cataclysm of Orr, the Flameseeker Prophecies, Shiro's return, Nightfall, and the Destroyers would turn old political wars into a struggle for the survival of entire nations.
