The Elder Races

After the Ancient Dragonrise

The Elder Dragons returned to sleep, but the world they left behind was no longer the same.

Five ancient peoples carried pieces of that older Tyria forward.

Some preserved knowledge. Some disappeared. Some hid in the Mists. And some survived long enough to shape events thousands of years later.

Archive Scope

This archive does not retell the Dragonrise. Instead, it follows the civilizations that survived it and the paths they took afterward.

Their stories explain later mysteries involving the Bloodstones, Glint, Ascension, the White Mantle, Janthir, and the Mists.

Record 01 // Seers

The architects of ancient magic

The Seers were among the most magically advanced peoples known from ancient Tyria.

Their creations included technologies such as the Shadowstone and the original Bloodstone.

The Bloodstone would outlive their civilization and become one of the most important magical artifacts in Tyrian history.

Their influence therefore continued long after the Seers themselves had almost disappeared.

Record 02 // An Ancient War

The Seers and mursaat become enemies

Whatever cooperation had once existed between the two peoples did not survive.

The Seers and mursaat fought a prolonged war.

Mursaat magic included the devastating ability known as Spectral Agony.

The conflict brought the Seers close to extinction.

Long Shadow

Thousands of years later, modern discoveries in Janthir and Castora would return Seer and mursaat history to the center of the story.

Record 03 // Mursaat

A civilization disappears into the Mists

The mursaat possessed an unusual advantage: they could phase themselves partly outside the physical world.

By retreating into the Mists, they became largely absent from the history of surface Tyria for thousands of years.

Their disappearance did not mean their civilization had ended.

They would later return to human history under another name: the Unseen Ones.

Record 04 // Forgotten

The Forgotten free Glint

The Forgotten continued to influence the world long after the Dragonrise.

Around 1680 BE, they performed a ritual upon Glint, the champion of Kralkatorrik.

The ritual broke the Elder Dragon's control over her.

Glint lost much of her former memory, but gained the freedom to choose her own path.

Her survival would eventually become critical to the future of the Dragon Cycle itself.

Record 05 // Guardianship

The Forgotten become keepers of hidden knowledge

The Forgotten remained closely associated with Glint.

In later history, they became guardians of the trials of Ascension in the Crystal Desert.

Their story therefore became increasingly connected to prophecy, dragon knowledge, and the ability of mortals to transcend ordinary limitations.

Conflicting Traditions

Later human traditions claimed the Forgotten arrived from the Mists with the Human Gods. Their much older involvement in the ancient Dragonrise makes that origin uncertain.

Record 06 // Dwarves

One ancient civilization rebuilds

Unlike some elder peoples, the dwarves remained highly visible through later Tyrian history.

They rebuilt powerful settlements throughout the Shiverpeak Mountains and eventually formed the kingdom of Deldrimor.

Dwarven legends became some of the few surviving windows into the ancient world.

Their civilization would endure all the way into the original Guild Wars era.

Record 07 // Jotun

A great civilization forgets itself

The jotun survived the ancient age, but their civilization did not.

Ancient jotun possessed sophisticated magical, astronomical, and historical knowledge.

Over generations, much of that knowledge disappeared.

The jotun encountered in modern Tyria are therefore remnants of a civilization far greater than the societies they now maintain.

Their surviving stelae remain some of the most valuable records of the ancient world.

Record 09 // The End of the Elder Age

The world is about to change again

For thousands of years, these ancient peoples carried the memory of the older world.

Some remained visible. Others disappeared into legend.

Then powerful beings arrived from the Mists.

With them came humanity.

The center of recorded history was about to shift.
