The Elder Races

Ancient Dragonrise – Before 786 BE

When the Elder Dragons rose, five ancient civilizations attempted to survive a catastrophe larger than any one people could face alone.

The Seers, Mursaat, Forgotten, Dwarves, and Jotun formed an alliance against the dragons.

That alliance would fracture, but the choices made during its collapse shaped Tyria for thousands of years.

Archive Scope

This archive follows the five civilizations known collectively as the Elder Races: the Seers, Mursaat, Forgotten, Dwarves, and Jotun.

They survived the previous Dragonrise, but they did not survive it in the same way. Their alliance fractured, some civilizations declined, and ancient rivalries continued long after the Elder Dragons returned to sleep.

Their history explains later mysteries involving the Bloodstones, Glint, Ascension, the White Mantle, Janthir, Castora, and the Mists.

Record 01 // The Alliance

Five peoples attempt to stand together

During the Dragonrise, the Seer leader Sidony helped rally representatives of the Elder Races.

The Seers, Mursaat, Forgotten, Dwarves, and Jotun formed an alliance against the Elder Dragons.

Their cooperation included the exchange of knowledge, magic, and information needed to understand the threat.

But when the alliance faced one of its greatest tests, that cooperation failed.

Record 02 // The Broken Alliance

The assault on Zhaitan fractures the Elder Races

The Forgotten and Mursaat prepared to confront Zhaitan.

Before the attack, Sidony withdrew the Seers from the assault.

The Dwarves and Jotun also did not join their allies in battle.

The Forgotten and Mursaat went forward without them and suffered terrible losses.

The Mursaat would remember the failure of their allies as a betrayal.

The Alliance Breaks

The Elder Races survived the Dragonrise, but their alliance did not. The consequences of this fracture would eventually lead to another ancient war.

Record 03 // Seers

The Seers hide magic from the dragons

The Seers were among the most magically advanced peoples known from ancient Tyria.

After withdrawing from the assault on Zhaitan, they turned toward another solution.

The Seers forged the Shadowstone, which amplified their magical abilities.

Using its power, they created the original Bloodstone and sealed the world's remaining untainted magic within it.

Rather than defeating the Elder Dragons, the strategy denied them access to the magic they consumed.

The Bloodstone would outlive the Seer civilization and become one of the most important artifacts in Tyrian history.

Southern Shiverpeaks

One of the later Bloodstones was located within Bloodstone Caves beneath the Southern Shiverpeaks. The cave system extended beneath the mountains to the chamber where the Bloodstone rested.

The stone was not the platform found within the Iron Mines of Moladune.

Record 04 // Mursaat

The Mursaat abandon the alliance

After the disastrous assault on Zhaitan, the Mursaat refused to surrender their magic to the Seers' Bloodstone.

Instead, they used their shadowcraft to withdraw from ordinary Tyria.

They shifted themselves beneath the skin of the world, into a shadowed existence out of phase with Tyria.

This allowed them to escape the Elder Dragons without sacrificing their magic.

To the peoples they abandoned, their disappearance became another act of betrayal.

Record 05 // Forgotten

The Forgotten free Glint

The Forgotten possessed powerful magic of their own.

In approximately 1680 BE, they performed a ritual upon Glint, champion and scion of the Elder Dragon Kralkatorrik.

The ritual freed Glint from Kralkatorrik's control while causing her to lose much of her former memory.

For the first time, she possessed the freedom to choose her own path.

Glint would eventually become one of the most important figures in the history of the Dragon Cycle.

Record 06 // Refuge

Glint helps conceal the survivors

After gaining her freedom, Glint aided several of the Elder Races by concealing them from the Elder Dragons.

This protection helped surviving members of the ancient peoples endure as the dragons gradually returned to slumber.

The Forgotten remained particularly closely associated with Glint.

In later ages, they would become guardians of knowledge, prophecy, and the trials of Ascension in the Crystal Desert.

Conflicting Traditions

Later human traditions associated the Forgotten with the Six Gods. Yet their involvement with the Elder Races and the ancient Dragonrise places them in Tyrian history long before humanity's earliest securely dated civilizations.

Record 07 // An Ancient War

The Mursaat return for revenge

Once the Elder Dragons had returned to slumber, the Mursaat returned to Tyria.

Their anger toward the Seers had not disappeared.

The two civilizations entered a war that continued for thousands of years.

The Mursaat wielded the devastating magical ability known as Spectral Agony.

The Seers eventually developed a defense against it, but too late to reverse the course of the war.

The Seers were driven to near extinction, while the Mursaat themselves also suffered heavy losses.

The battlefield where the war finally ended would later become the site of the Mursaat capital Bava Nisos in Janthir.

Long Shadow

Thousands of years later, discoveries in Janthir and Castora would expose pieces of this ancient conflict that had been lost to modern Tyria.

Record 08 // Dwarves

One ancient civilization rebuilds

Unlike several of the other Elder Races, the Dwarves remained highly visible through later Tyrian history.

They rebuilt settlements throughout the Shiverpeak Mountains and eventually established powerful kingdoms, including Deldrimor.

Dwarven records and legends preserved knowledge of the Elder Dragons long after most civilizations had forgotten the previous Dragonrise.

Their history would continue all the way into the original Guild Wars era.

Record 09 // Jotun

A great civilization forgets itself

The Jotun survived the ancient age, but their civilization declined.

Ancient Jotun possessed sophisticated magical, astronomical, and historical knowledge.

Over generations, much of that knowledge disappeared.

The Jotun encountered in modern Tyria are therefore remnants of a civilization far greater than the societies they now maintain.

Their surviving stelae remain some of the most valuable records of the ancient world.

Record 10 // Five Paths

The Elder Races do not share one fate

Surviving the Dragonrise did not guarantee that a civilization would endure.

Seers

Near Extinction

Their civilization nearly disappears, but their magical creations continue shaping Tyria.

Mursaat

Withdrawal and War

They escape the Dragonrise, return to battle the Seers, and later emerge in human history as the Unseen Ones.

Forgotten

Guardians

They become closely tied to Glint, prophecy, and Ascension.

Dwarves

Rebuilding

They establish new kingdoms and remain an active civilization for thousands of years.

Jotun

Decline

Their people survive, but much of their ancient civilization and knowledge is lost.

Record 11 // The End of the Elder Age

The center of history begins to shift

The Elder Races had survived a world dominated by dragons.

Some rebuilt. Some declined. Some nearly disappeared.

Their artifacts, wars, and unfinished histories remained buried across Tyria.

Then the Six Human Gods entered Tyria from the Mists.

Humanity was brought from a world beyond Tyria, and another great age of history began.
