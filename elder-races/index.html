Historical Archive // Record 02

The Elder Races

Ancient Dragonrise – 1769 BE

Ancient History Fragmentary Records

The Elder Dragons did not leave behind an empty world.

Some civilizations survived.

The Seers, mursaat, Forgotten, dwarves, and jotun carried pieces of the ancient world forward, even as much of their power, knowledge, and history disappeared.

Archive Context

These peoples are called elder races because their civilizations existed during the ancient Dragonrise, long before most modern nations appeared.

Much of what survives about them comes from later discoveries, fragmented writings, ruins, legends, and encounters with the remnants of their civilizations.

Record 01 // Survivors

Five peoples endure the Dragonrise

The surviving historical record identifies five major sentient elder races:

Elder Race

Seers

Advanced magical beings whose work produced the Shadowstone and later the first Bloodstone.

Elder Race

Mursaat

Powerful beings capable of shifting partly into the Mists, allowing them to escape the ancient Dragonrise.

Elder Race

Forgotten

Serpentine beings who later became closely tied to Glint, Ascension, and the Crystal Desert.

Elder Race

Dwarves

An ancient civilization whose legends preserved some of the oldest surviving memories of Tyria.

Elder Race

Jotun

Once highly advanced in magic and astronomy, but greatly diminished after the ancient Dragonrise.

Record 02 // Cooperation

The elder races unite against the dragons

The five elder races appear to have formed an alliance against the Elder Dragons.

Their goal was survival.

The dragons consumed enormous amounts of ambient magic, threatening civilizations whose own societies depended heavily upon magical power.

For a time, ancient rivals cooperated against a threat larger than any one people.

Record 03 // A Failed Strike

Mursaat and Forgotten attempt to kill Zhaitan

One of the alliance's known attempts to directly resist the Elder Dragons targeted Zhaitan.

Mursaat and Forgotten forces participated in the attempt.

It failed.

Both peoples suffered heavily, and the failure contributed to tension within the alliance.

Later Perspective

The mursaat appear to have believed they were abandoned during the failed campaign. Their later betrayal therefore has more context than a simple refusal to fight.

Record 04 // The Seers

The Seers try to hide magic itself

Directly defeating the Elder Dragons had failed.

The Seers turned toward another solution: denying the dragons access to the magic they consumed.

Their magical development appears to have progressed from the Shadowstone to the Bloodstone.

Record 05 // The Bloodstone

The Seers build a refuge for magic

The first Bloodstone was created by the Seers.

Its original purpose was to gather and hide magical energy from the Elder Dragons.

This is an important correction to older human accounts, which described the Human Gods as the original creators of the Bloodstone.

The gods would not divide and repurpose it until thousands of years later.

Record 06 // The Alliance Breaks

The mursaat refuse the Bloodstone plan

The mursaat did not surrender their magical power to the Seers' plan.

They abandoned the other elder races and escaped by phasing themselves into the Mists.

Their survival came at the cost of the alliance.

The exact sequence of events remains incomplete, but the ancient coalition never recovered.

Record 07 // Old Enemies

The Seers and mursaat turn on one another

Conflict between the two peoples became a prolonged war.

Mursaat abilities such as Spectral Agony proved devastating.

The Seers were nearly exterminated.

Their civilization largely disappeared, leaving artifacts and scattered survivors behind.

Record 08 // The Forgotten

Glint is freed from Kralkatorrik

In approximately 1680 BE, the Forgotten performed a ritual upon Glint.

Glint had been a champion of Kralkatorrik.

The ritual broke the Elder Dragon's control over her, though much of her former memory was lost.

Glint would become one of the most important bridges between the ancient Dragon Cycle and later mortal history.

Record 09 // The Survivors

Survival does not mean recovery

When the Elder Dragons returned to hibernation, the surviving elder races emerged into a diminished world.

The jotun had lost much of their former knowledge and civilization.

The Seers were nearly extinct.

The mursaat remained hidden.

The Forgotten continued into later ages, while the dwarves rebuilt kingdoms that would survive for thousands of years.

Record 10 // The Forgotten Question

Later human history tells a different story

Human traditions later claimed that the Forgotten arrived from the Mists around 1769 BE and were brought to Tyria by the Human Gods.

That account conflicts with evidence placing the Forgotten in Tyria during the much older Dragonrise.

The archive therefore preserves both traditions rather than forcing them into a single explanation.

Archive Rule

When later discoveries contradict older histories, GWlll records the difference instead of silently replacing one version with another.

Record 11 // The World Changes Again

The elder races are no longer alone

Thousands of years passed after the ancient Dragonrise.

New peoples expanded. The charr established their own territories. Surviving elder races followed increasingly separate paths.

Then powerful beings arrived from the Mists.

They would become known to humanity as the Six Gods.

And with them came the beginning of a new historical age.
