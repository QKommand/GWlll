Author's Preface

One stone, several histories

The Bloodstone was already ancient before it entered Human history.

The Seers created the original stone during the previous Dragonrise to preserve magic from the Elder Dragons. Thousands of years later, the Six Gods inherited that artifact and repurposed it during a crisis over mortal magic.

They divided the original into five pieces: four tied to the schools of Aggression, Destruction, Preservation, and Denial, with the fifth serving as the Keystone. The stones were sealed with the blood of King Doric and placed within Abaddon's Mouth.

After they were scattered, each fragment developed a history of its own. Guilds sought them. The Mursaat incorporated one into their sacrificial system. Gadd studied another beneath Sparkfly Swamp. The Maguuma Bloodstone later absorbed enormous amounts of magic before exploding in 1329 AE.

I

The Bloodstone begins with the Dragon Cycle

During the previous Dragonrise, the Elder Dragons consumed magic as they fed. The civilizations living through that age faced a world in which usable magical power was steadily disappearing.

The Seers developed another way to preserve part of it.

They created the original Bloodstone, placing uncorrupted magic within an artifact capable of holding immense power beyond the reach of the feeding Dragons.

The work belonged to a broader Seer tradition of magical containment. Shadowstone was another Seer creation associated with storing and amplifying magical power, although the surviving record does not preserve every step that led from one form of Seer artifice to the Bloodstone.

When the Elder Dragons eventually returned to slumber, magic gradually returned to Tyria.

The power sealed within the Bloodstone remained preserved.

Commander Q // Observation

The first Bloodstone was not created because someone wanted more magic. It was created because the world was losing it.

II

The Bloodstone enters Divine history

Thousands of years later, the Bloodstone passed into the possession of the Six Gods.

Exactly how they acquired it is unknown. By the Divine Age, however, Arah contained artifacts and knowledge inherited from civilizations far older than Humanity's presence in Tyria.

The Bloodstone became one of those ancient works.

Its Seer origin was eventually lost from much of Human historical memory. Later traditions instead began with the moment the Gods altered it, making Divine action appear to be the beginning of the artifact's history.

Historical Accounts

Older Human records credited the Gods with the Bloodstones because their histories preserved the Divine division of the artifact. Later evidence restored the older Seer origin of the original Bloodstone.

III

One Bloodstone becomes five

In 1 BE, expanded access to magic contributed to destructive conflicts among mortal civilizations. King Doric appealed to the Gods for help.

Their response returned the ancient Bloodstone to the center of history.

The Gods divided it into five major pieces. Four became associated with the schools of Aggression, Destruction, Preservation, and Denial.

The fifth was the Keystone, required to bring the divided system together again.

The stones were sealed with Doric's blood and placed within Abaddon's Mouth in the Ring of Fire.

The purpose had changed completely.

The Seers had created the original Bloodstone to preserve magic from the Elder Dragons. The Gods now used it to place limits and structure upon mortal access to that magic.

IV

The stones return to the wider world

The five Bloodstones did not remain together beneath Abaddon's Mouth.

A later eruption scattered the fragments, separating artifacts that had briefly belonged to one Divine system.

Their histories now diverged.

Bloodstones appeared in distant regions, including Maguuma, the Ring of Fire, and the underground complex later known as the Bloodstone Caves. The Keystone followed another path of its own.

Their dispersal also returned concentrated magical power to places where mortals could seek it.

Competition over Bloodstones and other magical resources became part of the conflicts remembered as the Guild Wars.

Commander Q // Observation

Hiding five enormous reservoirs of magic inside a volcano worked until the volcano stopped cooperating.

V

The Mursaat build sacrifice around a Bloodstone

By 1072 AE, the Maguuma Bloodstone had become part of the system the Mursaat used to prevent the Flameseeker Prophecies from reaching fulfillment.

Through the White Mantle, Humans identified as the Chosen were taken to the Bloodstone and sacrificed.

Their souls helped power the defenses surrounding the Door of Komalie, which the Mursaat desperately wanted to keep closed.

The distinction between the stone and the system built around it is important. The surviving evidence does not establish that Bloodstone naturally consumes souls whenever someone dies upon it.

The Mursaat had built rituals and magical machinery around the power available there.

Commander Q // Observation

The Seers created the Bloodstone to protect something from destruction. Thousands of years later, their ancient enemies were using one while sacrificing people to save themselves.

VI

Gadd finds another Bloodstone beneath Sparkfly Swamp

In 1078 AE, Gadd entered the underground complex known as the Bloodstone Caves.

Deep within the caves lay another of the scattered Bloodstones.

Gadd approached it as a source of magical power to be studied and extracted. Inscribed creatures within the complex carried Bloodstone essence, while the deeper site contained magical defenses and mechanisms that had remained active long before his expedition arrived.

The Bloodstone also affected the surrounding magical environment. Within its aura, death could transfer restorative energy to nearby creatures.

What remains unexplained is the complex around it.

Doors, inscriptions, constructed passages, defensive systems, and other artificial features surround the route toward a Bloodstone that reached the region only after the five stones had been scattered.

Historical Caution // Bloodstone Caves

The builders of the complex have not been securely identified. The presence of a Bloodstone does not prove that the Seers constructed the site, and the surviving record does not establish whether the structures predate the stone's arrival.

Commander Q // Observation

The Bloodstone is not the mystery here. We know why it could have ended up underground after the stones were scattered. I want to know who built everything around it.

VII

Dragon magic overwhelms the Maguuma Bloodstone

The deaths of Zhaitan and Mordremoth returned enormous amounts of magic to Tyria.

Some of that power accumulated within the Maguuma Bloodstone.

The White Mantle was also mining and experimenting with the increasingly unstable stone.

In 1329 AE, it exploded.

The blast devastated the surrounding region and left Bloodstone Fen saturated with unstable magic. Creatures exposed to concentrated Bloodstone energy changed, while spirits connected to earlier sacrifices remained visible around the shattered site.

Balthazar, hidden at the time behind the identity of Lazarus, absorbed much of the magic released by the explosion.

Power placed into the Bloodstone during several different ages had now entered yet another conflict.

VIII

Bloodstone stores power and changes what surrounds it

The most consistent property of Bloodstone is its ability to hold extraordinary amounts of magical energy.

That property reaches back to the original Seer purpose and remained important in every later age.

Bloodstone can also influence its surroundings. The aura within Bloodstone Caves changed the effect of nearby deaths, while the shattered Maguuma Bloodstone saturated an entire region with unstable magical energy.

Its essence can be extracted, divided, and used apart from the main stone. Gadd experimented with it directly, while later organizations mined fragments and developed other uses for Bloodstone power.

Heavy exposure can also affect living creatures, producing physical changes, aggression, and dependence upon additional magical energy.

Not every effect associated with a Bloodstone belongs naturally to the material itself. The Mursaat sacrificial system remains the clearest example of later magic being built around an ancient artifact.

IX

The Bloodstone record remains incomplete

The broad history of the Bloodstone is now clear.

The Seers created the original during the previous Dragonrise. The Gods inherited and divided it. The resulting Bloodstones were scattered and repeatedly reused by civilizations that attached new purposes to the power within them.

Several parts of the record remain open.

We do not know exactly how the Gods acquired the original Seer Bloodstone. The surviving stones have not all been securely matched to the four schools of magic. The full journey of every fragment after the eruption of Abaddon's Mouth is incomplete.

Bloodstone Caves remains especially uncertain. Its constructed passages and magical defenses are undeniable, but their builders have not been securely identified.

Those gaps matter because the Bloodstone repeatedly outlived the civilizations using it.

Seers, Gods, Guilds, Mursaat, Asura, White Mantle, and Balthazar all encountered the same ancient material under different circumstances.

Each left another layer of history upon it.

Open Record

The builders of Bloodstone Caves, the complete later locations of all five Bloodstones, the original Divine recovery of the Seer artifact, and several stages in the stones' dispersal remain unresolved.

Commander Q // Observation

The Bloodstone survived because everyone kept finding another use for it. That makes its history less like the story of one artifact and more like a trail left by every civilization that touched it.
