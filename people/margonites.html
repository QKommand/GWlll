175 BE

THE HUMAN MARGONITES

Peoples Archive // Human Civilization

The Margonites

On the Human civilization of the Crystal Sea, its devotion to Abaddon, and the transformed survivors who followed him into exile.

Compiled and Annotated By

Commander Q

Captain Commander of the Vanguard Knights
Researcher of Tyrian History and the Mists

Overview

Before the demons

The Margonites were a Human people centered upon the Crystal Sea and its surrounding coasts. By 175 BE, surviving chronology records Margonite settlements north and west of Elona and remembers their ships as a major power upon the Unending Ocean.

Their later history became inseparable from Abaddon. Many Margonites remained loyal to him during his conflict with the other Gods, and some were transformed into the demonic beings encountered centuries later in the Realm of Torment.

Those transformed survivors dominate the surviving record because they endured long after the destruction of the Crystal Sea.

They were not the beginning of Margonite history.

I

A civilization built around the sea

The earliest securely dated Margonite record belongs to 175 BE.

By then, Margonite settlements existed along the coastlines north and west of Elona, while their ships were remembered as a dominant presence upon the Unending Ocean.

Their homeland centered upon the Crystal Sea, the body of water that once occupied much of the region later known as the Crystal Desert.

The surviving evidence points toward a maritime people connected by ships, islands, ports, and coastal settlements. It does not preserve a reliable map of their political borders or identify a confirmed capital.

King Khimaar establishes that Margonite kingship existed during at least part of their Human history, but the extent of his authority is unknown.

Commander Q // Observation

The Margonites most people remember never saw the Crystal Sea as Humans. Their longevity preserved the aftermath more clearly than the civilization that came before it.

II

Abaddon becomes central to Margonite religion

The Margonites developed a strong religious connection to Abaddon, then one of the Six Gods and associated with knowledge, secrets, and water.

The surviving record does not tell us when that relationship began or whether every Margonite community worshiped him with the same devotion.

By the closing years of the Divine Age, however, loyalty to Abaddon had become strong enough among his followers to draw them into conflict with the rest of the pantheon.

Margonites associated with him desecrated the Temple of the Six Gods, defacing representations of the other Gods.

Their relationship with the Forgotten was already hostile. Religious conflict now gave that older hostility another cause.

III

King Khimaar seeks Ascension

King Khimaar is one of the few rulers preserved by name from the Human Margonite era.

Khimaar and many of his people entered lands associated with Ascension and came into conflict with the Forgotten.

Khimaar died during that struggle.

His spirit later remained in the Crystal Desert, where travelers following their own path toward Ascension encountered him.

His surviving title confirms a Margonite monarchy but does not tell us whether one king ruled the entire civilization or when Khimaar lived.

Chronology Note // King Khimaar

Khimaar belongs to the Human period of Margonite history, but no secure date for his reign survives. His story should remain undated rather than being forced into the events immediately surrounding Abaddon's fall.

IV

The problem of Jadoth

In 1 BE, the Scriptures of Abaddon describe Jadoth fleeing across the water while pursued by Forgotten warships.

Jadoth called upon Abaddon, who answered by destroying the pursuing fleet and granting power to his follower.

The scripture calls Jadoth the first of the Margonites.

That title cannot describe the beginning of the Human Margonite civilization in a literal chronological sense. Margonite settlements and maritime power are recorded generations earlier.

Jadoth may have been remembered as the first transformed Margonite or as the first member of a particular religious order associated with Abaddon.

The surviving source does not explain the title.

Historical Accounts // "First of the Margonites"

The contradiction should remain visible. Treating Jadoth as the first transformed Margonite fits the chronology, but it remains an interpretation rather than an explicit statement in the surviving text.

V

Abaddon's followers are transformed

During the conflict surrounding Abaddon's fall, loyal Human followers were transformed into the demonic beings later known as Margonites.

Their new forms were powerful, magical, and no longer subject to death from old age.

The transformation did not erase individual thought. Later Margonites could question Abaddon, reject his authority, and choose paths opposed to his plans.

Their longevity also came with a cost. Later testimony describes transformed Margonites as unable to produce another generation.

At the same time, Abaddon's rebellion became open Divine war.

The fighting destroyed the Crystal Sea, leaving the Crystal Desert and Desolation where the old Margonite maritime world had once existed.

The transformed Margonites survived.

Their Human civilization did not.

Commander Q // Observation

Their transformation preserved individuals while destroying continuity. They could live for ages, but the world that had made them Margonites was gone.

VI

Exile becomes their new world

Abaddon was defeated and imprisoned within the Realm of Torment.

His transformed Margonite followers were driven there with him.

The Realm became the center of their later history. Margonite temples, military positions, settlements, and strongholds developed within Abaddon's prison, including places later known as Ar'Challah and Torc'qua.

This society was no longer the maritime civilization of the Crystal Sea.

Its people had been transformed, displaced into another realm, separated from ordinary Human generations, and organized around a defeated God whose prison had become their home.

The Forgotten, once enemies of the Human Margonites, later served among the forces helping maintain Abaddon's imprisonment.

VII

The transformed Margonites return during Nightfall

More than a thousand years after Abaddon's defeat, Margonite forces remained active within the Realm of Torment.

In 1075 AE, they supported his attempt to break free and bring about Nightfall.

Their continued presence demonstrated the consequence of their transformation. Individuals could endure across spans of time impossible for ordinary Humans.

Their society was not completely united.

The Lost rejected the future offered by his people, while The Apostate rejected Abaddon's claim to unquestionable Divine authority.

Their dissent matters.

Margonite identity had become closely tied to Abaddon, but it had never reduced every Margonite to the same choice.

VIII

Abaddon dies, but the Margonite record remains open

The Nightfall crisis ended with the death of Abaddon.

His released Divine power was absorbed by Kormir, who ascended as his successor. Abaddon's remaining forces suffered heavily, and the political and religious order centered upon him was broken.

That does not require the conclusion that every Margonite everywhere died with him.

What ended beyond dispute was the world that had given them their later identity.

The Human Margonite civilization had already vanished more than a thousand years earlier. Its sea had become desert. Its transformed descendants could not renew themselves through ordinary generations. The God whose rebellion had shaped their exile was now dead.

Their surviving history is therefore unusually uneven.

We know more about their transformation, rebellion, and exile than we do about their homes, families, ports, ships, laws, or ordinary religious life before the catastrophe.

That imbalance is worth remembering whenever the word Margonite is used as another name for demon.

Open Record

The location of the Margonite political center, the date of King Khimaar's reign, the exact meaning of Jadoth's title, and the fate of any untransformed Margonite communities remain unresolved.

Commander Q // Observation

Their ending survived more clearly than their life. The archive should not mistake one for the other.

The Margonites began as a Human maritime civilization and survived in history through the followers Abaddon transformed before his fall.

Their record crosses the histories of the Forgotten, Abaddon, the Crystal Sea, the Exodus, and Nightfall.
